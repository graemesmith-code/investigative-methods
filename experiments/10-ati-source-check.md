# 10. Is Canada's ATI index a useful place to start?

**Question:** Is the completed-request index a practical route to the next useful document? This was the **30 September 2026 preliminary source check**, before the larger [ATI trial](07-canadian-ati.md). It inspected twenty summaries and worked through three selected examples. No underlying release was obtained at this stage.

## What the source contains

The index gives department, request number, summary, disclosure status and page count. It reported 1,234 completion-year 2026 entries for Global Affairs and National Defence combined; 926 were marked fully or partly disclosed. These were index counts, not documents read. Only the first ten entries from each department were inspected in this probe.

Previously released documents can be sought at no cost through the government's route. That does not guarantee quick delivery, complete text or useful surviving pages. Closure month is not the date of the event or necessarily the date the summary appeared online.

## A recipe you can follow

1. Read a few ordinary summaries from a department you cover.
2. Select a small, clearly labeled set of examples. Keep convenience samples separate from random ones.
3. Search for the event using names and phrases as well as the request number.
4. Decide whether the next step is to read an existing story, obtain a named release, or stop because the summary is too vague.

## What happened

| Example | What the bounded check found | Next useful step |
|---|---|---|
| Ottawa Treaty withdrawals, GAC A-2025-00627 | Withdrawals and Canada's dilemma were already reported | Compare the twelve-page release's recommendations and dates with the public account |
| C22 pistol incidents, DND A-2025-00945 | A CBC investigation was corroborated through attributed follow-on coverage and a Senate intervention; the original page was inaccessible in this probe | Use as a known-covered example; inspect the 153-page release for any distinct incident or warning |
| Brokering permits, GAC A-2023-02654 | Searches did not identify an exact event; the summary omitted broker, destination, goods and outcome | Obtain the five-page note to establish what happened before searching further |

Eighteen free-web queries were recorded across these cases, seven on the vague brokering example with little improvement. That suggests a practical stopping point: get the defining document first. It does not measure how well search performs generally.

## Cost, limits and the next useful step

No paid Jev or Exa calls were made in this probe. Human time and platform costs were not priced. The three examples were selected from a convenience sample, not evidence of the whole index's story yield. Coverage was checked in September 2026, not as it would have appeared when the requests closed.

The later ATI run retrieved and ranked all 1,234 summaries and obtained one different release. Read that [separate result](07-canadian-ati.md) before interpreting this earlier “no releases obtained” status as the project's final position.
