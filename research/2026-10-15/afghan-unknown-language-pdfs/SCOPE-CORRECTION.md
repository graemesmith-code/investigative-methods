# Scope correction: Afghan government foreign-language PDFs

## Finding

The original selection was too narrow. The request covered documents from Afghan government domains in languages other than English, Dari/Farsi and Pashto. The delivered corpus contained only FinePDFs records whose language category was `unknown`.

Documents already assigned to Chinese, Russian, Arabic and other language categories should have been included. There was no substantive reason to exclude them.

The roughly 350 estimate was an estimate for the unknown-language category. Its complete URL scan later returned 239 records. Neither number estimated the full foreign-language corpus.

## Consequences

- The two retained leads describe the outcome of screening the unknown-language category only.
- Their low yield cannot establish that foreign-language publications from Afghan government domains are rare.
- The 17-minute review cannot establish the cost or value of the intended all-language method.
- The screened package remains usable for inspecting those two leads, but it is not a complete answer to the requested corpus.
- Government hosting and government authorship remain separate questions.

## Correct scope

Search gov.af and its subdomains across all other language categories, then check the content of matches. Include substantive foreign-language sections in mixed documents. Keep the unknown-language results as an additional source of candidates.

Official sites outside gov.af and foreign-language passages in records assigned to English, Persian or Pashto remain further coverage questions; they cannot be ruled out by this domain-and-category selection.

## Status on 15 October 2026

The correction is acknowledged and the public descriptions are updated. A new search was started but was not completed. Its partial checks do not establish totals or absence of matches in any of the large omitted categories. No replacement all-language reading package has yet been produced.

## Technical annex

The expanded attempt used the existing FinePDFs Parquet inventory, public server-side filtering and selective reads of URL columns. The server-side Arabic result explicitly reported partial coverage. Other server-side queries returned timeouts or loading errors. A broader direct scan was interrupted before complete coverage and is not used to make a language-category count.

The completed 239-record audit and screening decisions are preserved unchanged. The scope error concerns how the candidate set was defined, not a new finding that any particular omitted language is present or absent.
