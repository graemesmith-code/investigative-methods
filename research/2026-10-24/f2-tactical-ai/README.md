# AI enabled tactical robots and weapons

F2 procurement-first investigation, evidence cut off 24 October 2026.

Read [the full report](report.md). It presents three conditional main reporting dossiers, five grouped reserves and explicit exclusions. It does not claim three proven scoops or autonomous combat deployment. An editable Word edition accompanies the report.

**Methods clarification dated 24 October 2026.** The original Strong threshold was overstrict for investigative journalism. Controlled same-hardware ablations are not required for strong practical evidence. Original grades are preserved as historical rubric outputs, not current overall practical-evidence ratings; mechanism/use, maturity, comparative causal advantage and editorial novelty are assessed separately. Contract amounts, test facts and unresolved attribution questions are unchanged. See the executive assessment and Appendix B.

## Scope and rights

The archive is selected FY2019–2026 material, not a complete Pentagon census. Every one of its 712,186 transaction keys was screened, including 88,273 assistance transactions that overlap the separate research-grant lane. Machine screening is not individual qualitative review.

Public sources are linked and analytically paraphrased. This package excludes full commercial articles, credentials, private correspondence, raw search article bodies, large source archives and the working SQLite database. Source hashes support provenance without redistributing those materials. The original source archives are identified in `audit/source-archives.json`; reproduction requires the same inputs. Search absence is never proof of worldwide absence of prior reporting.

No outreach was conducted. This is a reporting-lead dossier, not an allegation that a vendor or agency acted improperly.
