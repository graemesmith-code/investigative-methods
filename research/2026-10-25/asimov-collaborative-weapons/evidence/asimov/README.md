# ASIMOV completion review

Public-source evidence checked 24 October 2026. Read [primary findings](primary-findings.md), then the [seven-contract register](contract-register.csv) and [43-action history](fpds-actions.csv). The [people and public-output map](people-outputs.md) identifies professional sources and distinguishes research publications from accepted programme deliverables. The [public tool access review](software-access-review.md) covers all seven performers and separates three verified research outputs from unverified complete software or data releases. A [three-team supplement](software-access-supplement-unsw-saab-covar.md) documents the related but not yet ASIMOV-attributed SEED-SET lead.

The evidence establishes a completion notice by 17 June 2026 and no visible option exercise in seven identified performer contracts. It does not establish formal early termination, reasons for non-exercise, or the absence of follow-on work under other vehicles. Obligations are not expenditure; option ceilings are not promised funding. USAspending and FPDS are different representations of the same underlying reporting, not wholly independent sources.

No outreach or paid searches were conducted for this primary-source review. This package contains focused analyses, public links, extracted contract fields, parsing scripts and file hashes. It excludes raw administrative records, personal contracting-officer details, executive compensation fields, credentials and copyrighted article/paper fulltexts.

The financial script retrieves the seven exact PIIDs, then `parse_actions.py` reconciles returned actions. `fetch_archive.py` retrieves the four dated page captures used in the change window. Full source-file hashes identify retained evidence, including files not redistributed. A hash is provenance, not a substitute for checking the source. Primary source and archive indexes supply verification links.

The original published F2 screening register is discovery context, not proof of ASIMOV programme disposition. This focused review checked fresh primary records rather than treating the prior screen as an audited contract lifecycle.
