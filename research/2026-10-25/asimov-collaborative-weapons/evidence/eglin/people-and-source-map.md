# People and source map for Eglin collaborative autonomy reporting

Cutoff: 24 October 2026. These are public professional leads and document routes, not people who have been contacted. Historical titles are dated. A role in Golden Horde does not by itself establish involvement in a later exact contract. Questions are limited to acquisition, programme history, testing, assurance and accountability.

New affiliations visible on professional profiles do not establish an SSCI departure or end to his role on this award; no such departure is asserted.

## Independent counterpoints to pair with interested sources

The separate historical-context people map includes independently sourced leads at SIPRI, CNAS, the University of Southern Denmark and GAO. Their useful role is to test whether the procurement interpretation is sound: what evidence is needed to call a simulation a military capability, how independent assurance is demonstrated, and how meaningful human control differs from a formal human-authority statement. They should not be presented as direct observers of contracts they have not reviewed.

## Document routes with the highest evidentiary value

1. FPDS action histories for exact dates, modification reasons and changes in obligations/value. Their financial totals reconcile to USAspending, but both systems share underlying reporting and are not two independent confirmations of delivered capability.
2. USAspending current records for normalized award/parent/funding/performance fields, with source URLs in the cohort CSV. These are reported locations, not a complete map of actual work.
3. SBIR.gov for original proposal abstracts, topic linkage and named investigators. Its financial/schedule fields may remain stale after modifications.
4. Dated AFRL, APL and GTRI accounts for actual historical demonstrations and the people who observed them. They are institutional accounts, not independent performance audits.
5. Public acceptance reports, test reports, transition decisions and programme-office explanations would resolve maturity better than vendor marketing. They were not established for the six exact awards in this review.
