# Chronology of the audited research and its documented predecessors

Cutoff: 24 October 2026. Entries distinguish historical programme events, procurement actions and unresolved links. “Predecessor” here means documented institutional or programme history, not proof that one exact contract inherited another's technology. The complete 124-action procurement chronology is in `fa8651-contract-actions.csv`.

## Demonstration and evaluation history

- **December 2020:** AFRL conducted the first Golden Horde Collaborative Small Diameter Bomb flight demonstration. The official retrospective describes a software problem that prevented the intended coordination; the weapons reverted to predetermined behaviour. This is a publicly documented test limitation, not evidence that all later systems have the same problem. [AFRL, 7 January 2021](https://www.af.mil/News/Features/Article/2465160/afrl-demonstrates-first-collaborative-weapon-technologies/).
- **25 May 2021:** The final Golden Horde collaborative glide-bomb flight demonstration took place at White Sands Missile Range. This was physical test evidence for that demonstrator, not production evidence for any audited 2023–25 contract. [AFRL, 27 May 2021](https://www.afrl.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/2634538/afrl-successfully-completes-golden-horde-collaborative-small-diameter-bomb-flig/).
- **11 November 2021:** GTRI published its account of adapting earlier autonomous-aircraft expertise to Golden Horde, naming Pete Crump and Kyle Keppler. This establishes institutional experience, not their assignment to the later supersonic order. [GTRI](https://gtri.gatech.edu/newsroom/collaborative-weapons-work-together-respond-changing-conditions).
- **8–9 December 2021:** APL's first Operation Protovision competition used the Colosseum virtual environment. The 8 December announcement names Brandon Coloe and Christopher DeMay and explicitly connects the environment's development to APL's earlier AlphaDogfight work. The weapons scenario was simulated. [APL](https://www.jhuapl.edu/news/news-releases/211208-golden-horde-operation-protovision-first-competition-debuts-new-simulation-environment).
- **17 December 2021:** EpiSci announced its Tactical AI participation in that simulation competition and identified Chris Gentile as Golden Horde programme manager. This predates and does not identify the exact 2023 reinforcement-learning award. [EpiSci](https://www.episci.com/newsroom/episcis-tactical-ai-competes-in-the-golden-horde-colosseum).

## Limits on the lineage

Golden Horde physical glide-bomb demonstrations, Colosseum's virtual cruise-missile competition, later hardware-in-the-loop integration, and current supersonic/guidance research are distinct evidence categories. No specific public technical transfer document was established between every step. Gremlins, CODE, Gray Wolf and other autonomy efforts can provide historical comparison when supported by their own primary records; they should not be drawn as a single continuous product family merely because they share suppliers, themes or Air Force sponsorship.
