# Historical programme characters and independent interview leads

Roles below are dated facts, not assertions that these people lead the current FA8651 awards. Questions seek history, acquisition, assurance and accountability, not restricted design information. No one has been contacted.

## People with direct historical programme roles

### Garry Haase
- Evidence: AFRL identified him as Munitions Directorate director in its [7 January 2021 Golden Horde account](https://afresearchlab.com/afrl-demonstrates-first-collaborative-weapon-technologies/). Its [27 July 2021 change-of-command report](https://afresearchlab.com/afrl-munitions-directorate-welcomes-new-director/) records the handover on 13 July and his planned retirement at that month's end. The [current regional defence-support organisation page](https://mail.militaryfriendlynwf.com/about/) describes him as a retired colonel and its chair.
- Character: the institutional leader who can explain the decision to demonstrate collaboration on existing munitions and then build a testing ecosystem.
- Ask: What prevented the initial demonstrator from becoming an inventory programme? What evidence would distinguish a new transition from another round of enabling research?
- Departure: supported in 2021, not recent and not a departure from one of the audited 2024–25 awards.

### Steven Stockbridge and Norma Taylor
- Evidence: AFRL's [27 May 2021 release](https://afresearchlab.com/afrl-successfully-completes-golden-horde-collaborative-small-diameter-bomb-flight-demonstrations/) names Stockbridge as Golden Horde principal investigator and Taylor as CSDB programme manager.
- Character: close observers of the flight campaign, its initial failure and subsequent success.
- Ask Stockbridge: Which integration lesson mattered most, and what public milestone would prove the lessons reached operational equipment?
- Ask Taylor: What did the demonstrated connection to the wider command network actually establish, and what did it leave untested?
- Status: historical roles verified; present roles and departures not established. Route an inquiry through AFRL rather than treating an old title as current.

### Pete Crump and Kyle Keppler
- Evidence: [GTRI, 11 November 2021](https://gtri.gatech.edu/newsroom/collaborative-weapons-work-together-respond-changing-conditions) identifies Crump as the senior research engineer leading autonomy components and Keppler as the senior research engineer leading the algorithm system.
- Character: research engineers who adapted earlier aircraft-autonomy work to a different class of vehicle.
- Ask: What kinds of validation had to be repeated when transferring autonomy between platforms? How should a reporter distinguish reuse of expertise from reuse of certified capability?
- Status: no verified departure or demonstrated connection to the exact later supersonic award in this review.

### Brandon Coloe and Christopher DeMay
- Evidence: [APL, 8 December 2021](https://www.jhuapl.edu/news/news-releases/211208-golden-horde-operation-protovision-first-competition-debuts-new-simulation-environment) identifies Coloe as Colosseum technical lead and DeMay as manager of the Force Projection Sector's Intelligent Combat Platforms programme.
- Character: the facilitators building a competition environment for traditional and newer suppliers.
- Ask: Which conclusions from a digital competition transferred successfully into physical tests? Who independently checks that simulation advantages persist outside the competition? How are supplier claims compared fairly?
- Status: roles dated to 2021; no current-award leadership or departure inferred.

### Adam Corley and Conor Most
- Evidence: [Air Force Test Center, 14 July 2020](https://www.aftc.af.mil/News/Article/2273730/416th-flts-afrl-tests-gray-wolf-prototype-cruise-missile/) names Capt. Corley as AFRL Gray Wolf programme manager and Most as a 416th Flight Test Squadron flight-test engineer.
- Character: an acquisition/test pairing able to explain the gap between laboratory confidence and evidence from an actual flight.
- Ask: What did the Gray Wolf proof of concept leave behind for later programmes, and which publicly documented milestones were never reached?
- Status: 2020 titles only; no verified current position or departure.

### Jean-Charles Ledé and Scott Wierzbanowski
- Evidence: DARPA identifies Ledé as CODE programme manager in its [2016 Phase 2 account](https://www.darpa.mil/news/2016/code-unmanned-air-system), and Wierzbanowski in the [22 March 2019 capstone account](https://www.darpa.mil/news/2019/code-success).
- Character: successive programme managers from an earlier effort to supervise cooperating aircraft under difficult communications conditions.
- Ask: What was actually handed to the Navy at the end of CODE? What makes human supervision meaningful when the communications link is degraded? Which later connections can be documented, rather than presumed?
- Status: historical succession is documented. Present employer/departure dates were not established from sufficiently clear institutional evidence for inclusion here.

## Former senior officials who can explain decisions and continuity

### Heather Pringle
- Evidence: AFRL commander quoted in the [May 2021 Golden Horde release](https://afresearchlab.com/afrl-successfully-completes-golden-horde-collaborative-small-diameter-bomb-flight-demonstrations/); an [official change-of-command account](https://www.airforcemedicine.af.mil/News/Display/Article/3418589/afrl-welcomes-new-commander/) records her relinquishing AFRL command on 5 June 2023. [Space Foundation's current biography](https://www.spacefoundation.org/people/heather-pringle/) says she joined as CEO in July 2023.
- Ask: How did AFRL judge whether demonstrations merited transition funding? What accountability exists when research benefits several programmes rather than one identifiable product?
- Departure: verified 2023 institutional departure; broader portfolio leadership, not proven involvement in each audited contract.

### Will Roper
- Evidence: [Air Force testimony published by Congress](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-116hhrg41443/pdf/CHRG-116hhrg41443.pdf) describes Golden Horde among the initial Vanguards during his acquisition tenure. A [2023 Air University article](https://www.airuniversity.af.edu/JIPA/Display/Article/3371631/seize-the-technological-high-ground-for-success-in-great-power-competition/) by his former military assistant expressly dates his departure to January 2021. [NDIA's March 2026 event biography](https://www.ndia.org/events/2026/3/18/digital-transformation-for-acquisition-moving-faster-than-bullet-time/speakers/will-roper) identifies him as Istari Digital founder and CEO.
- Ask: Were Vanguards intended to produce identifiable fielded systems or transferable infrastructure? What procurement evidence now would show the promised acceleration actually happened?
- Interest disclosure: now leads a digital-engineering company. Useful acquisition character, not a disinterested evaluator or a recent programme departure.

### Michael C Horowitz
- Evidence: [Penn's 19 August 2024 interview](https://perryworldhouse.upenn.edu/news-and-insight/perry-world-house-qa-catching-up-with-pwh-faculty-director-michael-c-horowitz/) says he had returned after two years in the Pentagon, latterly as inaugural deputy assistant secretary for force development and emerging capabilities. ## Independent counterpoints

### Paul Scharre at CNAS
- [Current institutional biography](https://www.cnas.org/people/paul-scharre) identifies him as executive vice president and says he led the working group that drafted the original 3000.09 policy. His [2016 operational-risk report](https://s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/files.cnas.org/hero/documents/CNAS_Autonomous-weapons-operational-risk.pdf) supplies a long-running critique of reliability, human supervision and operational risk.
- Ask: Which claimed advances are genuinely new rather than old automation under an AI label? What evidence is necessary before a research contract should be described as a change in how lethal force is authorised?
- Position: independent of the named programme teams, but a former policy participant. Do not imply neutrality about the policy he helped develop.

### Vincent Boulanin and Netta Goussac at SIPRI
- [Boulanin's current biography](https://www.sipri.org/about/bios/dr-vincent-boulanin) identifies him as Governance of AI programme director. Their [February 2026 procurement study](https://www.sipri.org/publications/2026/other-publications/responsible-procurement-military-artificial-intelligence) argues that states need independent capacity to test suppliers and clear accountability in acquisition decisions.
- Ask: What assurance obligations should appear in contracts themselves? Does a competitive simulation create an independent test, or can it merely reproduce suppliers' assumptions? What should happen to assurance when software changes after acceptance?
- Related primary research: [SIPRI/ICRC, June 2020](https://www.sipri.org/sites/default/files/2020-06/2006_limits_of_autonomy_0.pdf) distinguishes constraints on a system, its environment and human supervision.

### Ingvild Bode at the University of Southern Denmark
- [Current university profile](https://portal.findresearcher.sdu.dk/en/persons/ingvild-bode/) identifies her as professor and Center for War Studies director; HuMach runs 2024–28. A [2026 university interview](https://www.sdu.dk/en/forskning/dias/news/news/ingvild-bode-interview) examines how machine recommendations can gradually reshape human judgment.
- Ask: What would count as evidence of meaningful human control in actual organisational practice? Can an operator formally retain authority while losing the time or understanding needed to exercise it?
- Date caution: interview displays 4/1/2026 in a locale-dependent format; cite year unless date is resolved.

### Travis J Masters and the GAO CJADC2 team
- [GAO's 8 April 2025 audit page](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-25-106454) names Masters as director, Contracting and National Security Acquisitions.
- Ask: What evidence would show that separate autonomy projects are delivering joint operational benefit? Have the unresolved governance and lessons-sharing recommendations been implemented since the audit?
- Position: independent federal oversight, not a technical performance evaluator of the exact contracts.

## Departure finding

This review established several former officials with relevant institutional memory, but did not establish a recent departure from any exact audited 2024–25 FA8651 project. Old biographies, changed titles and participation in an earlier programme are insufficient to claim such a departure. An interview request should ask about what the person directly observed and can discuss publicly, not presume access or willingness to disclose protected information.
