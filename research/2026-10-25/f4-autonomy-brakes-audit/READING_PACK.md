# Reading pack

Ranked for the investigation, not for agreement with the proposed hypothesis. “Read” below means inspected text at the access depth stated. Search-discovered metadata is separately retained in `audit/exa-discovery-register.json`; it is not a claim that every discovered webpage was read. Raw third-party narrative caches remain private; selected government procurement XML is public for auditability. Retrieval hashes and failures appear in the source and live registers and the specialist reviews.

## First: documents that change the verdict

1. **[NSPM-11, June 5, 2026, official PDF](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202600387/pdf/DCPD-202600387.pdf).** Operative policy: distinguish 90-day directive issuance from 120-day TEVV submission for review, classification and conditional publication. Primary text inspected; HTML list conversion is not authoritative paragraph lettering.
2. **[TEELAWS official SBIR award](https://www.sbir.gov/awards/215327).** Explicit ethical-instruction scope and CETLAWS lineage; no engagement decisions. SBIR's rounded money/start field requires procurement reconciliation. Read with the [April 23 Army implementation account](https://www.army.mil/article/291926/cadets_work_with_software_using_lethal_autonomous_weapons_systems_that_analyzes_tactical_decisions_provides_ethical_score).
3. **[CODORD official program](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/codord).** Express legal/ethical and dynamic-ROE ambitions; 2026 performer outputs. Read with [information-session slides](https://www.darpa.mil/sites/default/files/attachment/2024-11/codord-info-session-distro-a.pdf) and [FAQ](https://www.darpa.mil/sites/default/files/attachment/2024-11/codord-faqs.pdf): planned independent tests, prototype OT structure, ceilings and milestones are not delivered weapons-law validation.
4. **[HAMLET live primary grant](https://api.usaspending.gov/api/v2/awards/ASST_NON_FA9550261B207_097/).** New August 2026 ethical-reasoning TEV&V funding. Full award and single transaction inspected/reconciled. [MURI topic text](https://www.onr.navy.mil/assets/2025-03/N0001425SF002.pdf), printed pp56–58, supplies relevant ethical-autonomy context; matching HAMLET to that exact topic remains an inference without an award topic-number field or work plan.
5. **[RDRTec maritime classifier](https://www.sbir.gov/awards/218158) and [Areté NSAM](https://www.sbir.gov/awards/218265).** Explicit combatant/noncombatant vessel-classification objectives. Primary abstracts inspected; stale SBIR dates/dollars corrected against live awards. A class label is not a full legal-target judgment.
6. **[Navy CAMP demonstration, September 22, 2026](https://www.dvidshub.net/news/575299/navy-demonstrates-collaborative-autonomy-mission-planning-future-combat-aircraft).** Real end-to-end demonstration of operator constraints translated into autonomous execution. [April contractor announcement](https://www.ga-asi.com/ga-asi-selected-by-us-navy-pma-281-for-collaborative-autonomy-mission-planning-and-debrief-project) supplies earlier advertised scope; neither supplies the matched award value or universal enforcement proof.
7. **[SEI ethical-conduct reference architecture, full report](https://www.sei.cmu.edu/documents/6225/CaTE_LAWS_Reference_Architecture_9VryTJo.pdf).** Physical nonlethal demonstrator, requirements/ethical formalizations and substantial excluded functions. Funding statement p63. Read with [CaTE completion page](https://www.sei.cmu.edu/projects/cate-increasing-warfighter-trust-autonomous-systems-accelerate-adoption-cutting-edge-capabilities/): pilot completed spring 2025, not a current program inferred from an FFRDC vehicle.
8. **[GAO-27-107837, October 14, 2026](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-27-107837), [full report](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-27-107837.pdf).** Continuing RAI implementation, Maven integration and governance/workforce gaps. Ten selected cases are nongeneralizable; observation dates vary. Full/report text inspected.
9. **[IG DOWIG-2026-084, May 13, 2026](https://www.oversight.gov/sites/default/files/documents/reports/2026-05/DOWIG-2026-084_Redacted%20SECURE.pdf).** Primary documentary evidence of CHMR implementation failures and capacity erosion. Federal mirror successfully inspected when original host returned 403. Does not by itself establish October remediation status or formal abolition.
10. **[FPDS public notice](https://www.fpds.gov/common/html/public_welcome_text.html).** Ninety-day DoD contract release delay, plus limits of FPDS content. Essential to every continuation/absence inference; not automatically a delay rule for every assistance record.

## Then: technical and institutional distinctions

11. **[September 2025 autonomous-systems DT&E guidebook](https://www.cto.mil/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/DTE-of-AS-GB.pdf).** Ethics implementation/testing gaps alongside ROE-related LVC and certification methods. Printed pp51–52, 124, 172–173 inspected. Guidance is not universal implementation or an NSPM-11 completion receipt.
12. **[February 2025 AI-enabled systems DT&E guidebook](https://www.cto.mil/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/TE_of_AIES_Guidebook_Final_26Feb25.pdf).** AI testing and human-system integration; inspect actual criteria before calling engineering work normative compliance.
13. **[ASIMOV official program](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/autonomy-standards-and-ideals-with-military-operational-values).** Marks completion and describes benchmark research. Does not provide the documentary reason for every contract option decision.

Publisher direct access failed in some passes; an authorized defense-library mirror was used by the independent editor. Historical paper is not current award evidence.

16. **[ANSR](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/assured-neuro-symbolic-learning-and-reasoning) and [TIAMAT](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/transfer-from-imprecise).** Assurance architectures and semantic anchors, including ROE. 17. **[JATIC/MAITE repository](https://github.com/mit-ll-ai-technology/maite), [NRTK migration notice](https://github.com/Kitware/nrtk-jatic/blob/main/README.md).** Real testing software and provenance. A package archive during migration is not program termination.
18. **[RAI GenAI Toolkit](https://www.ai.mil/Portals/137/Documents/Resources%20Page/2024-12GenAI-Responsible-AI-Toolkit.pdf), [official DTIC-hosted SHIELD presentation](https://csiac.dtic.mil/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Webinar_CSIAC_The-DoDs-Approach-to-Responsible-AI-and-the-RAI-Toolkit_v3.pdf).** Assessment gates and illustrative tools; inclusion is not endorsement or guaranteed weapon enforcement. SHIELD singular should not become a fictional separate “SHIELDS” program.
19. **[TETRAS full award](https://api.usaspending.gov/api/v2/awards/CONT_AWD_FA248722FA002_9700_FA248718D4700_9700/).** Live exact award key, money fields and dates. Thirty-eight disclosed FPDS actions reconcile. The entire broad vehicle is not an allocable ethics budget.

21. **[Switchblade Army exercise](https://www.dvidshub.net/news/549283/1st-cavalry-division-conducts-first-switchblade-600-live-fire-fort-hood), [manufacturer 2025 Block 2 data sheet](https://www.avinc.com/images/uploads/product_docs/2025_Switchblade-600b2_Datasheet_250923_v13.pdf).** Operator wave-off capability; separate reported exercise from advertised configuration and denied-communications claims. No whole-munition spending attribution to safeguards.
22. **[Hunter WOLF/CROWS Army report, August 21, 2026](https://www.army.mil/article/294796/armaments_center_partners_with_industry_to_explore_concepts_for_integrating_lethal_payloads_to_unmanned_ground_vehicles).** Demonstrated human lethal control versus future supervisory-autonomy research. CRADA does not supply a procurement dollar amount.
