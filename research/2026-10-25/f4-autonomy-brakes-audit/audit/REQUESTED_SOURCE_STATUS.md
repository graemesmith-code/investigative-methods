## Request-ledger loss and exact accounting boundary

The first unscoped free FPDS exploration of a short delivery-order PIID produced many irrelevant parent matches. Its in-memory request metadata was overwritten during correction before preservation. Surviving cached responses are hash-inventoried, inferred URLs are labeled, and exact original times/request count are **not invented**. The final scoped pass and later durable request-start/finish events are retained. The 381 retained request observations and their 25 failures are not a claim to count every initial exploratory request. This loss affects audit completeness, not the verified final award-key/action sums.

DoD's documented 90-day contract/subcontract release restriction is not automatically applied to assistance: HAMLET's August2026 new grant and CASPER++'s September2026 continuation are already visible. Negative evidence remains bounded by each route's actual availability and scope.
