## Verified leads

### Thales: technical firing prerequisite; non-US listed family
https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2025056720A1/en
Priority 13 September 2023, WO publication 20 March 2025. Claim 1 makes high-power laser activation conditional on established tracking alignment. This is a technical firing prerequisite, not a test of target legality, civilian presence or ethical acceptability. AI use is not established. The family table lists France, EP, Australia, WO, and no US member. Absence from Google's table is not definitive absence of US filing. Family members include FR3152871B1 (grant publication 9 January 2026), EP4777795A1 (22 July 2026), AU2024341246A1 (26 March 2026). French grant page has abstract but no claims, so claim-level conclusion here attaches to the WO application, not presumed identical granted claim.
https://patents.google.com/patent/FR3152871B1/en

### MBDA UK: training/test safety, not battlefield ethics
https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2021229208A1/en
Priority 12 May 2020, WO publication 18 November 2021 (inside rolling publication window, outside five-year priority window). Claim 1 conditions laser-directed-energy-weapon firing on confirming attributes of a signal from equipment attached to the target. Claim 2 blocks firing when that signal is absent; claims 12-13 specify permission/inhibition outputs. Description explicitly explains training/testing use, including stopping when pointing cannot be confirmed. Do not present as live civilian-recognition system or AI-specific safeguard. Same family includes CA3182938A1 (opened, bibliographic and text), EP4150287B1, US12292263B2, GB2595213B (fetch failed), not independent inventions.
https://patents.google.com/patent/CA3182938A1/en

### Thales: AI navigation constraint, adjacent not weapons
https://patents.google.com/patent/EP4288847B1/en
Priority 5 February 2021; EP A1 13 December 2023; EP grant 23 April 2025. Claim 1 concerns a neural-network-guided aircraft photographing objects, with a safety corridor outside prohibited zones and training using progressively more complex simulated flights. Dependent claim 7 specifies positive rewards for staying in corridor and minimum rewards for leaving it. Reward design is not proof of a hard invariant guaranteeing no breach. Relevant adjacent AI geofencing example, not evidence of weapon deployment or fire authorization. Family includes US12393202B2, CA3207450A1, FR3119602B1, WO2022167441A1; therefore not new US-independent family merely because found through EP.
