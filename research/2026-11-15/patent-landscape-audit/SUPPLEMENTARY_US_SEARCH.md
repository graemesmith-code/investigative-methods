## Interpretation and gaps

The complementary pass demonstrates both missed prioritization within the export and relevant subject matter outside retrieved publication IDs. It does not establish omission rate: purposive examples are not random recall measurements. Continue claims/full-text screening in weak title bucket and broaden safety vocabulary beyond ethical/guardrail to abort, keep-out, external-control, disablement, inhibit and operational modes. Include post-2021 publications with older priorities. Safeguard variants are not necessarily present in every embodiment or independent claim, and published patents do not prove manufacture, military adoption or tested effectiveness.
