1. US12253603B2 — Mitsubishi Heavy Industries — Threat coping system
Grant2025-03-18; reported priority2019-07-12.
https://patents.google.com/patent/US12253603B2/en
Claim1 expressly creates a protected area for moving non-targets including commercial aircraft or satellites, and prohibits attacking a drone/UAV target when effects reach that area. Dependent6–8 consider weapon characteristics/effects. This is direct claimed protection, not merely an optional description. It does not establish autonomous ethical reasoning or successful deployment. US20220163670A1 is the earlier application from initial57 export; grant is not another invention.

US20210389070A1 in initial57 export is its earlier application, not a separate discovery.
