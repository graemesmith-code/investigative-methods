# Lawful access to sensitive records: audit the refusal and trace the document

Recorded: 12 November 2026. Status: source assessment and proposed reporting method. No information request, appeal, litigation or model benchmark was conducted.

## Reporting takeaway

A refusal is a claim about what the law permits an institution to withhold. Treat that claim as something to investigate. Identify the exact record, the rule invoked, the conditions that rule requires, and the evidence that those conditions apply. An error may concern the rule's validity, its scope, the document's origin, the institution holding it, or a disclosure duty elsewhere in the law.

The most promising use of artificial intelligence is a bounded review of actual refusals and their supporting authorities. It could help reporters find arguments worth taking to an information officer, commissioner or lawyer. Whether it improves access, saves time or reduces costs needs testing. A persuasive model answer is not a disclosure entitlement.

## What prompted this note

Felix Ringe and Linus Folkerts' [article](https://www.felixringe.com/legal-exploits/) describes three alleged German regulatory defects involving confidentiality, transit visas and soldiers' working time. Its journalism example proposes challenging a confidentiality amendment because it allegedly omits a necessary statutory authority. The example assumes an external submission without competing grounds for withholding. It is hypothetical, not a documented disclosure victory. The post supplies selected arguments, but no reproducible model evaluation, search denominator or measured success rate. Its claim that artificial intelligence could discover legal defects at scale remains unestablished by those examples alone.

### What the independent checks establish

- **The constitutional requirement is real.** [Article 80(1) of Germany's Basic Law](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gg/art_80.html) requires a regulation to identify its legal basis.
- **Citation checking requires legal interpretation.** In [Federal Administrative Court case 3 B 51.18, paragraph 35](https://www.bverwg.de/160120B3B51.18.0), the court explains that relevant authorizations must be cited, but also accepts that every subdivision need not be enumerated when all are used. A missing textual match is therefore only a candidate defect.
- **The confidentiality change is corroborated.** The [current regulation, section 9(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bkv/__9.html) contains the broader wording. The [government's explanatory document, printed page 8 / PDF page 15](https://dserver.bundestag.de/brd/2026/0312-26.pdf) explains that the previous wording concerned internally prepared material and that the change also covers incoming submissions and correspondence. The [ministry's notice](https://www.bmas.de/DE/Soziales/Gesetzliche-Unfallversicherung/Aktuelles-aus-dem-Berufskrankheitenrecht/ab-august-2026-neue-berufskrankheit-parkinson-durch-pestizide.html) confirms commencement on 1 August 2026.
- **The alleged invalidity is not established here.** The draft's preamble cites section 9(1), while [section 9(1a) of the parent statute](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_7/__9.html) addresses the council's organization. Direct retrieval of the [enacted gazette PDF](https://www.recht.bund.de/eli/bund/bgbl-1/2026/210/regelungstext.pdf) failed. The draft cannot substitute for checking the promulgated instrument. No ruling resolving this particular challenge was verified.

The strongest immediately usable lesson comes from an existing access judgment, rather than from treating this proposed invalidity argument as settled.

## Six lessons worth applying

### 1. Turn each refusal into a short, testable legal claim

Ask: which provision permits withholding; what elements must be satisfied; what facts satisfy them; and what other grounds independently prevent release? Distinguish a confidentiality label, an institutional policy, a contractual promise and a statutory prohibition. Their legal effects depend on the jurisdiction and access regime.

Start with applicability. Does the cited protection cover this type of record, this holder, this date and this purpose? If those checks fail to resolve the issue, examine the regulation's enabling authority, enactment history and relevant judgments. A highly technical validity challenge is most useful when it could actually change access to a valuable record.

Request an explicit decision and preserve the applicable review deadline. Pursue disputed entitlement through the lawful review process. A model's theory does not authorize bypassing a portal or asking an official to disregard a binding duty.

### 2. Trace the document's origin and other legitimate custodians

Make a simple map of who created a record, who received it, and which versions each institution holds. Seek an original submission separately from an annotated committee copy. A ministry, regulator, public university or court may possess related records under a different access regime; their authority to release them must be checked separately.

There is concrete German precedent. In [7 C 4.11](https://www.bverwg.de/031111U7C4.11.0), the Federal Administrative Court upheld access to a ministry's statements to Parliament's Petitions Committee. The ministry retained authority over its own information; the committee's confidentiality rules did not automatically prevent release by the ministry. The judgment also rejected the particular deliberation claim. This supports checking origin and custody. It does not make every outside submission public.

For reporting on lobbying or regulatory influence, the useful target may be the industry submission, its attachments and the covering correspondence. These can answer who sought what without requiring access to every internal discussion.

### 3. Choose the legal route that matches the information

These are established mechanisms to investigate, not newly discovered loopholes. The examples are jurisdiction-specific.

| Situation | Route worth examining | Material limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| United States federal agency withholds an entire record | Under [5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(8)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t5/s552/), examine foreseeable harm or a legal prohibition, and request separable non-exempt portions. | This does not override another law prohibiting disclosure. A recognized exemption may still protect the requested content. |
| A specific United States classified record is identifiable | Consider [mandatory declassification review](https://www.archives.gov/declassification/iscap/mdr-appeals.html), including its agency and interagency appeal process. | Eligibility restrictions apply; declassification does not resolve every other disclosure restriction. Requesting the same material under both this process and the Freedom of Information Act can require choosing one. |
| Environmental information held by a body subject to the United Kingdom's Environmental Information Regulations 2004 | Check whether it is information on emissions: [regulation 12(9)](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/foi/freedom-of-information-and-environmental-information-regulations/regulation-12-9-information-on-emissions/) disapplies four specified exceptions, including commercial confidentiality. | Other exceptions remain. An environmental connection alone does not establish that material concerns emissions. Scotland has a separate regime; this is not a Canadian rule. |
| Relevant documents were filed in United States federal litigation | Search public dockets, exhibits and judgments; consider a proper challenge to sealing where justified. The [federal courts' journalist guide](https://www.uscourts.gov/data-news/reports/handbooks-manuals/a-journalists-guide-federal-courts/accessing-court-documents-journalists-guide) describes access and limits. | Sealed documents remain unavailable absent lawful release. Material exchanged privately during litigation and never filed is not automatically open to reporters. |
| Alberta Energy Regulator records are sought | Check published data and routine products first; use the regulator's current [Access to Information process](https://www.aer.ca/about-aer/transparency/access-information) for remaining records. | Alberta's Access to Information Act took effect on 11 June 2025, replacing the access portions of the earlier legislation. Federal Canadian rules and United Kingdom environmental rules cannot simply be imported. |

Use this table to identify the next legal question, not as a universal request form.

### 4. Design a request that isolates the evidence you need

Separate existing records into identifiable groups: original outside submissions; factual measurements and datasets; final decisions and instructions; administrative transmission records; and internal advice. Request them distinctly so that a disputed category does not obscure the others.

Where suitable, request an existing attachment without internal annotations, a redacted version, or an existing register identifying records. Do not assume that factual material, metadata or final decisions are necessarily releasable, or that an authority must create a new inventory. Their treatment depends on applicable law.

United States Justice Department [guidance on foreseeable harm and partial disclosure](https://www.justice.gov/oip/oip-guidance-applying-presumption-openness-and-foreseeable-harm-standard) explains that generalized fears are insufficient and that non-exempt information must be considered for release. The reporting inference is practical: make the smallest useful unit of evidence clear.

A narrow request can establish the existence, date, author or implementation of a decision even when the reasoning remains protected. Decide in advance which of those facts would advance the story.

### 5. Treat time as part of the legal analysis

Keep separate dates for creation, submission, decision, amendment, commencement, request, refusal and appeal. Check transitional provisions and later judgments. The law at the time of the underlying event is not necessarily the law governing a present request.

Some protections change with time. For example, [5 U.S.C. § 552(b)(5)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t5/s552/) removes the deliberative-process privilege for records created at least 25 years before the request. That is not an expiration date for every privilege or exemption. Similarly, the German judgment discussed above expressly recognizes that protection can sometimes continue after proceedings finish.

For a recently closed military research program, closure makes a focused request for final deliverables, acceptance records and closure instructions worth considering. It does not itself make classified, commercial or privileged information accessible.

### 6. Make artificial intelligence produce a case for review, including the counterargument

Supply the actual request, refusal, relevant record description and verified legal sources. Require the system to produce:

1. The precise disputed proposition.
2. The controlling provision, version and relevant passage.
3. The facts that support or undermine each legal condition.
4. The strongest contrary authority and any independent withholding ground.
5. The narrowest useful request or review argument.
6. Missing evidence and the practical consequence if the argument succeeds.

For this repository's method, the comparison becomes **REFUSAL + LEGAL TEST + RECORD FACTS**. It resembles the existing [rule-based source-comparison approach](../../docs/rule-based-source-comparisons.md), but the output is a reporting decision rather than an automatic conclusion of illegality.

Retrieve the cited judgment before relying on it. Check whether it is binding, whether it has been overturned or displaced, and whether its facts match. For a consequential novel argument, get jurisdiction-specific legal review before filing or publishing it as established law.

## A bounded experiment

Use ten actual, completed refusals involving records with clear public-interest value and permission to use the material. Redact source identities and unrelated personal information before model processing. This retrospective review does not replace action on any live deadline.

- Have a reporter first identify the best next step using ordinary research.
- Run the structured review on the same source material. Record tool, model, date, settings, inputs and outputs.
- Compare usefulness: did the system find a verified alternative holder, an overlooked disclosure duty, a narrower request, or a defensible review argument?
- Record errors as well as successes: invented authority, wrong jurisdiction, superseded law, inapplicable facts and overlooked competing restrictions.
- Advance only the most valuable one or two candidates to legal review and, if appropriate, an actual request or appeal.

Measure additional useful records released, the reporting facts they establish, elapsed time, fees and human review time. Count all ten cases, including failures. Distinguish argument identified, expert accepted, authority accepted, and records actually obtained. None is a substitute for the next.

If there is no viable path to a consequential record, park the argument. A clever defect without reporting value is a poor use of a journalist's budget.

## Plain assessment

An institution's refusal can sound more authoritative than it is. Sometimes the useful move is to make it explain exactly why this particular document must stay hidden. A machine may help find the weak point, but the result that matters is a record in your hands that tells you something worth publishing. Spend the effort there.

### Reusable case record

Preserve: case identifier; jurisdiction; authority; exact record sought; public-interest question; request and refusal dates; review deadline and source; refusal text; each independent exemption; statute and regulation versions; official source links; judgment identifiers and passages; creator and other holders; proposed scope change; strongest counterargument; reviewer's conclusion; next action; fees and time; and eventual disclosure outcome.

For document-heavy work, preserve originals and extracted text separately. A missing subsection in machine-read text may be an extraction error. The [Drex assessment](drex-document-processing.md) is relevant to checking the scan before treating a legal citation as missing.

### Limits and next step

This review verified selected primary authorities and corroborated the confidentiality amendment, but did not independently validate all three alleged regulatory defects. The original constitutional judgment cited by the article could not be fetched; the later administrative judgment was inspected instead. No exhaustive search for subsequent cases or amendments was performed, and no local lawyer reviewed a live fact pattern. Recheck authorities and deadlines for a real request.

No new open-source dataset or data repository was evaluated, so no Rabbit catalog addition is warranted. The next useful test is one consequential refusal with complete documents, not a scan of an entire country's laws.
