# Checks before publication

This is an exploratory methods package, not a validated detection system.

- Source-backed financial arithmetic and the main filing cutoffs were independently checked
- The Enron proxy itself contains the core conflict in one paragraph; the report does not claim joining documents is necessary or proven superior
- The Enron reproduction script rejects changed source hashes before applying the fixed, manually checked numerical inputs; a tampered-file test stopped without numerical output
- OCC category coverage and percentage row totals were checked; product-specific respondent counts are not systematically extracted by the script
- API totals and facet counts are kept separate from the twenty Canadian summaries inspected and three selected examples
- Coverage checks are dated September 2026 and do not claim historical January novelty
- No Jev/Exa inference or model-comparison performance is reported as completed
- Missing contemporary archive coverage, revision uncertainty, source-access errors, document-acquisition friction and unmeasured human costs remain explicit
- Full corporate filings, full news articles and unrelated Canadian records are not bundled; public-source pointers and selected aggregates support reproduction

The plain-English recommendation is a next experiment to test, not a claim that the proposed workflow already works better.
