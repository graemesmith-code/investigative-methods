**[Read the plain-English report](report.md).** We scored 1,234 completed-request summaries, obtained one seven-page release, and retained two precise acquisition priorities. No new allegation was verified.

Query plans and result files preserve queries, result order, URLs, titles and text hashes. Full third-party news text, publisher HTML, vendor documentation snapshots, credentials, and the live database are deliberately excluded. Public government-source records and the seven-page government policy scan are preserved for source review; the scan's ATI-number association comes from its publisher, not an independently obtained DND response letter.

Costs are USD0.061954 conservatively estimated Jev usage plus USD0.119 Exa-reported usage, **not a tax-inclusive audited bill**. No further paid calls, outreach or publication occurred during archival. This is an exploratory method demonstration, not a precision/recall benchmark.
