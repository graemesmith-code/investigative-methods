The study uses 16 primary-source reference entries. Actual acquisition includes 21 successful URL snapshots, two Git clones, ten pinned Parquet downloads and fifteen historical hard JSON extracts. The five whole splits contain 6,232 rows; hard splits contain 251 rows. DAR uses four subdomains totalling 173 source cases.

No paid inference, full benchmark experiment, generated Prolog execution, purchase or outreach was performed. The synthetic scoring check demonstrates a current code path; it does not establish an effect on published tables. Military assurance is accountability context only. Civilian failures cannot establish unsafe deployed weapons or legal violations.

The source manifest publishes metadata rather than third-party raw corpora. Acquired evidence is retained locally outside the repository. Future experiment sizes, costs and human hours are planning estimates and require separate authorization.
