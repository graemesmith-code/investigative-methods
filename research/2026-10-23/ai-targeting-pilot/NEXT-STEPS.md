# Proposed next steps

**Dated 23 October 2026. All steps below are proposals, NOT RUN.** Saving the pilot does not launch a new experiment, paid review, corpus scan, outreach or collection. Sequence T1 before deciding whether T2/T3 justify scaling.

## T1. Add the public sales pitch to the existing pilot — recommended first

**Bounded task:** re-read the existing 15 event/document clusters, retaining their current sources rather than expanding the corpus. Extract and code officials' explicit public justifications: military necessity/strategic competition; precision and civilian protection; human-control reassurance; sovereignty and legal sufficiency; and emergent frames that need a documented new category. Record speaker, audience when evidenced, policy scope and source language. Separate official words from reporter/editorial framing. Multiple frames may coexist; non-mention is not opposition.

**Question:** can comparable safeguards be sold through different arguments, and can apparent policy differences instead be differences in public emphasis?

**Deliverable:** an event-level matrix linking justification to the formal safeguard where the evidence supports the link, plus a short comparative note and a small set of inspectable examples. Show unpaired events and unsupported links explicitly. Do not manufacture a formal-policy match for a generic speech.

**Limits/gate:** existing sources only; no broad corpus scan, new paid service or national frequency/trend estimates. If evidence is too thin, report the yield and stop. Human validation is still outstanding. This is a proposed coding pass, not a claim that the frames are already measured.

## T2. Repair the comparison with matched policy/explanation pairs

**Bounded task:** seek up to five qualifying event-level pairs per country, each comprising an original formal-policy or proposal text and an attributable public explanation/news account about the same position. First use existing clusters. For gaps, use a prespecified shortlist of official and news sources, a fixed date window and a search/exclusion log. A reasonable initial search ceiling is 30 additional candidate documents per country, stopping earlier if five pairs qualify. Report actual yield rather than fill a quota with tangential material.

**Question:** does the contrast survive when countries are compared at equivalent levels of authority, policy scope and source type, rather than a detailed Russian working paper against a short US press quotation?

**Deliverable:** a linked event-source register, matched-pair review matrix, language/provenance flags and a revised feasibility judgment. Preserve native-language passages and labelled translations where access/licensing permits. Distinguish domestic and international audiences only where evidenced.

**Limits/gate:** maximum 15 qualifying pairs, no bulk Common Crawl job and no new paid access without separate authorization. If one country cannot supply comparable pairs, narrow the research question or use an explicitly asymmetric case study rather than publish misleading country rankings. Freeze specific source shortlist/date window before execution; do not pretend the pilot established longitudinal completeness.

## T3. Validate interpretation with independent human coders

**Bounded design:** after codebook refinement, create a held-out, stratified set of claims spanning the three countries, direct targeting/context, source types and difficult distinctions. A proposed first set is 30 claims, ten per country, scored independently by two qualified coders per relevant language before adjudication. Do not train or repeatedly tune on the held-out set. Native-language fluency, domain familiarity and source access are required.

**Question:** are the categories reproducible outside the AI workflow, particularly human judgment versus per-strike approval, existing law versus new instruments, and policy requirements versus political reassurance?

**Deliverable:** per-variable agreement and uncertainty, confusion/error log, disagreements before adjudication, adjudicated examples and revised rules. Use an appropriate reliability statistic only when category prevalence and sample size make it interpretable; a small set cannot certify whole-corpus validity.

**Limits/gate:** reviewer availability and compensation are unresolved. No human reliability result exists today. Obtain a bounded fee quote/budget and approval before hiring or transmitting material to external reviewers. Additional AI passes do not substitute for this independent human check.

## Only then: a longitudinal corpus study

Scale only after demonstrating comparable source coverage, retrieval precision/recall, date validity, stable source-language availability and acceptable interpretive reliability. Preregister retrieval and inclusion rules, preserve event clustering and test sensitivity to outlet/document mix. Choose the largest period that the verified data supports; do not assume continuous coverage through 2026. The abandoned broad Common Crawl extension is not revived by these proposals.
