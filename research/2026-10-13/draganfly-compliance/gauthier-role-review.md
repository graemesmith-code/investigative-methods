# Michel Gauthier role before the Draganfly CAF award

Research memo for Graeme Smith | 13 October 2026 | Public-source review

## Finding

The strongest substantiated lead is an established lobbying relationship that predates the public advisory appointment. Gauthier's Draganfly consultant registration began on 25 August 2025. The company announced his Military Advisory Board appointment on 20 February 2026. His registration links two pre-award communication reports with senior DND officials. Those records warrant examining agendas, briefing material and follow-up decisions, but do not establish improper influence or a connection to the particular winning bid.

The retirement waiting-period hypothesis is substantially weakened by the evidence: his service ended in May 2009, approximately seventeen years before the award. A later qualifying public appointment could change that analysis, but none was established here. This is not a legal clearance or a finding that every disclosure was complete.

## Identity and the two distinct roles

The subject is Lieutenant-General (retired) Michel Gauthier, former Commander of Canadian Expeditionary Force Command. Do not confuse him with the former Bloc Québécois leader, a police medal recipient of the same name, or other officials named Gauthier.

**Government relations consultancy.** Official registration 892334-382133, version 1, identifies Gauthier as a consultant with Rubicon Strategy for Draganfly Inc., effective 25 August 2025. The displayed search record says posted 27 August 2025. It includes pursuing CAF/government drone contracts and Ukraine-related contracts, alongside policy/program engagement. This supports describing a declared commercial lobbying mandate; it does not establish the first invoice date, compensation, exclusivity, employee status, or every activity performed. [Registration](https://lobbycanada.gc.ca/app/secure/ocl/lrs/do/vwRg?cno=382133&regId=973399), [official search record](https://lobbycanada.gc.ca/app/secure/ocl/lrs/do/advSrch?adv_2001_govInst=215&adv_2001_level1reg=govInst&adv_2002_activeOn=2025-08-31&adv_2002_level1reg=activeOn&documentType=registration&op_2002=AND&srch=Search).

**Military advisory role.** Draganfly's 20 February announcement places him on its Military Advisory Board and emphasizes military experience and government engagement. The same announcement was filed on EDGAR. Neither the announcement nor the relevant AIF passage inspected supplies an engagement agreement, pay terms, equity award or a mandate for this particular procurement. “Advisory board” should not be silently converted into corporate director, officer or employee. [Issuer announcement](https://draganfly.com/press-release/draganfly-welcomes-lieutenant-general-retd-michel-gauthier-to-military-advisory-board-in-support-of-canadas-defence-industrial-strategy/), [SEC exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1786286/000149315226007532/ex99-1.htm), [24 March AIF, subsequent-events chronology](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1786286/000149315226012377/ex99-1.htm).

The substantive follow-up is therefore whether the February appointment supplemented an existing Rubicon assignment, changed his duties or compensation, or affected the accuracy of later registration information. A continued consultant designation is not itself inconsistent with an advisory role.

## What the rules suggest for this person

**Cooling-off periods are not the leading concern on the present facts.** The general rules were verified in the separate post-employment QA addendum. A one-year period from the reported 2009 service end, or even a five-year period from a hypothetical designated office ending then, would not explain a restriction in 2025–26. A recent advisory appointment does not restart a retirement clock. Still check any later government appointment or return to service before closing this question.

**Consultant obligations remain relevant.** His actual registration is consultant, so the corporate-employee small-volume exception is not the natural starting point. The public start/posting dates do not show a late initial filing on their face; both June/July reports were posted well before the following month's 15th. This is a limited date comparison, not verification of underlying filing dates or completeness. September communications generally need reporting by 15 October: as of 13 October, an absent September entry cannot be called overdue. [Lobbying Act, section 5](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/L-12.4/page-1.html)

**Compensation needs evidence.** Obtain the Rubicon engagement and advisory agreement, fees, equity grants, amendments and any award-linked compensation. Consultant contingency-payment restrictions make the distinction important. A publicly advertised business-development vacancy shared through his LinkedIn profile mentions commission and equity, but that is not his compensation and must not be attributed to him. No payment violation was established. [Act, section 10.1](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/L-12.4/page-2.html), [public profile lead](https://ca.linkedin.com/in/michel-gauthier-b0804a42)

**Continuing duties need a specific factual bridge.** Prior access to protected information, participation in drafting requirements, an undisclosed decision-maker interest or preferential treatment could matter independently of elapsed time. No reviewed source establishes those facts. Do not apply the Conflict of Interest Act automatically because he was a general; test the actual office and statute. The registry's non-designated-office description is relevant evidence, not a substitute for that analysis.

## Priority evidence tests

1. **Reconstruct both DND contacts.** Match the two reports to calendars, invitations, attendees, briefing decks, handwritten notes and resulting instructions. Innocent explanation: ordinary market engagement, broader capability discussions or Ukraine procurement unrelated to the eventual award.
2. **Trace any DDI-specific contribution.** Compare contemporaneous industry submissions with draft requirements, change histories and equal-access records. Innocent explanation: permitted consultation available to other suppliers, with independent drafting/evaluation safeguards.
3. **Resolve dual roles and compensation.** Determine who contracted with whom, what services Gauthier personally delivered, and whether advisory work changed the lobbyist-client relationship. Innocent explanation: complementary technical/strategic advice and separately disclosed consulting.
4. **Check the post-contact decision chain.** Identify whether Hadwen or Fox directed anything relevant to qualification, security review, invitation, evaluation, approval or options. Mere seniority is insufficient; the needed evidence is an actual instruction or participation and its effect.
5. **Reconcile reports with independent records.** Look for communication-level mismatches only after confirming reportability, filing deadlines, later amendments and shared meetings. Written contact and contacts with non-designated officials will not necessarily appear as monthly reports.
6. **Check confidentiality and conflicts precisely.** Identify the particular protected material, relationship, gift or prior official task, its source and applicable duty before suggesting unfair advantage.

## Negative findings and limitations

This pass did not establish that Gauthier drafted the RFP, influenced scoring, obtained confidential competitor information, negotiated this specific contract, secured a waiver, received a success fee, or personally participated in the March demonstration. It also did not obtain his retirement instrument, advisory agreement or meeting materials. Those are unknowns, not allegations.

Searches included the company's announcements, EDGAR-indexed filings, official lobbying records, DND archives, Commons records and public professional-profile results. The AIF's Rubicon text search returned no match; that does not rule out an agreement that was not individually disclosed. Secondary lobbying indexes are leads only. The official report pages were available through web retrieval, but a direct filesystem download of a registry page returned HTTP 403; no raw HTML capture was successfully archived. Retrieved page footers show August 2026 modification dates and web-cache crawl dates, so this memo does not certify a complete live registry export through 13 October. A renewed registry export after the September reporting deadline would close a meaningful remaining gap.

No outreach, paid requests, complaints, public allegations or external publication were made in this review. Public business/home contact details appearing in the registry were deliberately omitted from this memo as irrelevant.
