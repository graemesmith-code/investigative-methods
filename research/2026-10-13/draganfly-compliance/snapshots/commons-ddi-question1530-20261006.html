Q-1530 , 45th Parliament, 1st session May 26, 2025, to present

Question details

Asked by
Scott Anderson (Vernon—Lake Country—Monashee)
Date asked
October 6, 2026
Awaiting response
November 23, 2026
With regard to the Defence Drone Initiative and the Defence Drone Initiative Marketplace: (a) what are the Defence Drone Initiative's approved objectives and what is the projected timeline associated with each objective; (b) which minister and senior official are responsible for overseeing the Defence Drone Initiative's overall budget, schedule and delivery of operational capability; (c) what are the respective Defence Drone Initiative responsibilities and reporting relationships of National Defence, the Canadian Armed Forces, the Canadian Coast Guard, Public Services and Procurement Canada, the Defence Investment Agency, the Canadian Joint Forces Command, the Joint Uncrewed Systems Office, Defence Research and Development Canada, and any other participating federal organization; (d) what boards, steering committees, working groups or advisory bodies govern or advise the Defence Drone Initiative, and, for each, what (i) are its mandate and establishment date, (ii) are the names, titles, and affiliations of its chair and members, (iii) are its decision-making or advisory powers, (iv) is its reporting relationship, (v) are its meeting dates; (e) who has final authority for making decisions on (i) operational requirements and priorities, (ii) supplier qualification, (iii) procurement strategies and contract awards, (iv) budget changes, (v) acceptance and fielding of equipment, (vi) production increases, (vii) suspension or termination of projects; (f) how many positions or full-time equivalents support the Defence Drone Initiative, broken down by organization, type of position and by military or civilian employees; and (g) broken down by fiscal year since the Defence Drone Initiative was established and for each of the next five fiscal years, what was the organization's actual or projected spending, in total, and broken down by type of expense?
Topics
Defence Remotely piloted aircraft
This question is awaiting a response. The response is expected by November 23, 2026.
