Meet the MINERVA Initiative team
MINERVA regularly hosts events, workshops and working groups that bring together soldiers, industry partners, researchers and government organizations.
These activities support collaboration, information sharing and the development of uncrewed systems capabilities in support of Army modernization.
Upcoming events
Details of upcoming events, workshops and information sessions will be published as they become available.
Please check this page regularly for updates.
