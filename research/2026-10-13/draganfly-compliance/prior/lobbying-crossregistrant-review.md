# Draganfly cross-registrant lobbying review

Research cut-off: October 13, 2026. Public-source review only. No contact with officials, lobbyists, or the company. This is an evidentiary chronology, not a finding of wrongdoing.

## Main findings

1. The federal registry corroborates a substantial senior-official contact trail. Andre Corbould has 12 current communication reports, including reports naming Army Commander Michael Wright on March 24 and 25, July 20 and 21, August 7, and September 8, 9 and 10, 2026. These are **report records, not 12 independently established meetings**.
2. Three different consultants filed July 21 reports naming Deputy Minister Christiane Fox. Andy Singh's filing also names Acting Assistant Deputy Minister (Materiel) Robert Gray. They may describe one shared communication, multiple communications, or overlapping participation. The registry provides no event identifier, time, attendance sheet or meeting minutes to resolve that question.
3. Gauthier and Cadario both report June 23 contact with Wendy Hadwen. Cadario additionally lists Ty Curran and Nicolas Verrault. Again, two filings should not automatically be counted as two meetings or treated as inconsistent evidence of attendance.
4. None of the inspected report narratives identifies CW2467949, the August 27–September 3 solicitation, a bid score, evaluator, award decision, quantity, or contract price. Reported subjects establish defence/UAV advocacy and contracting interests, not a proven connection to the particular award.
5. September reporting was not complete as of the cut-off: the ordinary September deadline is October 15. The absence of an additional September filing is not evidence that no contact occurred or that a report is overdue.

## Counting and legal limits

The OCL explains that consultant lobbyists individually report communications in which they participate. Consequently several registrants may describe the same event. Reportable communications include oral, arranged conversations, not just in-person meetings. Monthly reports are not exhaustive records of every email, informal exchange or contact with every public servant. Sources: https://www.lobbycanada.gc.ca/en/reports-and-publications/improving-the-lobbying-act-preliminary-recommendations and https://lobbycanada.gc.ca/en/registration-and-compliance/frequently-asked-questions/

The ordinary deadline is the 15th of the following month. September's deadline is therefore October 15, two days after this review. For the inspected June–September entries, displayed posting dates fall no later than the ordinary following-month deadline. A posted date alone cannot settle filing compliance in every case, especially where amendments or processing differences exist. https://lobbycanada.gc.ca/en/registration-and-compliance/verification-of-monthly-communication-reports/?wbdisable=true

Gauthier's historical service and current commercial role require analysis of applicable rules and facts, not a presumption that every former military officer remains barred. This chronology makes no post-employment breach finding.
