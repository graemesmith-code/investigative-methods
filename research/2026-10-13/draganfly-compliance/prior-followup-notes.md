# 1. Post-employment coverage QA addendum

13 October 2026. Earlier read-only review; no individual had yet been identified or linked to Draganfly by that review. It identified specificity missing in checklist checks 58–59, not a breach.

Helping a firm obtain a defence contract has no single waiting period. Classify the former office and actual work before measuring time. Paid consultant communications about contract awards and paid meeting-arranging are captured by Lobbying Act section 5; section 7's in-house subject list does not include contract awards. Policy/program/regulation/grant communications can still be captured. Do not infer missing registration or a five-year restriction breach from an employee's contract-award contact alone, or call an external adviser an employee. Sources: [s.5](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/L-12.4/page-1.html), [ss.7,10.11](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/L-12.4/page-2.html), [MCAP investigation](https://lobbycanada.gc.ca/en/investigations/reports-on-investigation/mark-aldridge-president-ceo-mcap-commercial-lp/).

The DPOH schedule identifies CDS, VCDS, maritime/land/air chiefs, Chief of Military Personnel and JAG, not all lieutenant-generals by rank. Other positions may qualify through comparable rank. OCL contextual factors include seniority, authority and pay; no single factor determines it. Seek appointment history and written status advice. Sources: [regulations](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2008-117/FullText.html), [interpretation](https://lobbycanada.gc.ca/en/rules/the-lobbying-act/advice-and-interpretation-lobbying-act/identifying-designated-public-office-holders/).

Five years run from leaving designated duties, not necessarily CAF retirement. Section 10.11 distinguishes consultant/organization work from the corporation-employee significant-work exception. Check any Commissioner exemption and conditions; a CAF waiver does not waive statute. The 2026 interpretation regards eight hours **or more** in consecutive four weeks as significant, including preparation/drafting. A grandfathered cohort hired before July 16, 2025 can retain the previous not-exceeding-32-hours treatment with the same corporation; changing employer ends it. Hires from that date come under the new rule January 19, 2026. Do not substitute blanket old 20% wording or conflate an individual's limits with employer collective registration. Sources: [five-year interpretation](https://lobbycanada.gc.ca/en/rules/the-lobbying-act/advice-and-interpretation-lobbying-act/five-year-restriction-on-lobbying-for-former-designated-public-office-holders/), [transition](https://lobbycanada.gc.ca/en/rules/the-lobbying-act/advice-and-interpretation-lobbying-act/five-year-restriction-on-lobbying-for-former-designated-public-office-holders/transitional-provisions-application-to-former-designated-public-office-holders-employed-by-corporations/).

DAOD 7021-2 broadly covers Regular Force lieutenant-colonels/commanders and above, and corresponding Class B/C reserve officers. Its one-year limits concern DND/CAF direct contact, specified other-government contact, employment with significant-dealings entities and specified nonpublic advice. Regular Force clock starts retirement leave or release, whichever first. Obtain exit advice, declaration, prior dealings, waiver/reduction and conditions if relevant. Office exclusions in 3.3–3.4 and bid conflict/unfair advantage in 4.3 are separate. Source: [DAOD](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/corporate/policies-standards/defence-administrative-orders-directives/7000-series/7021/7021-2-post-employment.html).

COIA coverage must be tested using its actual office/appointment definitions. If covered, ss.33–34 address improper advantage, switching sides on handled Crown matters and nonpublic-information advice without a one-/five-year expiry. Reporting office holders have additional ss.35–36 limits, generally one year for non-ministers, with s.39 powers. Older DAOD military-judge wording cannot override the current statute's exclusion. Sources: [definition](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-36.65/page-1.html), [postemployment](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-36.65/page-3.html).

Expired periods do not authorize disclosure/unfair advantage. Information-security statute s.4 can concern information acquired through military/former Crown service; ss.8–14 separately concern permanent secrecy for designated persons/posts/units. Do not assume every retiree has that particular designation. Establish protected material, provenance, access authority and precise criminal elements with legal review. Sources: [s.4](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/O-5/page-1.html), [ss.8–14](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/O-5/page-2.html).
