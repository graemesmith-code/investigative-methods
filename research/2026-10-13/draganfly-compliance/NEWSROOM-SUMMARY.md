# Draganfly procurement: newsroom summary

13 October 2026. Independent review corrections incorporated. Public records establish a disclosed lobbying relationship before a small initial drone procurement accompanied by much larger issuer-described options. They do not connect a particular contact to the requirements, evaluation or award decision. No misconduct or complete compliance has been established.

For the shorter editorial judgment and named contact chronology, start with [What this means](WHAT-THIS-MEANS.md). This file supplies the detailed findings; exact legal tests and the report inventory remain in the annexes.

## What is established

The federal procurement portal, CanadaBuys, identifies Draganfly Inc., CW2467949, C$439,851.55, September 10, 2026–September 10, 2031, and selective competition under the first stream of the Defence Drone Initiative supplier marketplace. The signed instrument remains missing. Draganfly's September 11 filed release describes 100 firm systems with associated equipment/support and 4,900 discretionary optional systems worth approximately C$24.25 million if fully exercised. Options require written authorization or amendment. Those claims are not paid expenditure, booked revenue or an immediate 5,000-system production duty. The initial aggregate is not a bare-aircraft price. [Government record and limits](annexes/procurement-records.md), [filed release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1786286/000149315226042365/ex99-1.htm).

Retired Lieutenant-General Michel Gauthier's disclosed consultant registration dates from August 25, 2025. That field is not a verified engagement-contract signing date. Rubicon Strategy is the disclosed firm. Draganfly announced his Military Advisory Board appointment February 20, 2026; no reviewed source establishes corporate directorship, employment, bid authorship, retained Crown authority or payment terms. Two preserved reports identify June 23 contact with Wendy Hadwen, a National Defence assistant deputy minister, and July 21 contact with Christiane Fox, identified as the department's deputy minister. Their broad subjects concern Canadian Armed Forces applications and Ukraine contracts, without naming Astiia or this award. [Registration](https://lobbycanada.gc.ca/app/secure/ocl/lrs/do/vwRg?cno=382133&regId=973399), [reconciliation](annexes/gauthier-and-lobbying.md).

Andre Corbould, a consultant with Integrated Strategic Partners Inc., separately disclosed contacts with Canadian Army commander Lieutenant-General Michael Wright near award and defence ministerial regional adviser Quinn Rinke during bidding. Content and consequences remain unknown; they must not be attributed to Gauthier. Companion filings may concern a shared communication, and amended versions are not extra meetings. These disclosures do not exhaust all lobbying or provide a complete decision record. The bounded report inventory and exact reporting rules are in the [ledger annex](annexes/lobbying-ledger-pass-20261013.md).

## What weakens the initial suspicions

Gauthier's filed service end is May 2009; official handover and parliamentary records corroborate retired status that year. No later qualifying office was established. A restriction arising solely from a one-year or five-year period after that service cannot explain a 2025–26 prohibition. Continuing confidentiality, conflict and compensation duties require their own facts.

Four of nine inherited award rows display long terms. Volatus describes the same 100 firm plus 4,900 optional systems and five years despite a short displayed expiry. The original Prime Minister's backgrounder applies its evaluation and foreign ownership, control or influence assessment condition to all six named suppliers and describes several allied foreign designs. These features do not establish a Draganfly-specific exception. No nine-contract matched-scope comparison or original peer-contact census was recovered. [Comparator test](annexes/comparator-falsification-20261013.md).

A newly acquired original DND notice names Draganfly Innovations Inc. on a C$23,800 competitively sourced contract dated January 22, 2010. Further issuer filings document 2017 and 2019 amalgamations linking the older business to today's subsidiary group. The current Controlled Goods directory and matching detail name that subsidiary. This qualifies a sweeping no-prior-business narrative; it does not establish the modern parent's own completed military production, Astiia experience or permitted tender credit. Certificates and the operative qualification clause remain missing. [Historical record](annexes/review-corrections-and-history-20261013.md), [entity and certification chain](annexes/legal-entity-and-certification-chain-20261013.md).

## Questions that still matter

The first bid request's aircraft title conflicts with its ground-vehicle body. Secondary cache differences and current mirrors have not authenticated a bidder amendment, extension or unequal distribution. The original qualification documents, supply arrangement, operative bid-request versions and evaluation records remain missing.

The earliest verified Canadian Astiia licence claim found here is the government's September 10 backgrounder. No dated pre-award licence instrument was recovered. Material-contract and compensation summaries have period/category limits; they cannot establish absent agreements or Gauthier's pay. Unnamed May 2025 adviser shares cannot be assigned to him. [Filings/version test](annexes/filings-and-version-provenance-20261013.md).

Approval and pricing require the actual value estimate, signing authority, option treatment and supporting decisions. Different departments and procurement routes have different limits, so the public amount cannot establish an approval breach by itself. The [rule-by-rule analysis](annexes/compliance-concern-cards-20261013.md) preserves the exact thresholds and legal distinctions.

The exemption, security and compensation questions also require actual instruments and payment terms. No exemption letter, completed award-specific security decision, prohibited fee or false declaration has been established. The timing and continuing contract obligations are explained in the [verified legal corrections](annexes/review-corrections-and-history-20261013.md).

September lobbying returns ordinarily fall due October 15; July–September contract publication is ordinarily due October 30. Neither had passed on October 13. Public silence, access failures and document-limited checklist cells are not proof of missing compliance or misconduct.

The useful next evidence is signed scope/prices, qualification/version timestamps, rights/security decisions, approval chain and contact follow-up linked to procurement.
