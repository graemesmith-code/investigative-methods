# Draganfly procurement: newsroom summary

13 October 2026. Independent review corrections incorporated. Public records establish a disclosed lobbying relationship before a small initial drone procurement accompanied by much larger issuer-described options. They do not connect a particular contact to the requirements, evaluation or award decision. No misconduct or complete compliance has been established.

## What is established

CanadaBuys identifies Draganfly Inc., CW2467949, C$439,851.55, September 10, 2026–September 10, 2031, and selective competition against DDI Stream 1. The signed instrument remains missing. Draganfly's September 11 filed release describes 100 firm systems with associated equipment/support and 4,900 discretionary optional systems worth approximately C$24.25 million if fully exercised. Options require written authorization or amendment. Those claims are not paid expenditure, booked revenue or an immediate 5,000-system production duty. The initial aggregate is not a bare-aircraft price. [Government record and limits](annexes/procurement-records.md), [filed release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1786286/000149315226042365/ex99-1.htm).

Michel Gauthier's disclosed consultant registration dates from August 25, 2025. That field is not a verified engagement-contract signing date. Rubicon Strategy is the disclosed firm. Draganfly announced his Military Advisory Board appointment February 20, 2026; no reviewed source establishes corporate directorship, employment, bid authorship, retained Crown authority or payment terms. Two preserved reports identify June 23 Hadwen and July 21 Fox contacts with broad CAF/Ukraine subjects, without naming Astiia or this award. [Registration](https://lobbycanada.gc.ca/app/secure/ocl/lrs/do/vwRg?cno=382133&regId=973399), [reconciliation](annexes/gauthier-and-lobbying.md).

Corbould separately disclosed Wright contacts near award and Rinke contacts during bidding. Content and consequences remain unknown; they must not be attributed to Gauthier. Companion filings may concern a shared communication, and amended versions are not extra meetings. Consultant monthly reporting covers prescribed oral, advance-arranged communications with designated officials, with subject/initiator distinctions. It does not exhaust written lobbying, spontaneous contacts or other officials. Registration also covers expected activities. The 44-row inventory is a bounded report ledger, not 44 proven meetings or all lobbying. [Ledger and legal scope](annexes/lobbying-ledger-pass-20261013.md).

## What weakens the initial suspicions

Gauthier's filed service end is May 2009; official handover and parliamentary records corroborate retired status that year. No later qualifying office was established. A restriction arising solely from a one-year or five-year period after that service cannot explain a 2025–26 prohibition. Continuing confidentiality, conflict and compensation duties require their own facts.

Four of nine inherited award rows display long terms. Volatus describes the same 100 firm plus 4,900 optional systems and five years despite a short displayed expiry. The original PM backgrounder applies its evaluation/FOCI condition to all six named suppliers and describes several allied foreign designs. These features do not establish a Draganfly-specific exception. No nine-contract matched-scope comparison or original peer-contact census was recovered. [Comparator test](annexes/comparator-falsification-20261013.md).

A newly acquired original DND notice names Draganfly Innovations Inc. on a C$23,800 competitively sourced contract dated January 22, 2010. Today's issuer statements list a subsidiary with that name. This qualifies a sweeping no-prior-business narrative; it does not prove entity continuity, completed military production, Astiia experience or a 2026 qualification criterion. [Historical record and limits](annexes/review-corrections-and-history-20261013.md).

## Questions that still matter

The RFP-A aircraft title conflicts with its ground-vehicle body. Secondary cache differences and current mirrors have not authenticated a bidder amendment, extension or unequal distribution. Operative RFSA/SA/RFP versions and evaluation records remain missing.

The earliest verified Canadian Astiia licence claim found here is the government's September 10 backgrounder. No dated pre-award licence instrument was recovered. Material-contract and compensation summaries have period/category limits; they cannot establish absent agreements or Gauthier's pay. Unnamed May 2025 adviser shares cannot be assigned to him. [Filings/version test](annexes/filings-and-version-provenance-20261013.md).

Approval and pricing require actual records. The official directive includes DIA within PSPC references and gives a C$90 million competitive-goods basic limit, distinct from DND's C$9 million. Lower delegation/instrument limits and risk routes can still matter. The March 25 guide separately requires price consultation at C$10 million competitive estimates including taxes/options/amendments, C$7.5 million including taxes/options for one compliant offer, and C$7.5 million including taxes for specified negotiated-pricing situations. Actual estimate, price provisions and consultation remain unknown. [Exact rules](annexes/compliance-concern-cards-20261013.md).

An NSE requires the responsible ADM/equivalent's signed letter dated **before the award day**. If September 10 is the actual award date, a letter dated September 10 would not satisfy that wording; no letter was recovered. GCR section 18 survives the NSE exclusion and supplies deemed contingency-fee declarations. Its compensation-record provision invokes the contract's actual accounting/audit terms, without establishing those terms or journalist access. No prohibited fee or false declaration is established. [Verified legal corrections](annexes/review-corrections-and-history-20261013.md).

September lobbying returns ordinarily fall due October 15; July–September contract publication is ordinarily due October 30. Neither had passed on October 13. Public silence, access failures and document-limited checklist cells are not proof of missing compliance or misconduct.

The useful next evidence is signed scope/prices, qualification/version timestamps, rights/security decisions, approval chain and contact follow-up linked to procurement. DND title reference DRDC2026-0005257, received July 3, adds a precise program-context lead; the briefing itself was not obtained. The [ranked records plan](annexes/ranked-records-plan-draft-20261013.md) remains a draft. No outreach or records requests were sent.
