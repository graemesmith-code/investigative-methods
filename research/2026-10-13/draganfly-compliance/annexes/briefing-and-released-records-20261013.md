## An actual tabled response, with a narrower question

[Commons Q-1241](https://www.ourcommons.ca/written-questions/45-1/Q-1241), asked May 29, was answered and tabled September 21, 2026 as Sessional Paper **8555-451-1241**. It concerns government-owned drones manufactured in China. The [public full-text response](https://www.ourcommons.ca/written-questions/45-1/q-1241/14224001) initially showed no selected sections; selecting National Defence and Public Works through the visible controls exposed their actual response text. The rendered HTML and labelled selected-response extraction are archived.

DND's ministerial response says National Defence and the Coast Guard use such drones for operational and training activities; details of systems and assessments are withheld for national and operational security. It says departmental review, oversight, controls, limits on sensitive information and restrictions on network connectivity apply. That is an attributable government statement, not an acquired assessment, configuration inventory or independent verification of those safeguards.

PSPC reports owning 20 Chinese-manufactured drones and describes local recording, government storage and reduced connectivity. Its make/model table still displayed “Loading...” during capture, so no model breakdown was read or reconstructed. Its low-risk infrastructure/imagery use is not a CAF ISR acceptance test. Neither section names Draganfly, Astiia or CW2467949. These answers cannot establish this award's FOCI result, licence rights, SRCL or clearance milestones. The holder for underlying assessment records would be the responding department and actual program/security authority, to verify; Q-1241 is the exact published response reference, not an ATI file number.
