## Concrete extension: notification after organizational change

**Question:** If Draganfly Inc. was CSP-registered under the relevant security agreement, did its security officer promptly notify the CSP of the September 29 equity issuance and any relevant corporate-structure changes, and did the resulting review preserve or revise the applicable screening/mitigation position?

### Rule actually recovered

The official [Contract Security Manual](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-services-procurement/services/industrial-security/security-requirements-contracting/contract-security-manual-contracting-government-canada/contract-security-manual.html) identifies itself as effective August 13, 2020. Annex A VI, Organizational changes, requires the CSO/ACSO to promptly notify CSP when aware of changes in legal status, corporate structure, ownership or key-senior-official list, initiating registration/screening review. Section 3.2.1 also addresses keeping CSP informed after screening. Section 3.3 says foreign ownership does not itself preclude clearance; risk mitigation and CSP judgment matter. Applicability is tied to CSP registration and relevant security requirements. The current text was read, but a frozen September 2026 version was not authenticated here. Its effective-title date alone does not prove every current sentence was unchanged then.

The separate official [security officer responsibilities page](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-services-procurement/services/industrial-security/security-requirements-contracting/organization-security-screening/roles-responsibilities-security-officer.html) corroborates reporting organizational changes, including ownership, and compliance with the organization's security agreement. Its current guidance is not the actual agreement.

### Factual anchor, not a new ownership allegation

The [September 28 issuer release filed at SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1786286/000149315226044481/ex99-1.htm) announces US$5 million each from Unusual Machines and a U.S. investment fund. The parent investigation already obtained the September 29 closing and [prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1786286/000149315226044659/formsuppl.htm) arithmetic: 1,869,159 new shares, 37,273,597 before and 39,142,756 after; approximately 4.775% combined and 2.388% each, excluding prior holdings and other rights. This pass does not independently reverify those inherited calculations. No majority acquisition is established. Securities issuance, foreign control, board influence, access rights and a supplier-component relationship are separate facts.

### Smallest discriminating record set

- Applicable CSP registration/security agreement and contract security-requirements checklist, identifying exact legal entity and relevant period
- Dated organizational-change notice or acknowledgment following the financing, plus the registration/screening review disposition and any revised mitigation conditions
- If the October U.S. subsidiary formation changed the relevant registered entity's structure: corresponding notice/review; do not assume CAF work or sensitive information transferred to that subsidiary
- Operative version of the notification obligation in the applicable agreement/manual at the time

Public sources establish the event and general rule, not performance of the duty. No public notice was found; that is not non-notification, and public release of a security record cannot be assumed. A completed initial FOCI assessment, even if later established, would not answer a separate post-change notification question. Conversely, a minority U.S. investment does not establish that a fresh full FOCI assessment was mandatory or failed.
