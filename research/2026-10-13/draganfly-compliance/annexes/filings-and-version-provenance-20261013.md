The [September 28 prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1786286/000149315226044659/formsuppl.htm), incorporated-document list on printed pages 4–5, links directly to a [material-change report dated September 14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1786286/000149315226042839/ex99-2.htm), furnished September 16 according to the SEC feed. This original report was fully read. It calls September 11 the date of material change and announcement, describes 100 firm systems with associated equipment/training, discretionary 4,900 options at approximately C$24.25 million, and written authorization or amendment for exercise. Its omitted-information field is “Not applicable.”

Those issuer-reporting dates do not displace the government notice's September 10 award date. The report does not attach the signed contract, identify Astiia/AMSL licence terms, certify FOCI completion, disclose approval authority or identify Gauthier's work/payment. The omitted-information answer is about this report, not a representation that every underlying instrument is public. No disclosure breach follows from the date difference without the actual material-change facts and applicable timing rules.
