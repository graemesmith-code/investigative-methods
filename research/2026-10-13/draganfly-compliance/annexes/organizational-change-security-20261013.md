# Security notification after corporate change

Checked October 13, 2026, approximately 23:35–23:40 UTC. The [independent acquisition memo](independent-acquisition-memo-20261013.md) is preserved separately. This pass verifies a narrower post-award question; it establishes neither a missed notification nor an invalid award.

## The historical rule is now supported by an actual capture

The current [Contract Security Manual](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-services-procurement/services/industrial-security/security-requirements-contracting/contract-security-manual-contracting-government-canada/contract-security-manual.html) is headed effective August 13, 2020 and carries modified metadata March 27, 2026. Its organizational-change provision requires the security officer or alternate, once aware of changes to legal status, corporate structure, ownership or key senior officials, to inform the Contract Security Program promptly so registration and screening can be reviewed. “Promptly” is the wording; no fixed number of days was found in that provision.

A public Wayback search exposed thirteen captures, including the actual [April 22, 2026, 21:38:21 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20260422213821/https://www.canada.ca/en/public-services-procurement/services/industrial-security/security-requirements-contracting/contract-security-manual-contracting-government-canada/contract-security-manual.html). That exact linked replay returned readable HTML, archived locally. Normalized text for scope, section 3.2.1, section 3.5 and the organizational-change provision matches the existing current capture. This establishes that the relevant wording was present before September. It does not authenticate a September agreement or prove that no intervening version or contract-specific condition existed.

The duty depends on the relevant registered organization and security agreement. The manual treats subsidiaries separately and distinguishes organization clearance from contract/site safeguards. A review following change is not automatically a fresh full foreign-ownership assessment. The [security officer responsibilities page](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-services-procurement/services/industrial-security/security-requirements-contracting/organization-security-screening/roles-responsibilities-security-officer.html), modified metadata July 4, 2025, corroborates change reporting and agreement compliance; it is current guidance, not Draganfly's signed agreement.

## Smallest decisive test

First establish the exact legal entity registered with CSP, applicable agreement/manual version, contract security-requirements checklist and relevant dates. Then obtain the change notice or acknowledgment and review disposition covering the September 28 announcement/September 29 closing through October 13. Any October corporate-structure notice should use the actual effective date, which remains unknown. The likely holders are PSPC's CSP and the relevant company's security officer, with contract-specific requirements held by the contracting authority. No public notice is expected or assumed to be mandatory, and failure to find one does not establish non-notification.

This narrows the existing security questions without changing their evidence categories. Initial clearance, procurement-specific foreign-influence assessment and continuing change notification are distinct tests.
