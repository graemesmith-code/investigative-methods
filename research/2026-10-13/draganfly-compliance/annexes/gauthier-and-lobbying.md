## The identity and chronology that matter

The person is retired Lieutenant-General **Michel Gauthier**, former CEFCOM commander. [DND's May 11, 2009 record](https://www.canada.ca/en/news/archive/2009/05/change-command-canadian-expeditionary-force-command-449469.html) records the command handover to Marc Lessard. [The Commons November 25, 2009 notice](https://www.ourcommons.ca/documentviewer/en/40-2/AFGH/meeting-16/notice) calls him retired. His [Draganfly registration](https://lobbycanada.gc.ca/app/secure/ocl/lrs/do/vwRg?cno=382133&regId=973399) discloses military service ending May 2009. These establish a retired former commander, not his exact release or retirement-leave day. An association biography says summer 2009; this small precision difference needs a personnel instrument if the exact date becomes consequential.

His consultant registration through Rubicon began **August 25, 2025**. It covers CAF platform applications, Canadian government contracts and Ukraine-related contracts. Draganfly announced a **Military Advisory Board** appointment on **February 20, 2026**, repeated in its [March 24 AIF, page 17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1786286/000149315226012377/ex99-1.htm). No reviewed source gives his engagement contract, personal compensation or September-bid mandate. Advisory membership does not prove he was a corporate director, officer or employee.

The disclosed commercial relationship predates the public appointment by nearly six months. The question is what his two roles entailed, how compensation worked, and which actual procurement decisions followed the contacts. The title by itself cannot answer those questions.

Current OCL comparable-rank guidance dates September 2024, modified July 2026. It is useful for today's interpretation but cannot be represented as a 2009 individualized decision. Gauthier's military expertise and disclosed lobbying are not themselves misconduct.

The [2023 Lobbyists' Code](https://lobbycanada.gc.ca/en/rules/the-lobbyists-code-of-conduct/lobbyists-code-of-conduct-2023/) supplies relevant continuing rules independently of the service-exit clock: accurate client/purpose disclosure, good faith, confidential information, and relationships creating a sense of obligation. It explicitly says having worked for the same employer does not alone establish a close relationship. No such relationship, gift, misleading communication or confidential-information misuse was established for Gauthier here. The source dates its monetary-limit update July 7, 2026; June and July conduct must not be tested using an undated current amount. That is a version safeguard, not a gift allegation.

## Disclosure timing question and disconfirming evidence

For Corbould report 697412, the exploratory posting-date question was resolved during QA. The ordinary monthly filing rule points to September 15 for August contacts, so **this particular report is not late on those displayed dates**. The tempting August 15 deadline is wrong: it belongs to July communications. This correction defeats that hypothesis. Preserve the exact dates to avoid making it recur.

The July 20 amendment demonstrates another date trap: the original report was posted July 22, while the current amended entry displays August 15 and an amendment date July 22. Current posting is not necessarily first disclosure. The verified September 9–10 corrections change Wright's name; they do not supply extra communications.

Neither reviewed Gauthier report identifies Draganfly's RFP, contract number or Astiia. The July Ukraine subject provides a plausible separate procurement context. Draganfly's March demonstration announcement does not name Gauthier as organizer/attendee or establish evaluation of the winning product. Exact-name/product/solicitation searches found no direct public linkage in this pass; that bounded negative search cannot rule out unindexed meetings or correspondence.
