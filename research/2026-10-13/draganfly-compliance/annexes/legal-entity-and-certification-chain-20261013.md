## The chain is more specific than a shared name

The native [October 29, 2019 CSE listing statement](https://webfiles.thecse.com/DFLY_Form_2A.PDF) encloses the final October 23 prospectus and historical financial statements. It has 199 PDF pages; selected relevant sections were read, with PDF pages 16 and 68 visually checked. Those two pages establish the following **issuer-disclosed** sequence:

1. The older Draganfly Innovations Inc. was incorporated February 24, 1998. This is the predecessor named in the financial history, not the later listed parent.
2. January 1, 2017: the Alberta corporation described as formerly Trace Live Network Inc. completed a vertical amalgamation with its wholly owned Draganfly Innovations subsidiary, and used the name Draganfly Innovations Inc. The financial note describes January 1, 2017 articles under Alberta law.
3. June 1, 2018: Drone Acquisition Corp., the later parent, was incorporated in British Columbia.
4. August 15, 2019: the predecessor continued to British Columbia and amalgamated with **1187607 B.C. Ltd.**, the parent's subsidiary. The resulting wholly owned subsidiary was named Draganfly Innovations Inc.; the parent changed its name to **Draganfly Inc.**

This supports a business-successor connection between the older Draganfly name and the modern group. It also shows why the 2010 government supplier record cannot simply be described as a contract awarded to the modern parent. Certificates, executed amalgamation instruments and any contractual succession remain unacquired. A statutory conclusion about transferred liabilities or tender credit would need its own applicable law and documents; none is inferred from consolidation accounting.

The [April 30, 2020 issuer release filed with CSE](https://webfiles.thecse.com/DFLY20200430NR.pdf), acquired as a native PDF, says Draganfly Inc. completed acquisition of all shares in **Dronelogics Systems Inc.** The [March 24, 2026 annual information form](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1786286/000149315226012377/ex99-1.htm), printed pages 6 and 17, identifies three wholly owned subsidiaries and their business roles. The [June 30 financial statements, authorized August 10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1786286/000149315226036867/ex99-1.htm), note 2, again list Draganfly Innovations Inc., Draganfly Innovations USA, Inc. and Dronelogics Systems Inc. at 100%. The June schedule is not a complete September or October entity certificate.

## Current manufacturing representations are not Astiia assignments

The annual form's Canadian-operations passage, printed page 22, names Draganfly Innovations Inc. as operator of the Saskatoon leased facility. It identifies Dronelogics' separate Burnaby premises and describes that subsidiary's integration, training, maintenance and other services. One sentence uses singular “Draganfly Innovation Inc.” before the same paragraph uses the defined plural name. That isolated wording variation is not evidence of another registered entity; the subsidiary schedule remains the stronger naming source.

The government's September 10 announcement describes Canadian licensed manufacture and support under the Draganfly brand. It does not name the actual Canadian manufacturing licensee, give a corporate number or assign Astiia work to either subsidiary. The older manufacturing disclosures concern general operations and other products. They cannot prove Astiia's factory, licence holder, subcontractor, configuration or capacity at the bid deadline.
