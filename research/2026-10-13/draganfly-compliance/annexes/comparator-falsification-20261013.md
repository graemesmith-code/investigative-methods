## Strongest Gauthier narrative challenged

The strongest factual narrative is that Draganfly retained a retired operational commander as a disclosed consultant, then publicly appointed him to a military advisory board, and disclosed senior DND contacts before a later award. That supports inquiry into the decision chain. It does not supply the missing bridge between a specific contact and the winning requirements, evaluation or decision.

The [identity and source reconciliation](gauthier-and-lobbying.md) ties **Michel Gauthier** to CEFCOM, Rubicon's Draganfly registration and the issuer's February appointment. Broad name searches return unrelated people, including a same-named politician, and unrelated businesses using Rubicon. Those results cannot supply his employment history or party role. Military Advisory Board membership is not a corporate directorship. Neither consultant registration nor advisory publicity proves employment, bid authorship, contingent compensation or retained procurement authority.

Five concrete alternatives remain consistent with the reviewed records: operational advice and ordinary government-relations work; Ukraine-related opportunities separate from this CAF bid; more than one consultant reporting a shared communication; wider program engagement before the particular solicitation; and a publicity announcement after an existing commercial engagement. These are competing explanations, not established excuses. The disclosure topics and chronology support testing them.

His disclosed service ending in 2009 makes a 2025–26 retirement waiting-period claim substantially weaker. No later qualifying office has been established. Expertise does not itself prove confidential-information misuse; a historical title does not establish current control over evaluators. The June 23 and July 21 Gauthier reports name no Astiia, solicitation or award. Corbould's bidding-window contacts require their own examination and must not be attributed to Gauthier.

The investigation should now focus on actual content, participants, follow-up instructions, engagement terms and links to procurement decisions. Context reduces several dramatic shortcuts. **No misconduct or complete compliance has been established.**
