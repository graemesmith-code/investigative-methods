## Equal-access test: specific information, recipients and timing

If the CFTA chapter applies without a relevant exclusion, Article 503.5(g) identifies selectively supplying information to confer an advantage as an inconsistent practice; Article 509.7 addresses the information needed for responsive tenders; Article 510 requires new answers/clarifications to be shared openly, fairly and promptly and publication of changed criteria/requirements, with an appropriate extension where needed. Article 511 requires a reasonable tender period considering the procurement's circumstances. It does not state a universal numeric minimum. Exact provisions were checked in the archived August 27 text at printed pages 34, 43–44. A seven-day window alone does not establish a CFTA violation. Other agreements have their own rules and coverage tests; their deadlines cannot simply be substituted.

The discriminating evidence would be the actual question-and-answer documents and publication dates, bidder distribution logs, amendment redlines, invitations and eligibility, any private technical briefings and what was later made available to all eligible bidders. Public consultant reports establish communications, not their full content. No record presently proves that Draganfly received an unreleased requirement, evaluator advice, competitor information or a special extension.
