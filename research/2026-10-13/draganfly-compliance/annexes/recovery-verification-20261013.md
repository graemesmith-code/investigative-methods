## A date trap to avoid

The February 16 posting dates do not establish a one-day filing breach. February 15, 2026 was Sunday. [Interpretation Act section 26](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/I-21/section-26.html) moves a holiday deadline to the next non-holiday; [section 35](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/I-21/section-35.html) includes Sunday in the definition and specifies further local holiday possibilities. This is a reason to reject a raw day-of-month lateness inference, not a certification of the original filing timestamp or exact deadline in every jurisdiction. Both native legal pages were downloaded and hashed.

[Lobbyists Registration Regulations section 4](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2008-116/FullText.html) distinguishes deemed receipt methods; section 6 specifies oral communications arranged in advance, including relevant public-official-initiated financial-benefit/contract-award communications. This reinforces why monthly reports are not every email or contact. The operative Act, initiation facts and reporting category still matter.
