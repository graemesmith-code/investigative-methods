# Corporate changes: dates and limits

Checked 13 October 2026. This branch tests changes that might matter to actual security, supplier or contract obligations. **An investment or foreign subsidiary is not itself a breach.** Issuer sources establish disclosures; actual transaction rights and procurement duties still require documents.

## September financing

The [September 28 prospectus supplement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1786286/000149315226044659/formsuppl.htm), “The Offering,” printed page 7, gives 1,869,159 newly offered common shares, 37,273,597 before and 39,142,756 after. The [closing release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/30/3371481/0/en/draganfly-announces-closing-of-us-10-million-strategic-investment.html) is dated September 29, 21:00 ET despite its September 30 URL. It states the US$10 million investment closed. This resolves the earlier uncertainty over whether it was only proposed.

Calculation: `1,869,159 / 39,142,756 × 100 = 4.775236%`. That is the new issuance as a proportion of the stated post-offering common shares, not a beneficial-ownership determination. The [initial announcement](https://draganfly.com/press-release/draganfly-announces-strategic-investment-from-unusual-machines-and-a-leading-u-s-investment-fund/) describes equal US$5 million subscriptions from Unusual Machines and an unnamed US fund. Approximate half-issuance proportions are 2.38762% each; allocation rounding, prior holdings, warrants, other transactions and side rights are not resolved.

The issuance alone does not establish majority control. Minority ownership does not exclude influence or a reportable-event duty. The actual security/FOCI clause, ownership and governance rights, transaction agreements and any notification/reassessment are the necessary bridge to a compliance finding.

## October subsidiary

The [October 12 issuer announcement](https://rss.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/12/3378866/0/en/draganfly-establishes-dedicated-u-s-defense-subsidiary-appoints-former-under-secretary-acting-secretary-of-the-navy-thomas-modly-as-chairman.html) describes Draganfly Defense USA, Inc., Thomas Modly as chairman, Cameron Chell as director and AJ Pasagian as president appointed in August. It says Modly will relinquish his parent-board position. The announcement date is not authenticated incorporation or effective resignation date.

The release describes intended US defence governance and information/technology segregation. It does not show that the Canadian contract, Astiia licence or controlled Canadian information moved to the subsidiary. Obtain any relevant assignment, subcontract, personnel-access change or reporting obligation before suggesting migration of CAF work. The earlier March subsidiary schedule is not a complete October group map.

## New October 13 development

Draganfly [announced an investment in DoD Solution](https://draganfly.com/press-release/draganfly-makes-strategic-investment-in-ukraine-defence-technology-company-solution/), described as an Estonian-Ukrainian autonomy company, and says Chell will join its board. It describes the AURA onboard-computer/software platform and intended integration across unmanned systems. The reviewed release gives no investment amount, ownership percentage, agreement terms or reference to Astiia, CW2467949 or this procurement.

This is an outward investment by Draganfly, not evidence of a takeover of Draganfly. Its relevance is conditional: did it change the CAF configuration, approved supply chain, software/data access or a contractual reporting fact? No reviewed source establishes that it did. A general integration aspiration cannot be substituted for the CAF bill of materials or acceptance record.
