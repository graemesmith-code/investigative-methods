Research checked 13 October 2026. Cash spend: **US$0**. No paid model calls, outreach, emails or allegations. This team contributes one **conditional methods pilot**, not a proven discrepancy. No GFI or Public Eye opportunity cleared all the selection gates.

## Conditional pilot GT-PSC: compare ownership filings with their own dated control claims

Take a reproducible sample of ordinary UK private companies, selected by company number or incorporation date rather than reputation. Extract the original shareholder list and subsequent changes from public filing images. Reconstruct the percentage held at a specific date; compare it with the company's control declaration effective at that same date. The potential story is a measurable class of inconsistent public declarations, including whether corrections resolve those inconsistencies. It is not a claim of money laundering.

The original bottleneck is unusually concrete. Global Witness's [The companies we keep](https://globalwitness.org/en/campaigns/corruption-and-money-laundering/the-companies-we-keep/) recommends: “Cross-checking data against other government datasets, in particular shareholder data, which would help weed out inconsistencies.” [Original PDF](https://globalwitness.org/documents/466/The_companies_we_keep.pdf), physical PDF page 30, corresponding to web parser index P29. The site dates the report 1 December 2020, but its substantive statistical snapshot is **1 March 2018**. Do not describe its statistics as a 2020 or current audit.

**Falsifiable test.** If a complete, dated shareholder chain shows an individual holding more than 25% of ordinary shares while a legally applicable, overlapping company declaration says no qualifying person controls it, then the two filings merit human reconciliation. A shareholder total that fails to sum to the issued capital is an extraction failure, not a discovery. A corporate shareholder, nominee, differing share class, transfer, exemption, or timing gap blocks this simple test.

**Both sides and joins.** Companies House daily PSC JSON supplies company numbers, statement/record type and notified/ceased dates. Public incorporation and confirmation-statement filing images supply shareholders, share classes, numbers, changes and confirmation dates. Company number is the stable join. Names are claims in documents, not universal person identifiers. The [current CS01 documentation](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/confirmation-statement-form-cs01-new-version) states that Part 4 updates shareholder changes; one CS01 is therefore not a complete ownership snapshot. Reconstruct the baseline and all relevant changes, and separately reconstruct historical PSC events. Today's daily snapshot is overwritten and cannot by itself establish an older contradiction.

**Successful bounded access check.** A raw HTTPS download of the smallest current PSC ZIP succeeded: [psc-snapshot-2026-10-13_33of33.zip](https://download.companieshouse.gov.uk/psc-snapshot-2026-10-13_33of33.zip), 2,533,020 bytes; ZIP decompression and JSON parsing succeeded. Its first record is an overseas-entity statement, so this proves download access only, not the UK shareholder test. No case-level contradiction was tested by this team. The independent audit team was given the method and handles the filing-image check. Details and hashes are in `data-checks.json`.

**AI's restricted job.** Read awkward, scanned or changing PDF form layouts; return shareholder/class/count/date fields with page references and uncertainty. Use ordinary PDF text extraction first. Code handles arithmetic, company-number joins, time intervals, deterministic sampling and manifest validation. Humans resolve classes, nominees, indirect control and legal applicability. An LLM must never invent ownership hidden behind a company or trust.

**Why now, honestly.** This is a potentially cheaper transcription task, not newly available evidence or a new logical test. Global Witness already developed ordinary-code bulk red flags and graph exploration. It also described a previously reported individual shareholder/PSC inconsistency. The novel deliverable would be a reproducible, population-selected audit with extraction accuracy and time cost measured, not a rerun against a famous person. Cheap-model prices predate 2026; stronger vision extraction may expand affordable throughput, but this team made no paid benchmark and does not claim a demonstrated 2026 capability breakthrough. Parent's pricing annex supplies official prices.

**Innocent explanations and missing evidence.** Different effective dates, late or corrected filings, clerical mistakes, multiple share classes, shareholder nominees, corporate members, shares that lack relevant control rights and statutory exemptions can explain apparent conflicts. Even a genuine filing inconsistency does not prove concealment or criminal intent. Governing documents, transfer chronology and exact historical PSC events are required before deciding whether a contradiction exists.

**Pilot and stop rule.** Start with 20 seeded, ordinary private limited companies having simple ordinary-share capital. Save document URLs/hashes and field-level page citations. Require complete baseline-plus-change histories, matching periods, arithmetic agreement and two human reviewers for every flag. Go only if at least 90% of the bounded sample has both complete histories and at least 98% of material extraction fields match manual transcription. Stop or narrow the method if historical events or document rights are unavailable, or if extraction+review costs exceed manual review. A suggested cash cap of US$20 for a future 200-page benchmark is a budget ceiling, not a priced estimate; no calls were made. Human work provisionally 8–16 hours for acquisition, transcription comparison and legal/temporal checks, subject to measurement. No allegation or named-subject publication is authorized by the pilot.

## Separate ordinary-code machine screen

`machine-screen.py` screened all **15 nonempty parsed Public Eye PDFs**, producing **1,571 lexical term hits** in `machine-screen.jsonl`. Every cached PDF hash matched the acquisition manifest. The ledger includes canonical source URL, original report hash, parsed-text SHA-256, byte/character counts, multilingual category counts and every hit’s physical PDF page and character offsets; it publishes no copyrighted fulltext or passage excerpts. The categories cover bottlenecks, manual processing, data/document formats, registers, ownership, payments, finance, trade valuations, secrecy, methods and entity matching. This is deterministic nomination of passages for subsequent review, **not contextual reading or evidence of a problem**. Contextual/deep counts stay unchanged. Reproduction requires permitted acquisition/parser caches in `/tmp/ngo-global-trade`; no paid services or model calls. Validation confirmed every recorded page-local offset maps to the same global text substring.
