# Registry-selected ownership interpretation controls

The public rights/history pilot is ready to test. The initial clean 11-page incorporation control did not exercise the companion task: interpreting scanned constitutional clauses and chronology. The expanded, registry-selected sample supplies that task without choosing a suspected person or requiring a discovered contradiction.

## What was actually checked

The official [basic-company snapshot](https://download.companieshouse.gov.uk/en_output.html) linked a 50 MB ZIP for 1 October 2026, part 7 of 7. It downloaded successfully, with 604,714 CSV rows parsed. Filtering active, numeric-eight-digit private limited companies incorporated in 2024 yielded 41,395 eligible records in this partition. A fixed SHA 256 seed selected three IDs before reading their names or documents. The partition is alphabetically restricted; this is reproducible transport/interpretation sampling, not a national prevalence sample.

All three filing-history pages and currentPSCpages were reachable. A refined exact-table-row parser downloaded each company's incorporation document and its 2025/2026 confirmation statements: **nine originalPDFs,92 physicalpages**, each with URL, bytes, hash, filing date and form type in `history-companion.json`. The first probe also retrieved a few latest accounting/appointment items and HTML alternatives; those are explicitly not the rights-test input. None of these company documents inflates the NGO corpus count.

Three NEWINC documents total 74 pages. Page-level inspection found **44 pages with fewer than 30 non-whitespace native-extracted characters**. Seven evenly spaced sample pages were processed with free local English Tesseract. This recovered image text; it was not an AI-model comparison. One image was visually reviewed against the OCR: company 15895159's physicalp 20, articles 26–28. The page distinguishes a transfer remaining with the existing holder until membership registration, and inherited/transmitted rights whose meeting/voting exercise is conditional on becoming the holder. A bare “same rights” phrase is therefore insufficient to classify current voting rights. This is an actual interpretive companion task, not evidence that the sampled holder lacks a right.

The electronic prescribed-particulars pages describe ordinary-share rights in differing language. Two “no updates” CS 01 s per company contain no new shareholder table. They are continuity/abstention controls, not empty-member lists. Complete capital, articles/amendment, transfer and PSC history still needs verification before a documentary-inconsistency conclusion.

## Nine-gate supplement to RIGHTS-HISTORY-01 / GT-PSC

1. **NGO origin:** Global Witness's shareholder cross-check passage and TJN/Open Ownership's rights/range/history limitations are retained in the original and revisit source ledgers. Existing method, newly tested companion task. No extra NGO reading count is claimed for rereading those passages here.
2. **Proposition:** contemporaneous declared shares plus operative publicly documented rights should support the applicable declared control category. Compare only explicit conditions/holders/periods; abstain if membership, amendments or nominee evidence is absent.
3. **Inputs and joins:** exact company number, document/form ID, share class/denominator, membership/event/statement/filing dates. Same company is the original declarant in both views, not independent verification of true beneficial ownership.
4. **Bounded test:** three registry-nominated controls; successful originals on both filing/control views, nine relevant PDFs 92 pages and seven localOCRpage samples. No contradiction or full-history legal adjudication.
5. **AI versus baseline:** baseline is native extraction plus appropriate localOCR, numeric/date parsing and human reading. Proposed model labels present versus contingent rights, affected holder roles, overrides and effective-date relationships, citing original page/clause. It must not turn an inheritance exception into a current-owner restriction. Code calculates percentages/chronology; humans judge legal applicability and completeness.
6. **Innocent alternatives:** membership registration, transmission, capital changes, class rights, redundant PSC conditions, corrections, OCR errors or undisclosed private arrangements. Models cannot create missing private agreements or determine informal control.
7. **Why now:** inexpensive semantic processing makes a clause-and-history interpretation pilot plausible. No claim of pre 2026 impossibility or already measured savings. The empirical new observation is mixed text/image filings that native extraction silently leaves incomplete.
8. **Prior versus new:** NGO threshold/graph checks already exist. New-case discovery would come from a preregistered cohort after the method passes, not these ordinary setup controls or a famous case. Scoop novelty is a later gate.
9. **Pilot:** five companies from a fixed registry partition/date cohort; maximum 100 pages initially; include ordinary, conditional-rights, “no update” and correction controls. Review all relevant histories. Require every material field to be page-supported,95%fieldprecision and no false contradiction on conditional-rights controls; continue only if review minutes improve 20%against OCR+human baseline. Stop if histories/rights are missing or code/human is equally efficient. ## Reproduce and limits

`check_cohort.py` enumerates a fresh official snapshot, selects controls and checks public pages. `check_history_companion.py` uses exact cached filing table rows for NEWINC/CS 01 links, dates and original PDFs. `check_page_quality.py` examines native extraction per page and OCRs only a seven-page quality sample. FullCSV/PDF/PNG/OCR remain under `/tmp/ngo-revisit-psc`; metadata and hashes alone are committed. TLS stays enabled and there are no credentials, externalAIcalls, outreach or paid services. A new live snapshot may change the cohort; freeze and record its original hash for a particular run.

These are public-document interpretation controls. No person or company is nominated for wrongdoing. The sampled articles' clauses are interpreted only as disclosed text; their occurrence does not establish the triggering facts or legal effect in a case.
