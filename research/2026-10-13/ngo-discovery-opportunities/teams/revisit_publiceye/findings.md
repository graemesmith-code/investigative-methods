# Public Eye revisit: read scanned disclosure tables against the same report's narrative

**PE-DISCLOSURE is pilot_ready as a document-interpretation benchmark.** It is **conditional_blocked as a claim that a company failed certification requirements**: dated certificate history and exact applicable obligations were not freshly obtained. No contradiction, supplier abuse or illicit financial flow is alleged. Cash spend remains **US$0**.

The useful journalism test is whether a company's own report tells readers a consistent, dated story about its suppliers: do countries, regions and production stages in its narrative agree with the table it labels as its current sourcing list? The second question is whether measures described as completed, planned or effective remain clearly distinguished when reports are converted into a searchable dataset. These are claims in public documents; hidden transactions are unnecessary for this limited test.

## Nine selection gates

1. **Original bottleneck, date and location.** Public Eye/FEMNET's January 2021 German report [Does the Grüner Knopf deliver on its promise?](https://www.publiceye.ch/en/publications/detail/does-the-gruener-knopf-deliver-on-its-promise), original PDF p2: “Doch nur bei einer Minderheit konnten präzise und aussagekräftige Informationen zu identifizierten und priorisierten Risiken sowie angemessenen Maßnahmen festgestellt werden.” The detailed methodology is p8; the analysis grid is p12; p11 explains information availability and transition-period limits; pp32–37 explain interpretation and sources. This prior work itself collected websites, government-provided documents and solicited company information. The proposed pilot uses only online public material.

2. **Consequential, falsifiable proposition.** A table headed as the company's current procurement countries, broken down by production stage, should be compatible with the same report's explicit narrative country/stage/year claims. If both statements clearly refer to the same cutoff and stage and yield different sets, nominate a documentary discrepancy for reconciliation; if they agree, retain it as a negative control. If scope or cutoff differs, record non-comparability. Separately classify an action as completed, planned, conditional or unmeasured effect: a stated future plan is not evidence that a measure occurred or achieved an outcome. The result concerns what readers can verify from the disclosure, not actual factory conditions.

3. **Named public data, joins and provenance.** The [official Green Button company directory](https://gruener-knopf.de/gk-unternehmen) provides the population and links to companies and certificate entries. A raw download yielded **50 distinct company certificate-entry links**, excluding login/logout; its first listed company is Amidex. It was selected solely as the first entry in the saved directory, not for suspicion. The two original company reports are [English sustainability-report-2025.pdf](https://www.amidex.at/downloads/sustainability-report-2025.pdf) and [German nachhaltigkeitsbericht-2026-01.pdf](https://amidex.at/downloads/nachhaltigkeitsbericht-2026-01.pdf). The German report is headed 2025 and signed **6 December 2025** on PDF p13; its 2026 filename is not a reporting cutoff. The English report discusses 2024 outcomes but its publication/signature date is unverified. Do not treat them as same-period translations. Within each PDF the stable join is document hash + company identity + reporting period + production stage, with page references. A common company name alone does not prove group scope or legal entity equivalence.

4. **Successful bounded checks and clean control.** Both original PDFs downloaded successfully with verified TLS. They have 8 and 14 physical pages. PyMuPDF found **zero non-whitespace text on all 22 pages**; pdftotext output consisted only of form-feeds. Free local Tesseract, English language pack, PSM6, two workers with one OpenMP thread each generated **30,368 characters** on all 22 pages, using 17.76 summed worker-seconds (not end-to-end wall time). The initial English-only flat OCR recovered the German narrative's new China supplier claim, but substantially lost the sourcing table's cells. A stronger bounded comparator then used the official free German OCR pack plus pixel-based grid segmentation: it recovered all five country cells and 14 of 15 normalized country/region/stage cells in 1.757 seconds for the cell OCR pass. Its table region was nominated manually; this is not a generic layout-detection benchmark. Visual review of German p6 found five rows: Slovakia/Zemplin/manufacturing; Pakistan/Sindh/full yarn-and-fabric production; Pakistan/Punjab/same; China/Hebei/same; Turkey/Denizli and Karamanmaras/same. These four unique supplier countries agree with German pp1–2, including China introduced in 2025. This is a **control agreement**, not an inconsistency. Austria occurs as the office location and must not be counted as a supplier merely because a country dictionary finds it. The older English narrative lacks China and refers to older outcomes: that is not a translation contradiction. Neither table statements nor narratives prove transactions or environmental outcomes.

5. **Precise AI companion and code baseline.** Ordinary code downloads, hashes, renders pages, runs local OCR, nominates country words and compares normalized sets. The candidate AI step is layout-aware table reconstruction plus multilingual semantic labels for supplier versus office countries, production stages, reporting periods, and promise versus completed action. It must return page/region evidence, confidence and abstention. This addresses interpretation that a flat country regex cannot perform reliably; it does not recover secrets. `pilot_baseline.py` is the initial flat OCR baseline; `table_baseline.py` is the stronger German OCR-plus-grid comparator. `control-records.json` contains independently transcribed page evidence. No paid API calls or externally benchmarked AI model were run. The stronger local OCR comparator performs much of the transcription job; AI must earn a place by identifying semantic roles, stages, periods and action/outcome distinctions, or reducing generic-layout setup/review time at equal accuracy. It must be compared against multilingual OCR and ordinary table segmentation, not just English flat OCR.

6. **Strongest innocent explanation and missing evidence.** Table/narrative differences may reflect stage exclusions, office versus sourcing geography, report updates, supplier exits, different group coverage or OCR errors. Public reports can also omit confidential factory identities while accurately disclose countries. Stage names have to be bridged before declaring disagreement. Exact regulatory/certification failure would require normative version, licensing date, evaluation stage, transition timetable and full declared reporting corpus; the portal returned a JavaScript shell on fresh access, so those requirements remain blocked here. No regulator outreach is proposed.

7. **Why now without a novelty slogan.** Public Eye already used a manual disclosure grid in 2021; that is a precedent, not a disqualification. A current first-entry company demonstrates accessible scans that require OCR and interpretation; the possible benefit is affordable multilingual and layout-aware triage across a wider population. The actual timing result shows that careful configuration of existing free OCR can already be fast. Cheap AI's incremental accuracy/review-time benefit remains a measurable hypothesis. The August 2026 Green Button guide clarifies reporting issues, but its commercial/outside-organization reuse restrictions mean it is **not** supplied to third-party AI or redistributed. An independently written documentary-consistency rubric avoids copying the guide. A certification-compliance implementation remains conditional on permitted normative use and applicable dates.

8. **Prior coverage and new case versus reused method.** The NGO's 2021 sample consisted of 31 companies listed in May 2020; its table p8 does not include Amidex. The current institution's company entry dates Amidex's license to February 2025 in a search-rendered record, but fresh portal details were not obtained. This is a newly accessed company-report pair and a tested control for an established method, not an untouched research field or a new accusation. A broader pilot should sample from the saved current directory and seek agreements as actively as discrepancies. No exhaustive prior-coverage search was completed; novelty beyond a specific sample must be rechecked before publication.

9. **Reproducible pilot, stop/go and costs.** Sample ten directory entries with a fixed seed and order; obtain one dated public report per company, then evaluate 50 stratified narrative/table fields with two reviewers. Run native extraction, multilingual local OCR plus ordinary-code table/region segmentation and role/tense rules, and a permitted local multimodal model with the same field schema. Annotate office/supplier role, stage, country/region, cutoff, action tense and whether any outcome metric is actually reported. Use precision/recall against the double-reviewed control, table row completeness, abstention accuracy, total elapsed time and human correction minutes. Proceed only with 98% precision on material extracted fields, at least 95% table-row completeness and a measured 25% reduction in review minutes versus local OCR; disagreement between reviewers or mixed periods is an abstention. Stop if document use is restricted, sampling cannot be reproduced, or local model setup exceeds a precommitted cash cap. Estimated future cash **US$0–50** if suitable local compute already exists; this is a cap/scenario, not a verified market price. Hardware/GPU access is unknown; human review provisionally 6–12 hours and setup 2–6 hours, to be measured separately. If local compute is unavailable, do the human/OCR benchmark first; no paid service is authorized.

## Artifacts and limitations

`access-checks.json` records successful original downloads and denied raw GFI PDF downloads; no denial was bypassed or retried. `population-links.json` preserves discovery links; `population.json` contains only the50 distinct company entries and deterministic first-entry choice. `inventory.jsonl` separates repeated NGO records, new institutional context and new company reports. `ocr-baseline.jsonl` records22 page-level runs without copyrighted text. `control-records.json` gives normalized factual transcription, not source reproductions. `pilot_baseline.py` and `table_baseline.py` have no model/network paid calls and never inputs the restricted Green Button guide. Full reports, images and OCR stay under `/tmp`. Raw PDFs cannot be reproduced from Git alone: re-acquisition must respect source rights/access and match recorded hashes. Publication remains research/design only.
