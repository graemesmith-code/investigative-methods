# OCCRP targeted revisit: public document interpretation, 13 October 2026

Three additional eligible investigations received contextual and deeper reading, selected for method diversity: charity/related-party disclosures, transnational infrastructure ownership, and French corporate property. Two are existing catalog records whose reading flags should be upgraded, and one is a newly enumerated article. A fourth attempted article failed and is not counted as read. A dated OCCRP/GACC case study was read only as supporting provenance, separate from the three primary reads. There is no local OCCRP fulltext cache: a low-keyword-hit sample cannot honestly be nominated. This is purposive methodology sampling rather than keyword triage or exhaustive reading.

None becomes a finalist here. The revisit establishes promising AI marginal steps without claiming the underlying methods are new. The weakest gate remains fresh access to both original-record datasets, rather than model expense.

## RO-CHARITY: public charity-adjacency discovery

1. **Original bottleneck and locator.** [Ferrexpo charity investigation](https://www.occrp.org/en/investigation/instead-of-helping-ukraines-poor-ferrexpos-charitable-donations-flowed-to-companies-associated-with-its-then-ceo), 16 September 2026, opening methods paragraph following the Crowe report's introduction: “Drawing on bank account data and internal charity documents”. Read surrounding methods, control assertions, reporting disagreements and responses. This makes the public/private boundary explicit.
2. **Consequential test.** In a data-nominated universe of listed companies reporting charity spending, identify charities whose publicly named founders/directors also have documented historical roles at donor-group entities. The proposition is an undisclosed organizational association deserving review. It does not establish control, misuse or a payment chain. An if/then test could ask: if a same-period authoritative record names an officer at both organizations, does the donor's relevant-period disclosure explain that association? The accounting requirement must be separately evaluated.
3. **Datasets and equivalence.** Company annual reports/related-party notes and official charity/corporate incorporation histories could establish roles and claims. Use company/charity registration IDs, officer dates, legal-entity names and explicit role types. Names alone, a current board record or a common professional address are insufficient. Ukraine's underlying registry/court records and private charity payment data were not acquired here. A UK public benchmark would use Companies House plus the appropriate charity regulator and annual reports, but is not substituted as evidence for Ukraine.
4. **Bounded access check.** Ordinary download of the original Ferrexpo 2018 annual-report URL returned 403. Companies House public search returned a browser-access error. Both recorded; no retry, credentials, paid source or alternative route. Search snippets identifying the annual report are not counted as reading or successful dataset acquisition.
5. **AI/code/human split.** AI might identify the charity's precise legal name and interpret nuanced denial/control/auditor-qualified statements across long multilingual reports. Code should resolve IDs, compare officer periods and build a provenance graph. A human must establish accounting materiality and distinguish associated personnel from control. Routing specific funds requires bank/internal evidence, which the model cannot supply.
6. **Innocent explanation/missing evidence.** Shared professionals may provide ordinary services; a company-founded charity can remain independently governed. Public records do not show instruction, influence or who ultimately received money. The missing evidence includes governance agreements and performed transactions.
7. **Why now.** No 2026-specific capability or pricing advantage established by this article. The plausible change is cheaper semantic triage, subject to a blinded benchmark against ordinary search; old methods remain valid. The source's actual breakthrough was newly obtained forensic/banking material, not cheap AI.
8. **Prior coverage/newness.** OCCRP already investigated the named donor/charity. A reusable donor-universe screen, with corrected entity/time joins and negative controls, would be new work; reproducing that famous case would only benchmark extraction.
9. **Pilot and stop/go.** Proposed, not performed: ten donor annual reports chosen by a deterministic charity-spend criterion, historical records for the named recipients, and human-labeled role/control passages. Stop if fewer than eight recipient IDs can be resolved, source documents are unavailable, or the model confuses association and control in any consequential output. Go only on measurable analyst time saved with no unsupported control claims. Cash estimate: US$0 open-source baseline, optional model use limited to a future US$20 cap; unpriced human review approximately 8–16 hours. No paid calls authorized or made. This is a feasibility design, not a priced vendor quote.

## RO-HOLDINGS: mine public annual reports for ownership edges hidden behind offshore shells

1. **Original bottleneck and locator.** [European holdings investigation](https://www.occrp.org/en/investigation/a-police-hq-wind-farms-and-natural-gas-mapping-the-european-holdings-of-chinas-central-bank), 24 July 2026, dated official catalog metadata; body paragraph immediately after discussion of BVI registry response: “requiring reporters to painstakingly piece together the limited information available in public records.” Methods and limitations read in full.
2. **Consequential test.** Starting with an infrastructure-operator universe, find dated report statements identifying shareholders, nominee relationships or ultimate parent entities behind registered holding companies. The test concerns transparent attribution of equity/nominee chains, not malign intent: does a report-explicit ultimate-parent edge explain an operator's public corporate chain?
3. **Datasets/joins.** Operator annual reports/company accounts and corporate filing histories are the intended pair. Stable company numbers, statement dates, share classes, subsidiary scope and effective ownership dates are required. Reported equity interest is not voting control. The underlying Luxembourg/UK reports used by OCCRP were not downloaded/tested here. BVI beneficial ownership and contemporary European restrictions remain access gaps.
4. **Bounded check.** No successfully acquired pair of original operator/holding filings; the Companies House error above is relevant but not catalog-as-proof. The OCCRP article itself is readable. An article's statement that reporters accessed records is not a fresh dataset check.
5. **AI/code/human split.** AI could convert narrative notes and irregular tables into typed edges: legal ownership, nominee-for, parent-of, equity percentage, voting control, with quoted provenance. Code traverses dated graphs and computes equity only where definitions permit. Humans review every consequential edge and resolve ambiguous reporting bases.
6. **Innocent explanation/missing evidence.** Offshore holding structures can be routine investment/tax arrangements; shared directors/addresses often mark service providers. Missing parent edges cannot be inferred from co-location. Lawful state investment is not corruption.
7. **Why now.** The paper shows existing annual reports can bridge registry opacity. Cheap AI is a proposed extraction-cost reduction, not new access; no comparative benchmark was run. The 2022 ownership-access retreat can make some tasks harder today.
8. **Prior/new.** OCCRP's ownership map already exists. New work would systematically test publicly documented edges across a data-defined operator universe and disclose which chains remain unresolved, avoiding nationality-based suspect selection.
9. **Pilot/stop/go.** Proposed twenty annual reports from a published operator list, with ten pre-labeled positive/negative ownership statements. Stop on unresolved IDs, missing historical counterpart filings, or confusion of nominees, shareholders and controllers. Go only after every emitted consequential edge matches an original statement and time savings beat PDF text search. Future cash cap US$20 optional inference, human review 10–20 hours estimated. No actual spend. Parked pending original-record access tests.

## RO-PROPERTY: company-to-parcel joins plus document interpretation

1. **Original method/bottleneck.** [French property investigation](https://www.occrp.org/en/investigation/the-luxury-french-real-estate-of-alleged-latin-american-money-launderers-and-officials-accused-of-corruption), published 5 July 2023 by inference described below; “Data Analysis” section: “reporters sent requests to France’s property registry services — known by its French acronym, SPF — to obtain deeds of sale”. Full body/context read, including bank-record dependencies and responses.
2. **Proposition/test.** A legal entity listed as holding a parcel may disclose human partners or an upstream parent in public corporate filings. Test historical company IDs and dated equity/partnership records against a parcel-holder snapshot; do not label named people landowners merely from a company link.
3. **Data/joins/equivalence.** Intended datasets: official French corporate filings (SIREN) and legal-person land ownership/parcel records. Parcel ID, SIREN, record date and legal right must match. Company's share ownership and personal property ownership differ; deeds are needed for transaction details. Current beneficial-ownership access, original deed availability and snapshot freshness were not tested successfully here.
4. **Access.** Readable OCCRP methods identify a historical scraped corpus, followed by deed requests. No currently acquired two-sided dataset or free deed sample here. No outreach or registry-request substitute attempted.
5. **AI role.** AI could parse statutory partnership clauses or deeds already lawfully available; numeric SIREN/parcel joins belong in code. Interpretation of legal rights, validity periods and family identities requires review. AI cannot create inaccessible deeds or bank records.
6. **Innocent/missing.** Shared names, family companies, nominee/professional appointments and past ownership create false matches. Lack of a register entry may reflect an obligation exemption or access/snapshot gap. Money-source claims require separate evidence.
7. **Why now.** These joins and search-engine construction existed in 2023. Cheaper document interpretation is possible in principle but unbenchmarked; access has not been shown to improve.
8. **Prior/new.** OCCRP and TI/ACDC/BIRD already performed the French matching. Newness would require a current, data-nominated, historically equivalent disclosure test—not republishing the famous-person list. The article's initial selection of public-interest people is unsuitable as this project's discovery design.
9. **Pilot/stop/go.** Parked. A later twenty-company sample must first obtain an authorized current parcel snapshot and counterpart historical filings. Stop before model spend if either side or deed evidence needed for the proposition is unavailable. No reliable affordability estimate until access is known; US$0 spent.

**Date provenance.** The article body does not display its original dateline in the browser representation. OCCRP's [GACC case study](https://gacc.occrp.org/post/who-really-owns-french-property), dated 15 October 2023 and read in full, says the OCCRP article and TI's Behind a Wall report were published on the same day inJuly 2023. TI's official companion publication is dated 5 July 2023. The exact original day is therefore an explicit inference from two official sources, not a visible original dateline. This limitation is retained in the inventory.

The denied UK-property article is not counted. No original public-record pair cleared access checks, so these remain parked hypotheses rather than findings or allegations. Actual cash spendUS$0. No paid API, outreach, email, live investigation or source-access bypass. The report and manifest contain short excerpts and metadata only; no bulk copyrighted texts.
