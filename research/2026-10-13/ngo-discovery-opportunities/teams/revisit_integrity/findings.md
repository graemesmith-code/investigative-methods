# Revisit: a ready procurement interpretation pilot and a conditional environmental-document test

**A 20-tender pilot can now test whether cheap AI helps a reporter connect a tender's requirements to the bidder's evidence and the procuring authority's explanation.** The public originals are accessible, IDs tie the documents together, and a small control bundle exposes exactly the work a semantic companion could help with. This is ready to test as a method. It has produced no contradiction, no eligibility determination and no measured AI savings.

The best PPLAAF-derived design is narrower than detecting corruption: **find environmental assessments whose site-specific statements appear to describe another place, while separating those statements from legitimate comparisons and citations.** One public assessment and a free gazetteer downloaded successfully, but the original Banana Port assessment and a defensible geometry comparison remain unavailable or unresolved. Keep this conditional.

## Readiness levels

- **Pilot-ready:** enough lawful original records are accessible to run the proposed small interpretation benchmark. Neither a misconduct finding nor previously measured AI benefit is required.
- **Conditional:** a named critical access, meaning or measurement blocker prevents the consequential test, even if a preparatory extraction benchmark is possible.
- **Rejected:** the proposed factual claim depends on unavailable private evidence, unsafe identity inference or non-equivalent measurements. An old method is not rejected merely because it is old.

## RI-01 — procurement requirements versus evidence and decision: PILOT-READY

**1. NGO bottleneck, exact passage and relationship.** AntAC's [25 May 2026 analysis](https://antac.org.ua/en/news/cabinet-of-ministers-allows-construction-material-prices-to-be-concealed-in-procurement-for-the-reconstruction-of-war-affected-regions/), HTML section “Risk of artificially restricted competition”, warns: “Contracting authorities will be able to write requirements into the terms of the framework agreements tailored to specific contractors”. This is a warning about rules and competition, not a claim that AI could solve an impossible task. The proposed narrower check asks whether published decisions accurately apply the published rules to the published evidence; it does not equate geographic restrictions with illegality.

**2. Consequential proposition and falsifiable test.** A contracting authority records why an offer qualifies or fails. A reporter can ask whether that description is supported by the bid documentation under the operative requirement. If a decision says a specified document is missing but the correct bidder/lot's valid evidence was uploaded at the required stage and actually satisfies that clause, flag a documentary inconsistency for review. If documents show no inconsistency, record a negative control. An accepted offer is not defective just because a keyword search fails. A model may correctly answer “conditional requirement; legal applicability unresolved” and pass the interpretation benchmark without nominating a story.

**3. Two original public sources, claims and joins.** [Prozorro's tender API](https://public.api.openprocurement.org/api/2.5/tenders/bc2b85df808042fa8157d6ddbf79d0c6) exposes the authority's tender rules and original award protocol, and the bidder's original evidence through distinct document arrays. The document authors are the original claimants; hosting in one official system does not independently prove the truth of credentials. Join internal tender ID `bc2b85df808042fa8157d6ddbf79d0c6`, public tender `UA-2026-09-10-011727-a`, lot `a2165570d9f85330abd58e02fc8f967d`, bid `5ace784b03c2470bb50fa8d9d1fde212`, award `c8150b2e897b414b9b00dfa168e69851`, and bidder identifier `UA-EDR:33412958`. Never join on worker/company names alone. The criterion version, document version and procurement stage form the unit of comparison; national price statistics are irrelevant to this test.

**4. Actual bounded check and original context.** The earlier 100-recent-tender check found five construction tenders. This revisit acquired the first construction tender's entire currently exposed native document bundle: 63 listed records, 33 non-signature documentary files downloaded successfully, and 30 signature-container records preserved as metadata. No non-public file was accessed. An employment-evidence ZIP contains 32 PDF members, with hashes preserved but staff names, contact details, bank data and filenames omitted from committed output. A current API snapshot is not an immutable historical archive.

Appendix 1, staffing criterion 1.1, requests a qualified workforce, including an estimating/design engineer, plus evidence of employment relationships. Criterion 1.2 requests a professional certificate **if its acquisition for the work is required by applicable law**; subcontractor capacities are allowed. A separate clause says an absent subcontractor-intention statement is not a nonconformity when no such intention exists. The main tender documentation explicitly recognizes a 24-hour correction procedure and harmless formal errors. The complete award protocol dated 29 September says the bidder supplied the required documents and that the proposal complied. The employee-list evidence names appropriate roles, but the archive PDFs need OCR/visual review before any credential conclusion: all32 archive members yielded nonzero `pdftotext` characters yet zero Cyrillic letters. “Downloaded and parsed” therefore cannot mean “qualification proved” or “certificate absent”.

The deadline was 28 September 2026 at 09:00; the bid was submitted 24 September. Some documents appear on 29 September **after the award**, potentially at the winner-evidence stage. The rule is not to label them late bids; classify the required stage and correction window first. The current API has no top-level revisions or complaints, which is not proof that every historical event is visible. Control result: access verified, original criterion and decision understood, eligibility unadjudicated, no contradiction found.

**5. AI companion, ordinary code and human work.** Code acquires arrays and versions, joins IDs, validates chronology, unzips files and runs PDF extraction/OCR. Free OCR and keyword/table matching are the baseline. AI's proposed role is semantic: identify mandatory versus conditional versus optional clauses, classify bidder/winner/correction stages, connect Ukrainian role descriptions and evidence types, and output cited requirement–evidence–decision triples. It should not process unnecessary staff identities; replace names with local opaque aliases if a model is used. A reporter checks cited pages; a procurement specialist determines legal applicability, permissible equivalent evidence and correction validity. AI cannot license an engineer or infer corrupt intent.

**6. Innocent explanations and missing evidence.** Conditional certification may be unnecessary for this work; lawful subcontracting or an equivalence provision may satisfy the clause; evidence may exist in an archive, correction or other approved source; winner-stage documents have different deadlines. Missing evidence: usable OCR/visual reading, complete corrections/version history, the applicable legal qualification rule, and authoritative credential validity. These are reporting gates, not blockers to a bounded extraction benchmark. No regulator contact is proposed.

**7. Why now and falsifiable cost claim.** Prozorro records and AI procurement monitoring are old. The current hypothesis is that cheap general-purpose semantic processing can reduce reporter effort for unstructured attachments and conditional rules, without maintaining a custom classifier. That is unmeasured. Compare specialist minutes per correctly cited triple for ordinary extraction/OCR versus the same baseline plus AI; retain the companion only if it reduces median review time by at least 20% while maintaining at least 90% extraction precision and no unsupported legal or identity conclusion. This is an affordability test, not “intractable until 2026”.

**8. Prior coverage and new case versus old method.** [DOZORRO described AI procurement-risk screening in 2018](https://dozorro.org/blog/yak-shtuchnij-intelekt-dozorro-monitorit-zakupivli), including unjustified selection/disqualification, and [proBI documents existing tender-file/proposal-file analysis](https://dozorro.org/tools/pro-bi). The 2018 source is extended search-index text; direct access returned403. No route around that denial was used. These precedents remain valuable comparison tools. The pilot adds a transparent cited-triple workflow and measured reporter time to an existing method family; it does not establish a new method, a novel story or a capability gap in DOZORRO. The gated proBI application itself was not accessed. Novel **cases** would have to emerge from systematic tender selection after methods validation.

**9. Modest reproducible pilot, cost and stop/go.** Define a fixed month of completed civilian construction tenders, rank canonical tender IDs by `sha256(seed+tender_id)` using a published seed, and take the first 20 IDs before access testing. Do not replace failed draws. Attempt rules, original bidder evidence and award/rejection retrieval for each; retain access failures in the denominator. Do not select famous officials or named contractors. Record all excluded IDs and access reasons. The accessed September tender is a setup control, not representative of that frame. Have a specialist annotate two relevant clauses per tender, including conditional clauses and both supported and unresolved conclusions. Randomize baseline/AI-assisted order to reduce learning effects. Stop if fewer than 16 of 20 have the necessary original bundle, OCR is unusable in over20%, or evidence chronology cannot be reconstructed. Proceed only on the accuracy/time thresholds above; a positive finding is not required. Future model cash estimate: up to one million input and50,000 output tokens at assumed ceiling rates US$2/million andUS$8/million isUS$2.40; reserveUS$20 for retries, stop beforeUS$50. These are ceilings/assumptions, not current-provider pricing. Local acquisition/OCR uses existing free tools; human review6–12hours estimated, specialist rate unknown. Large archives, scans, model context size, corrections and legal review drive costs. Actual spendUS$0; no model calls made.

## RI-02 — site-specific geography assertions in environmental assessments: CONDITIONAL

**1. Exact NGO passage and date.** PPLAAF's [Mangrove Marine Park investigation,11November2025](https://www.pplaaf.org/cases/drc-mangrove-marine-park-whistleblower.html), HTML section “DP World and the Banana Port Papers”, describes the earlier assessment: “references to other countries such as Togo, as well as factual errors and the use of maps of unrelated sites”. This is the original NGO's reported criticism of an assessment, not independently confirmed by this review. The surrounding passage says the early ESIA was based largely on bibliographic sources and omitted required site description, mangrove-impact analysis and consultation. The company response says supplemental studies were commissioned and the dredging footprint modified. The earlier assessment and current geometry could therefore differ substantially.

**2. Proposition and test.** An assessment's site-specific baseline, project description and map should refer to its own named project and declared assessment phase. If a paragraph presented as a local observation puts the project in an incompatible country/province or describes a map belonging to another site, retain a question about assessment reliability. Exclude citations, regional comparators, inherited jurisdiction names and historical background. A reused legal-framework paragraph or generic boilerplate is not evidence of a false field survey. Spatial overlap or a crime allegation is a different test with additional gates.

**3. Free original datasets and joins.** Public operator/lender disclosure documents are one original claimant stream; [GeoNames' free country gazetteer](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/PH.zip), with its [CC BY4.0 schema and source notes](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/readme.txt), is an independent reference for place identity, coordinates and administrative hierarchy, not authoritative proof of property/permit boundaries. For the setup control, use the [Bauan International Port ESIA v3](https://cdnweb.ictsi.com/Final%20Draft%20-%20BIPI%20ESIA%20v3.pdf), dated11May2026. Join project title/operator, version hash, declared locality and geographic context to GeoNames IDs plus country/admin code and feature type. A place-name string is never sufficient. The corpus selection should enumerate a disclosed-project list before reading assessments; Bauan was found while checking public port assessments and is only an access control, not a data-nominated suspected subject.

**4. Bounded access check and control limits.** The397-page,24,184,524-byte Bauan PDF downloaded with TLS verification intact; its hash is in `environment-access.json`. The2,555,380-byte Philippine gazetteer downloaded and contains96,645 records. A bounded exact-name check returned multiple Bauans and Batangas entries. Geographic hierarchy and feature class distinguish the Batangas municipality from northern Philippine namesakes; Batangas Bay has GeoNames ID1726278. The report's executive scope (printedp1/PDFp12) and project-location text (printedp37/PDFp48) identify Bauan/Batangas, not those northern namesakes. No wrong-site claim was established. This demonstrates accessible originals and a real disambiguation task.

The report says phase1 is covered by an existing ECC and excludes phase2; those approvals were not independently retrieved. Its boundary table is labelled WGS84 but uses northing/easting values without a resolved projection/zone. **Do not convert those numbers using a guessed EPSG code or infer a protected-area intersection.** The file bears an “OFFICIAL USE ONLY” watermark despite being publicly reachable and describing disclosure; no external AI transmission occurred. Confirm the applicable reuse/processing terms through already public policy/document notices before using a third-party model, or keep preparation local. No outreach is authorized. An official Ramsar788 information-sheet PDF also downloaded as supporting context, but its historic boundary/version is not a current park-geometry proof. Direct BII and AIIB project pages returned403; no alternative route around either denial was pursued. The original early Banana ESIA remains unacquired.

**5. AI versus code/human.** Code extracts place names, tables, coordinates, versions and administrative candidates. AI labels the **function** of each geographic statement: project location, site-specific observation, regional comparison, citation, historical context or generic legal text; it identifies which assessment phase the statement concerns. That is the semantic task a plain country-name regex cannot reliably perform. Code then checks only the location/site-observation categories against a resolved place hierarchy. Humans adjudicate citations, mapped coordinate systems, aliases and the significance of errors. AI does not create hidden permits, reconstruct historic boundaries or verify a survey occurred.

**6. Innocent explanation and missing evidence.** A comparison with Togo may be legitimate, a map may show regional context, a mistranslated place may have an alias, an older edition may be superseded, and phases may have different footprints. A wrong label may be a harmless editorial error with sound underlying fieldwork. Missing: original Banana ESIA, full current/superseded versions, coordinate reference system and independently published approval/footprint records for the consequential spatial test. Reuse terms must be clarified without contacting third parties before external AI processing.

**7. Why now and measured hypothesis.** Assessments have always been readable and geographic consistency checks are established work. Cheap multilingual models could classify thousands of references by their semantic role, reducing false flags and human sorting effort. Test that against regex plus human review. A practical improvement, rather than historical novelty, would justify reuse. No AI-quality or cost-saving result exists yet.

**8. Prior coverage and possible new cases.** PPLAAF already published the Banana criticism and VisioTerra already monitored the park with satellite images. Repeating those findings is not new reporting. The new-case opportunity would be a systematic, public-document sample in which a different project's consequential site-specific assertion survives original-source and expert review. Plagiarism/text-similarity screening itself is long-established. Prior search did not identify a verified public corpus-level role-labelled geographic consistency benchmark; that absence is not proof of novelty.

**9. Conditional pilot and costs.** Once rights and stable public corpus access are clear, take ten civilian coastal-project ESIAs from one official publication list, with publicly stated country/locality and an independent reference stream. Use a published sampling seed. Annotate100 geographic passages across project-location, baseline and comparator/citation categories; compare regex-only and AI-companion precision and reviewer minutes. No accusation follows a classifier label. Stop if place hierarchies cannot resolve at least80% or if phase/CRS ambiguity prevents the claimed comparison; continue semantic role classification only if at least90% precise and median review time falls20%. Estimated future input600,000/output40,000tokens at assumed ceilingUS$2/US$8 per million givesUS$1.52 before retries; reserveUS$20, hardstopUS$50. Human review6–10hours estimated, geography/ESIA specialist rate unknown. GeoNames requires attribution; its “as-is” accuracy limitations remain. Whole397-page documents should not be sent blindly to a model: narrow to cited relevant sections. Actual spendUS$0.

## Rejected extensions

| Extension | Level and reason |
|---|---|
| Infer corrupt tender from one bid or missing keyword | Rejected: neither proves misconduct; conditional certification, archives and correction stages matter. |

## Reproducibility, counts and provenance

This revisit adds **zero new NGO report URLs**. `inventory.jsonl` contains two canonical reports already present in the prior inventory, with previous and new reading flags. It adds one deep relevant-section read for the PPLAAF report; the AntAC report was already deeply read. Do not add the two rows to the corpus denominator. The public procurement and environmental control documents are test evidence, not NGO report counts. Prior discovery and reading ledgers are preserved.

`procurement_bundle.py` downloads only explicitly public non-signature documents and caches originals in `/tmp/ngo-revisit-integrity`; committed manifest contains metadata/hashes and omits staff identities. It does not evaluate signatures or authenticate credentials. `environment_check.py` reproduces bounded ESIA, gazetteer and supporting Ramsar access checks. Copyrighted fulltexts remain outside GitHub. Successful acquisition is separate from machine parsing, contextual reading, legal interpretation and factual proof. All TLS verification stays enabled, and no denied source is accessed by an alternative route. Neither pilot used a paid model, external processing, contact, email or repository write beyond the authorized local team directory.

### Suitable Ukrainian OCR baseline check

A bounded two-page check used Tesseract5.5.0 with the official `tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast` Ukrainian trained data, downloaded with verified TLS into `/tmp`, under its Apache2.0 licence.
