## Supporting evidence for payments reconciliation (LE-SUPPORT-PAYMENTS)

[OPEN DATA : L’exemple des industries extractives](https://www.asso-sherpa.org/open-data-lexemple-industries-extractives), 8 December 2016, HTML body final paragraph before the PGO summit discussion: “l’absence d’un registre centralisé, en format ouvert, de ces paiements.” This describes absence of a central open-format register of companies' government payments. The substantial HTML was read; its original linked report was not obtained and no PDF page is asserted.

[Transparence des industries extractives françaises : C’est encore flou !](https://www.asso-sherpa.org/transparence-industries-extractives-francaises-cest-flou), 13 April 2017, section “Les bonnes pratiques et les limites,” discusses difficult PDF formats alongside missing contextual definitions and currency conversion. Its appended original company response is essential counterevidence: Total disputes the comparison between rights acquired under production-sharing arrangements and government receipts. This is a reason to require period, accrual/cash, operator share, government entity and valuation equivalence before proposing wrongdoing. AI table extraction can reduce transcription expense; it cannot repair missing accounting bridges. Any finalist should use the NRGI team's separately tested datasets and preserve each original claimant's provenance.

## Coverage and reproducibility limits

Basel: 472 publication URLs discovered from its official sitemap, reduced to 457 canonical path candidates; 83 were outside the lower date bound, leaving 374 dated landing records. Exact PDF-hash deduplication, explicit language editions, and exclusion of newsletters/interviews/programme factsheets leave 321 eligible records. Six known multilingual manifestations were merged into five report identities, with version provenance retained. Other unidentified language/version duplicates remain a possible limitation. Newsletters are preserved only as rejected discovery metadata. This is the broadest census of the three organizations, but only six reports were substantively screened.

Sherpa: official WordPress public API categories `recherches-et-publications` (901) and `rapports` (2810), date-bounded, yielded 42 unique records before one identical-report duplicate was removed. Nineteen records expose no body text through this API: metadata only, explicitly marked. These legacy categories omit some newer site's Enquêtes et études, legal doctrine and substantial case pages, particularly 2024–2026. The census is therefore incomplete. The current public archive displays 23 studies and 28 focus-case items; those totals are not counted as read or added without record enumeration.

Spotlight: all eight official Reports & Briefings archive pages inspected; 63 visible entries contain repeated sanctions and asset-recovery entries, leaving 61 unique eligible sources, spanning 2019–2026 (organization founded 2019). Browser landing extracts acquired for all; only two PDF sections substantively screened. Submissions and court-calendar/case pages were not exhaustively enumerated. Ordinary robot/sitemap/report PDF requests returned 403; no access-denial bypass, TLS override or credential use. Browser-returned texts were assessed separately.

Run `census.py`, then `acquire.py`; all bulk originals stay `/tmp/legal-enforcement`. `finalize.py` and `repair_catalog.py` capture explicit manual adjudication and browser-derived catalog dates. They read the committed browser metadata, official archive dates and access-check results directly; reacquiring browser content requires the browser tool and the documented archive links. Every original URL and report hash is in `inventory.jsonl`; `counts.json` separates acquisition from reading. No copyrighted fulltext is in these deliverables.

## Additional automated fulltext index

`machine-screen.py` indexed all 305 nonempty canonical PDF text caches (294 Basel, 11 Sherpa), with zero missing/empty caches. This separate fulltext machine-screen count does not change the 12 contextual-screen or 10 deeper-read counts. `machine-screen.jsonl` records report/text hashes, character counts, keyword-family counts and the first three page/character positions per family, with no excerpts. `machine-screen-summary.json` documents English/French patterns and totals. Results are an acquisition triage index, not evidence that matching passages describe a genuine bottleneck. Merged language/version manifestations are excluded from the305count. Spotlight browser PDF text has no reproducible local cache and is excluded.
