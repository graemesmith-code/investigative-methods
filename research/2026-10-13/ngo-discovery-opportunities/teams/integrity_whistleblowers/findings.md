# Integrity and whistleblower source review — 13 October 2026

The strongest design from these three organizations is a document-level procurement consistency check: **does a published award or rejection accurately describe the evidence a bidder submitted under that tender's own qualification rules?** A bounded public-record check retrieved the tender rules, a bidder's employee evidence and the award decision. It found no contradiction. This is a conditional reproducible benchmark design, not an evidence-ready or overlooked finalist. Existing DOZORRO work makes its novelty doubtful; no improvement over ordinary text search has been demonstrated.

## IW-01: audit qualification decisions against the tender's own published record

**Status:** conditional benchmark only; retained_finalist=false; successful two-sided access check, no contradiction or new capability established.

**Original bottleneck and context.** AntAC's [25 May 2026 procurement analysis](https://antac.org.ua/en/news/cabinet-of-ministers-allows-construction-material-prices-to-be-concealed-in-procurement-for-the-reconstruction-of-war-affected-regions/), HTML heading “Risk of artificially restricted competition”, says: “Contracting authorities will be able to write requirements into the terms of the framework agreements tailored to specific contractors”. The passage identifies narrow local-experience criteria as one risk; it describes a policy concern, not an established violation in the sample below. It also discusses framework exceptions and the absence of construction material prices. The candidate translates that concern into a narrower observable documentary test rather than claiming to detect corruption.

**Consequential proposition and test.** Procurement decisions should describe bidder evidence accurately under the operative criteria. If a recorded rejection says an expressly required certificate was absent, but a valid pre-deadline document version contains that certificate for the same legally relevant role/person, retain a potential documentary contradiction. Conversely, if an acceptance says every requirement was met, but a clearly unconditional and applicable requirement has no supporting evidence after reviewing all published attachments, retain a question about completeness. An absent search hit alone never passes. A document showing a role or company-name match does not prove a legally valid qualification. Use the acceptance side initially as a known-positive control, not an allegation of improper admission.

**Both public sources and provenance.** [Prozorro original tender API](https://public.api.openprocurement.org/api/2.5/tenders/bc2b85df808042fa8157d6ddbf79d0c6) supplies contracting-authority qualification attachments and award decisions; that same record's `bids[].eligibilityDocuments` supplies the bidder's original evidence. These are two opposing original claimant streams in one official publication system, not independent corroboration of the factual truth of a certificate. Contracting-authority decision versus bidder submission is the comparison; underlying qualifications remain unverified. The bounded sample retrieved 100 most recently modified tender records, five CPV45 construction tenders, and two original DOCX attachments from the first construction tender. A follow-on fetched the bidder employee-list PDF and award decision PDF. All downloads succeeded. The signed employee-list variant produced identical extracted text and is not an independent document.

**Stable joins and period.** Internal tender ID `bc2b85df808042fa8157d6ddbf79d0c6`; public tender `UA-2026-09-10-011727-a`; bid `5ace784b03c2470bb50fa8d9d1fde212`; award `c8150b2e897b414b9b00dfa168e69851`; bidder `UA-EDR:33412958`; lot `a2165570d9f85330abd58e02fc8f967d`. Document IDs, datePublished/dateModified, original hash and downloaded SHA256 identify versions. Compare the criterion version operative at submission, the uploaded evidence by deadline, and corrections allowed before the particular decision. Use tender/lot/bid IDs, not name similarity, as joins. The sample employee list is dated 23 September 2026, submitted 24 September; award decision 29 September. Criteria and evidence share a single procurement and relevant role, avoiding cross-project price/product comparisons. This snapshot alone does not reconstruct every correction/version history.

**Observed control.** Appendix1's staffing criterion includes estimating/design engineering personnel, employment evidence and a professional certificate conditional on applicable law. The bidder employee list includes estimating/design engineers. The award decision states that all required documents/information were supplied and that the proposal met the tender requirements; the API award is active. **No mismatch, geographic restriction or wrongful award has been demonstrated.** The sample proves that originals on both sides can be downloaded, parsed and joined. The entirety of bidder documents, professional credentials and applicable conditional law were not assessed. Worker identities, personal contact information and bank details are unnecessary for the report and are not reproduced here.

**AI versus code versus human.** Ordinary code retrieves versions, unzips DOCX, runs PDF text extraction/OCR, joins IDs and enforces dates. AI proposes structured requirement/evidence pairs across Ukrainian wording, role variants, conditional clauses and narrative decisions; each output must cite a document ID plus original page/paragraph. Regex and table extraction are the baseline: a clean employee table needs no model. A Ukrainian procurement specialist decides whether a clause is applicable, whether evidence satisfies it, and whether a later correction cures the issue. AI cannot validate a credential or infer intent. A claimed cost advantage must be demonstrated against the baseline.

**Strongest innocent explanation and missing evidence.** A conditional credential may not be legally required; evidence may occur in another attachment or an allowed correction; the bidder may lawfully rely on subcontractors. Technical rules or locality-specific expertise can be necessary and lawful. The record may be incomplete because of safety redaction or system publication errors. Missing evidence includes the complete document/version chronology, correction notices, legally applicable qualification rule, professional certificate validity and any appeal disposition. No regulator outreach is proposed as a replacement for absent online evidence.

**Why now and prior coverage.** Free online procurement records long predate 2026. AntAC's [September 2024 construction disclosure article](https://antac.org.ua/en/news/parliament-adopted-the-law-on-opening-cost-estimates-in-construction-procurement/) described a separate new-access change, but prices are not this test. Cheap multilingual extraction may reduce review effort for this document-comparison task; this review did not benchmark it or make paid model calls. It is previously labour-intensive, potentially more affordable now—not proven intractable before 2026. AntAC and TI already monitor procurement and tailored conditions. Newness would be systematic data-nominated, per-clause comparisons with complete negative controls and version-aware proof, extending beyond existing NGO case selection. The prior-coverage check found [DOZORRO AI procurement screening in2018](https://dozorro.org/blog/yak-shtuchnij-intelekt-dozorro-monitorit-zakupivli), including winner-selection and disqualification risks, and existing [proBI document/proposal analysis workflows](https://dozorro.org/tools/pro-bi). [TIUkraine in2024](https://ti-ukraine.org/news/tymchasova-slidcha-komisiya-verhovnoyi-rady-radyt-vprovadyty-u-publichnyh-zakupivlyah-nyzku-propozytsij-dozorro/) called for machine-readable estimate files,e-contracts and risk indicators. The narrower semantic pairing may differ technically,but an overlooked reporting opportunity or cost advantage has not been established. Stop selection as a finalist until a comparison with these existing methods identifies a concrete unmet capability. The gated proBI application was not accessed.

**Pilot, stop/go and costs.** Draw 20 construction tenders with an observable award/rejection, without famous-suspect selection. Acquire every relevant attachment and correction first. Have a specialist annotate ten requirement/evidence/decision triples; compare basic text/table extraction against one proposed model. Stop if both original streams are unavailable for more than 20%, conditional-law interpretation dominates, or any model-produced unsupported accusation appears. Go to at most 100 tenders only if extraction precision is at least90% on ten annotated triples and the model saves measured review time relative to baseline. A contradiction must remain a reporter-review lead. Estimated cash ceiling for an optional future model pass: one million input tokens at assumed US$2/million plus50,000 output tokens at assumedUS$8/million is US$2.40, before retries/OCR/storage; reserve US$20 and stop beforeUS$50. Those assumed rates are cost ceilings, not current-provider pricing claims. Existing local open-source parsing and free endpoints costUS$0; specialist review8–16hours estimated, rate unknown. Model and human time depend on attachment volume, scanned pages, nested archives, correction histories, language and legal complexity. No paid test calls occurred.

## Reproduction and limitations

`census.py` enumerates TI catalogues and PPLAAF news discovery; `acquire.py` selects relevant TI titles, downloads PDFs and indexes text; `pplaaf.py` acquires the substantive custom-post corpus; `finalize.py` applies the explicit human reading ledger. Then run `machine-screen.py`, `prozorro_pilot.py` and `prozorro_compare.py` for bounded access checks. Python standard library plus `pdftotext` suffice. HTTPS certificate verification stays enabled. API responses and copyrighted PDF/HTML/DOCX fulltexts remain in `/tmp/ngo-integrity-cache`, not the repository. Inventory hashes identify the acquired bytes; browser-only AntAC hashes honestly remain null. Signed public attachment URLs may expire; retrieve current URLs from stable tender/document IDs rather than assuming those URLs are permanent.

The census is reproducible for the current website, not an immutable historical archive. Official site updates may alter hashes, dates and counts. Last-modified dates are not substituted for report dates. Full-text PDFs were machine indexed but only marked read through the explicit ledger. The report excludes broad summaries and reports no allegation or new investigative result. **Actual incremental cash spend: US$0. No paid APIs, outreach, or email.**
