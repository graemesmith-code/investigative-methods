Completed requests at Library and Archives Canada

Search our database of completed Access to information requests on archival records. If we have what you're looking for, you can download the release package directly at no cost.

This database is updated regularly. We are actively working on making more documents available to you and improving search functionalities. Only packages for requests that are partially or fully disclosed are available.

All copies of records have been reviewed as per the Access to Information and Privacy Acts. Redactions have been applied for any relevant exemptions or exclusions. Please note that historical documents are available in their original language.

If you can't find what you're looking for, you can consult Collection Search or submit a new ATIP request.

For more information about the use of materials, please see our Terms and conditions.

Email your comments on this new tool to: accesnumerique-digitalaccess@bac-lac.gc.ca or book a feedback session.
