Under a deliberately hypothetical identical-area/identical-definition comparison, 30,000 total minus 20,000 baseline is 10,000, while subtracting 17,200 gives 12,800. Neither is an established project result: the necessary comparability assumptions are unproved. Do not publish either as the amount achieved or the size of an overstatement.

## Chronology, receipt and novelty

The 2007/08 feasibility and 2009 inception documents, **as described by the 2015 synthesis**, predate the 2012 final report. Their original bodies, approval histories and distribution/receipt records are missing. The 2014 evaluation/fieldwork and 2015 synthesis postdate the public claim. No acquired document proves a named official received a conflicting measurement or warning before approving the 2012 statement. A 2011 imagery date is an observation date, not proof of when an assessment was written, circulated or read.

The [2015 Canadian Press account](https://infotel.ca/newsitem/afghanistan-audit-signatureprojects/cp32314565) already covered shifting irrigation estimates and outcome uncertainty. The [2015 synthesis](https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2016/amc-gac/FR5-95-2015-eng.pdf#page=36) itself describes differing baselines and targets. This follow-up therefore does **not** revive general Dahla-performance criticism as new. It checks a definitional inconsistency and eliminates an English-only translation explanation. It does not establish novelty beyond prior reporting; a full Graeme/Globe/French archive review has not been performed.
