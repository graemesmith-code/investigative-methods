Wells

Information related to well activity regulated by the AER as authorized by various acts of the Province of Alberta and the regulations under those acts. Some public information is available through the OneStop Public Map Viewer. For information related to well applications, please see Applications. Petrinex has well lists and production information available to the public.

Hydraulic Fracture Fluid Data

Frequency of Publication
Daily
Description

This product has three parts:

  • Hydraulic Fracturing Fluid Composition Data, Tableau report.
  • Hydraulic Fracturing Chemical Use Water Use Data, CSV file.
  • Hydraulic Fracturing Water Source Data, CSV file.

Hydraulic Fracture Fluid Composition Data attributes: Latest submission date, Latest fracture date, AER Field Centre, Surface location, Well licence number, Licensee name, Well UWI formatted, Well name, Number of stages, Bottom hole latitude, Bottom hole longitude, Lat/Long projection, Production fluid type, True vertical depth (metres),Total water volume used (m3), Fracture scenario, Fracture date, Fracture measured depth (top, base), Component type, Trade name, Type, Supplier, Ingredient CAS/HMIRC number, Fracture Ingr name, Max concentration in component, Max concentration in HF fluid, Purpose, Proppant size, Unit of measure, Volume/weight. Search by Well Licence Number, Well UWI, BA code or name, Surface location or Municipality. Data is for All of Alberta, updated one day in arrears.

Summary Chemical and Water Use attributes: well license number, last submission date, licensee, AER field center, UWI, well name, number of stages, bottom hole latitude, bottom hole longitude, production fluid type, max true vertical depth, total water volume, start date, end date, component type, component trade name, component quantity UOM, total component volume or weight, component supplier name, additive purpose, ingredient name, CAS number/ HMIRC number, maximum concentration component, maximum concentration HFF. Data is for All of Alberta and is updated monthly.

Water Source attributes: submitter licensee BA ID, submission date time, submitted UWI, water source type, water source latitude, water source longitude, water source supplier, water source geological zone, water source water well identifier, water source well UWI label, water source facility name, water source facility licence number, water source reporting facility ID, water diversion authorization ID, water diversion authorization type, water diversion start date, water diversion end date, water diversion max rate, water diversion max rate unit of measure, water diversion average daily rate, total water volume, total dissolved solids content. Attributes change depending on the water source type. Data is for All of Alberta and is updated monthly.

The requirement for this data through Directive 059 became effective on December 31, 2012.
