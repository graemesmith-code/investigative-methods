Field Surveillance Incident Inspection List

Since 1975, we have maintained a list of incidents and complaints reported to the AER. We update the list monthly and include incidents and complaints for wells, facilities, and pipelines concerning

  • odours
  • flaring,
  • public health,
  • safety,
  • lease management,
  • reportable releases (in accordance with AER rules and directives), and
  • pipeline hits (in accordance with AER rules and directives).
