## Uniform ranking, separate access

All55 primary formulations receive four new scores, each0–4: severity of a material established consequence; potential reach/materiality; public accountability; and specificity of the consequential contradiction. Anchors are0 none/undefined,1 narrow/modest,2 material localized,3 serious or broad,4 major harm/systemic money/explicit decisive contradiction as relevant to that dimension. Scores concern the conditional story, never estimated yield. The original card supplies the reason and consequence basis. Access and prior plumbing ratings are retained separately and never enter the sum.

Sort descending sum, break ties by formulation ID, and greedily take the first six distinct families. These produce R01,B02,Q03,X03,X11,Z01. Family diversity is enforced only for the six high-ranked choices. The three random comparisons are N04,N06,R04; they need not occupy new families.

Random seed: `graeme-cross-family-2026-10-06-v1`. For each of the49 remaining primary IDs, compute SHA256 of UTF-8 `seed + "|" + ID`, sort ascending digest, take first3. `code/select.py` reproduces both arms. Fifteen refinements receive no extra sampling weight and are attached to explicit parent buckets. Some original refinement ancestors lie outside the55 detailed primary cards; those attachments are operational sampling groups, not fabricated assertions of exact semantic equivalence. Original lineage is retained in `refinement-groups`.

## Fixed acquisition limits and selection safeguards

Each test gets at most15 active research minutes OR12 retrieval/search attempts, whichever comes first. We will use the stricter elapsed15-minute bound within each sequential scouting block, including reasoning and tool waits. A search query, page/PDF open or HTTP request each counts as one attempt; failures count. Search-result snippets are leads, not original evidence. Metadata selection steps count toward the same cap. No automatic retry that escapes the cap. Each attempt is logged with time, URL/query, result and evidence scope.

Shared retrievals are logged once globally but charged one attempt to every benefiting test; shared active time is split equally, and the raw elapsed cost is also retained. Existing October4 evidence is marked inherited, costs0 new acquisition, and cannot silently substitute for a missing historical original. The five quarantined browser observations remain unusable. Reuse of prior known examples is disclosed; this is not an independent or blinded performance estimate.

The case/period rules above freeze before inspecting potential anomalies. Metadata can resolve the named record, but failure, insufficient detail, paywalls or absent qualifying entries do not trigger replacement. No outcome-based case switching or rescue of favorites. Individual bodies are bounded to1.5MiB by default; no mass dataset download. PDF/page scope may further limit a check. Stop on authentication, payment, reuse restriction or certificate warning. No new credentials, paid APIs, access changes, outreach or security bypass.

## What outcomes mean

- **Blocked:** a necessary original cannot be acquired within permission/access/cap limits.
- **Indeterminate:** originals are acquired but identity, scope, dates or measurements cannot support a meaningful test.
- **Null:** an adequately supported check completes without the predicted discrepancy; coverage limits remain explicit.
- **Falsified lead:** a specific apparent discrepancy is defeated by an ordinary explanation or disqualifier.
- **Surviving lead:** the bounded check leaves a consequential discrepancy after tested ordinary explanations; this is not verified wrongdoing and remaining reporting is named.

An ineligible or unverified starting condition is recorded explicitly under blocked/indeterminate rather than converted into a null. Access success earns no lead claim. Final comparison reports counts, actual retrieval/time cost, original-evidence gaps and narrative lessons. Nine heterogeneous, deliberately chosen/random cases cannot establish statistical superiority, general hit rates or causal benefit of the ranking.
