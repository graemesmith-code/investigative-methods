ESTABLISHED
1. Official DPMA record DE102020127430.0 shows an examination notice dated 28 June 2021, and a withdrawal/waiver entry dated 5 August 2025. The administrative entry reads "Die Anmeldung wurde zurückgenommen/auf das Schutzrecht wurde verzichtet" (the application was withdrawn/the industrial property right was waived). This is the register's combined category, not proof of why the applicant stopped. The present German application cannot be described as kept alive on this evidence. A patent filing and a grant must be distinguished. Do not infer technical abandonment or removal of a safeguard from a product.
2. US20230392899A1 contains 17 claims, not 14. Claims 1-12 concern fire-control calculation and its parameters. Claim 13 adds a blocking parameter, including a definable restricted area; dependent claim 14 withholds a fire-control solution depending on that parameter. Claims 15-17 cover the control system and weapon-system implementation. The description explicitly gives a hospital among possible restricted areas. EP4229352A1 carries corresponding material; its Google machine-translated HTML merges some late claim numbering, so the original German claim text (not recovered in this batch) and US publication should be checked for exact quotation, not silently treating the translation as an authoritative numbered claim transcript.
3. KNDS product material says RCH155 combines automated firepower with a protected mobile Boxer vehicle and autonomy in navigation and fire control. Product terminology about automation does not establish autonomous target selection or the particular hospital-exclusion implementation claimed in the patent.
4. KNDS 19 December 2025 order release establishes 84 German RCH155 systems, roughly EUR1.2 billion including supporting equipment/services, under a framework for 500. That is procurement evidence, not a patent-to-delivered-configuration bridge.
5. UK MoD's 13 May 2026 release establishes 72 RCH155 systems in a GBP1 billion investment. No exact patent number is tied to a procured fire-control software configuration in this release.
6. Freundeskreis Artillerietruppe's first-hand account of the 9 May 2023 Kassel visit names Matthias Czok as development project manager in a visit covering RCH155, PzH2000 and MLRS. German source phrase: "Projektleiter Entwicklung, Herr Matthias Czok". Translation: development project manager, Mr Matthias Czok. It does not say which patent was implemented or certify the safety behavior of RCH155. Do not overread it as an exclusive RCH155 role.
7. RENK AGM2024 PDF page 10 identifies Axel Scheibel as KNDS CTO and connects his development leadership with RCH155, among other products. This was freshly readable through the web search PDF reader. Direct byte retrieval returned HTTP503 twice; no raw RENK PDF was preserved in this batch. Consequently it is a verified readable source with a raw-archive gap, not a recovered primary file. URL in manifest failures.
