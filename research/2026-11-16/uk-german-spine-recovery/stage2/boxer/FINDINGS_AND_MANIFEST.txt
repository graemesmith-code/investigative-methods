STAGE 2 GAP 3: BOXER / PROTECTOR RS4 / GVA / LOSA
Research date: 16 November 2026. Public-source targeted pass; no outreach.

BOTTOM LINE
Public documents substantiate integration of remote weapon stations and shared electronic architecture into Boxer, but not the installed safety circuit or the use of US11946710B1 Claim 14 in a named delivered UK Boxer configuration. GVA compliance must not be treated as proof of that patented safeguard.

NEW PRIMARY FINDINGS
1. Rheinmetall/RBSL, 12 September 2023, describes UK Boxer prototypes/production vehicles with UK-supplied remote weapon stations, GVA components, situational-awareness and emergency-lighting systems. This is hardware-programme integration evidence, not a wiring diagram or a safety acceptance record.
Source B02: https://www.rheinmetall.com/Rheinmetall%20Group/Presse/News/Documents/2023/9/Presskit%20DSEI/2023-09-12%20Rheinmetall%20RBSL%20Boxer%20MIV%20update%20engl%20final.pdf

2. UK MoD LOSA booklet (landing page published 21 September 2026, booklet copyright 2025), printed pp30–37: GVA integrates mission systems including remote weapon stations over a shared Ethernet network. It is an integration approach, not a prescriptive vehicle design. It lists supplier architecture-management plans, architecture cases, interface descriptions and deployed-system build sets as contract artefacts. Those are specific records to request for the Boxer baseline. Its Part2 overview includes hard control panels, but does not establish a dedicated weapon arming loop. Printed p34 has a low/high network cross-domain guard: that security separation must not be conflated with the weapon safety barrier.

PATENT COMPARISON
US11946710B1 describes human authorisation of semi-autonomous engagement and safety-barrier/control channels. The correct comparison is whether a supplied weapon configuration has independently assured means of inhibiting operation, not whether a general GVA diagram contains a safety-looking symbol. Its patent disclosure is not a certification, procurement specification or proof of implementation.
https://patents.google.com/patent/US11946710B1/en
Read Claim14 directly alongside Claim5 before quoting the physical disconnect. Do not conflate hardware isolation with a claim that all signals use physically separate cabling: this patent also describes barrier signalling transported through networks.

BOUNDED GAP
No examined current public normative GVA text or named Boxer acceptance document establishes a mandatory dedicated hardware safety loop/physical arming switch separated from its mission bus. We retrieved the MOD introduction, not the full currently contracted normative standard. Absence from this search is NOT evidence no requirement or safety loop exists. Historical sources saying GVA Part8 was under development are not reliable evidence of the2026 position.
