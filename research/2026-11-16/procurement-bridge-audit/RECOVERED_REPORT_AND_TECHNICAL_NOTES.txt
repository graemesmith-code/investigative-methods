PART I. HUMAN-READABLE FINDINGS

BOTTOM LINE
We moved beyond matching company names: we found inventors connected with programmes, named subsystems under contract, official patent-register facts and concrete requirements for testing changing AI. But no reviewed public document establishes that any of the seven purchased configurations incorporates the particular patented restraint. That remains a gap, not evidence that safeguards are absent.

The question for a newspaper reader is what restraints the buyer actually required, which configuration passed independent testing, who controls later changes, and what outsiders can inspect. A patent is not a promise to install its invention. Withdrawal does not mean abandoning technology. The strongest accountability material concerns procurement and verification, not allegations inferred from patent status.

1. KNDS / RCH155
The official DPMA register records the German priority application DE102020127430.0 withdrawn/waived on5 August2025, published11 September2025. The hypothesis that KNDS kept this German application alive while dropping the European route is contradicted. We do not know why, or whether the protected-area function remains in RCH155.

RENK's official2024 AGM biography attributes RCH155 development to patent inventor Axel Scheibel's R&D leadership. This is a genuine personnel/programme bridge. A firsthand artillery-association account identifies second inventor Matthias Czok as KMW development project leader. Neither proves the patented restricted-area safeguard is installed.

KNDS describes an onboard fire-control computer with integrated ballistics and a customer-specified link to an artillery command system. We did not establish the exact computer/software baseline or whether no-fire-area enforcement occurs onboard, externally or both. Do not transfer PzH2000 arrangements to RCH155.

2. KONGSBERG
Kongsberg's2018 article identifies inventor Lars Egil Bjørset as PROTECTOR MCT product manager. MCT is not RS4 and this predates the2022 application. The2023 Typhon contract announcement identifies CORTEX Integrated Combat Solution, remote weapon stations and Teledyne FLIR components. It establishes named contracted architecture, not the patented authorization switches or lost-link arrangement.

The January2022 disengagement invention and October2022 authorization invention are distinct applications. Shared subject matter/inventor does not prove consolidation or continuation. Earlier US abandonment remains an aggregator report, not an official prosecution conclusion.

3. NAVAL GROUP
INPI officially marks EP3812690 revoked in France, while separate FR3102239 records grant and a September2026 annuity payment. This is not merely inconsistent Google labels. However, central European revocation grounds/appeal and identical surviving French claims remain unverified. Atlas Elektronik's reported opposition is a lead, not a proven explanation.

A separate contracted autonomy subsystem exists: ADMP in DGA underwater-drone demonstrator work. Naval says it follows operator-defined objectives, behavioural rules and permitted freedom. This does not connect the torpedo patent to F21MK2. Keep drone demonstrator, torpedo and patent separate. Dutch sources identify COMMIT, Maritime Warfare Centre and Submarine Service as accountability routes; F21 contract signature was15 June2026, not its16 June announcement.

4. DIEHL
INPI records grant and March2026 French annuities for EP3882560/61. That does not mean all family rights are active everywhere. German priority withdrawal remained an aggregator lead.

The patents address cooperative friendly-identification signals and stopping an attack, including a separate transmitter in the companion invention. This is not general civilian recognition. No reviewed contract maps them to purchased IRIS-T variants. The original German BlockII announcement describes development and preparation for series readiness, not delivered capability.

5. RAYTHEON
Patent inventors Peggy Wu and Kunal Srivastava coauthored the2022 paper A Tiered Approach for Ethical AI Evaluation Metrics, identifying Raytheon Missiles & Defense funding and Naval Postgraduate School collaborators. That is actual research provenance, not a weapon acceptance test or ACTS adoption.

The official ACTS award notice gives contract707414450, concluded29 June2026, Raytheon Systems UK, £1,946,794,337.67 excluding VAT. Raytheon describes a Combat Laboratory digital-twin layer and ethical oversight of AI analysis. ACTS is collective training, not procurement of an autonomous weapon; ethical oversight is not automatically the patent's reasoning mechanism.

The separate firing-cutout patent has different inventors. No current UK/European launcher contract was connected to it. Operator removal of a protected object from a map is an interface fact, not proof of wrongdoing.

6. BAE
MBDA's December2025 production-contract article explicitly names BAE among participating organisations. Earlier emphasis on MBDA/QinetiQ/Leonardo was not proof BAE left DragonFire. No public source maps BAE's collateral-risk patent to the accepted configuration.

Inventor Keith Rigby's2025 Royal Aeronautical Society biography describes patented safe-control work over unreliable communications and leadership of Rapid Stores Integration Capability, subsequently adopted into Pyramid Reference Architecture. It does not name this patent or DragonFire. His2025 affiliation is a consultancy, not necessarily current BAE employment. A2022 programme lists his aircraft laser integration presentation, not its slides/results.

7. 21STRATEGIES / HELSING
Inventor Christian Brandlhuber coauthored Thinking Before Sinking (2025) with Heiko Borchert and ATLAS's Jeronimo Dzaack. Its Black Pearl is a fictitious simulation scenario, not a delivered ship. A Hofstetter ethics-engineering study illustrates testable requirements including authorization, uncertainty information and unfamiliar-data testing; that is not proof GhostPlay@SEA passed them.

Sim2Live Bridge/JATOC are useful named follow-up targets but described in development/future language. Helsing DE102025101672A1 includes a dependent no-engagement option, not necessarily an ethical veto or mandatory human approval. Its description permits deployed training but prefers simulation. Examiner citation of21strategies proves neither adoption, licensing nor partnership.

ASSURANCE AND TRANSPARENCY
JSP936 requires measures bounding behaviour, assessment across modes/autonomy levels, testing after updates and continuing assurance. It does not prescribe one universal patented interlock. Risk-managed tailoring exists, so ask which requirements/exceptions were accepted. DefStan00-055 software assurance matters alongside00-056 wider safety.

The UK's2024 response says material changes make renewed Article36 review appropriate. It does not say every learning update automatically invalidates approval. Germany's public K-9000/102 calls for independent-personnel validation, revalidation after retraining and conformity assessment for substantial changes; in-mission updates follow successful revalidation.

In Bundestag21/9054,10 November2026, the government refuses the specifically requested discrimination-testing details, including classified disclosure, subject to information already given. It also says the requested sensor/image/data-rate requirements are not unclassified. This is a concrete transparency boundary, not proof all scrutiny is barred or tests absent. Separate government answers from the AfD questioners' allegations.

PART V. METHODS, LIMITS AND CHECKER INSTRUCTIONS

The original run used targeted multilingual queries, official registers/metadata, public procurement/company papers and parliamentary sources. It separated patent, inventor, demonstrator, award, delivery and accepted configuration; originals from rendered extracts; dependent from independent claims. An independent check refined the standards conclusions to includeJSP75 behaviour bounds,17–18 tailoring and00-055 software standards.

Before interruption, the assembled package reported45 patent-family/lead records (not45 fully audited families),34 selected source rows,42 sourceURLs with archive/log association,132 query records including batches/repeats,211 files and a25,860,067-byte ZIP. These numbers describe a package previously assembled, not full records recoverable in thisTXT. Its original complete manifest and logs are missing; they are not fabricated here.

Not every early query timestamp was captured exactly; missing/reconstructed intervals were labelled. Engines often did not expose exhaustive result totals. Null was not zero. Raw originals sometimes failed while rendered extracts were available. Full family/prosecution review, exact counterpart claim equivalence, independent acceptance tests, contract-tailored standards and complete transition histories remained incomplete even before the preservation failure.

Access limits includedEPO403,USPTO403,HelsingDPMA401,INPI documentCAPTCHA,Find-a-Tender403 (separate public Welsh notice worked),DefStan403,ASEMS503,GermanPDF timeout/404,Google patent503,SPIE200 security wrapper. No bypass/accounts/payment/outreach.

FOR INDEPENDENT CHECKING:
1. Re-fetch exact sources and official registers; logUTC/status/MIME/bytes/SHA256. A200 security wrapper is not a document.
2. Reconstruct exact members and dates. EP designations are not national grants; family claims may differ.
3. Obtain decisions before assigning prosecution objections/motives. Withdrawal is not product abandonment.
4. Require explicit patent/subsystem/configuration evidence. Biography and ethics marketing do not establish implementation.
5. Distinguish supplier assurance from independent tests; award/delivery/acceptance; simulation/physical demonstration; each variant.
6. Check must/should, tailoring, contractual incorporation and update review. No universal automatic-invalidity inference.
7. Keep the German refusal limited to the requested information; do not infer absent tests or total oversight failure.
8. Restore full logs and raw copies before claiming a complete reproducible audit. This is a recovered substantive package, not an intact original evidence ledger.
