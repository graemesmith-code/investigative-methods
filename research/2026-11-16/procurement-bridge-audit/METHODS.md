## Question
What public documentary evidence links specified patented safeguards to named UK/European procurement configurations, and who independently verifies their operation?

## Protocol
Four parallel streams cover KNDS/Kongsberg; Naval Group/Diehl/Raytheon; BAE/21strategies/Helsing; and UK/German safety/legal review requirements. Search English, German, French and Norwegian as relevant. Record exact queries, tool/endpoint, retrieval time, returned counts where available (unknown is not zero), and access failures. Preserve raw accessible public sources and SHA-256 hashes. A search-engine extract is not a raw source. A failed fetch cannot establish absence.

Check official registers for exact publication/application and date. Aggregator status remains a lead unless independently established; expiry, withdrawal, revocation, non-entry and national validation must not be conflated. A patent family is not a single identical claim set. Track version-specific and dependent claims. Prosecution objections require actual prosecution documents.

Separate patent, inventor paper, research programme, demonstrator, contract, delivery and configured operational system. A shared company or technology class does not prove incorporation. Explicit flags: official_status_verified; inventor_paper_found; named_subsystem_linked; contract_safeguard_confirmed. Each requires scoped supporting text; false means not established in this audit, not that it does not exist.

Do not infer malice from claim hierarchy or standard fail-operational backups. Do not infer effectiveness from marketing. Use bounded negative statements, not 'proved public absence'. Regulatory conclusions distinguish mandatory requirements, guidance and our inference. Do not claim that online learning automatically invalidates approval without an explicit source.

No outreach, filings, accounts, payments or security-block bypass. Only public documentary research and authorized preservation. Technical analysis addresses safeguards/accountability, not weapon construction or operational optimization.
