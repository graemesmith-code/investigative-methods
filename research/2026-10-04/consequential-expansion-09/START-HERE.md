# Start with a claim you can disprove from a small set of originals

**The useful result is 55 reviewed investigative formulations, plus 15 refinements—not 5,000 proven opportunities.** The 344-source sweep produced a broad longlist; closer review found that most promising-looking joins still need original documents, reliable historical identity or a complete measurement boundary. None of the 70 detailed cards has been demonstrated as an end-to-end feasible investigation or a wrongdoing finding.

For a journalist starting now, use a consequential claim already in hand. Try the two checks below first. They can yield a meaningful rejection or a tightly defined contradiction from a small set of texts. Then consider the four more demanding routes. “Cheap” here means a limited evidence packet, not a measured number of hours or guaranteed free access.

## First choices

**1. Does an official's claim match the law actually enacted? — N04**

If someone says a named bill enacted a precise protection or spending authority, compare that exact dated assertion with the authenticated enacted provision, its commencement rule and relevant amendments. Stop if the assertion is only a future promise, refers to another Congress/version, or turns on unresolved legal interpretation. Remaining packet: the original statement, identified provision and applicable version history. This pass retrieved a complete official law through normal HTTPS and matched its law/bill identifiers; it did not select a misleading statement. For the historical example, Public Law 118-5 was approved June 3, 2023; an enrolled-bill catalogue date is not a substitute. [Official law and metadata](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/PLAW-118publ5/).

**2. Does a later paper actually rely on a withdrawn finding? — P01**

If an original publisher notice retracts a specific finding, read the exact passage in a later citing paper. Keep a candidate only when it affirmatively uses that affected finding, its relevant publication chronology follows the notice, and the use matters to a medical or policy conclusion. Remaining packet: publisher notice/scope, full citing passage and bibliography, online/acceptance/version dates. Criticism, discussion of the retraction, earlier acceptance or reliance on an unaffected result can defeat the lead. A readable citation is not an endorsement: this pass read a [CMAJ article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2831678/) that discusses the retraction and therefore must be excluded. That is a useful negative control, not a tested citation-graph match or a medical conclusion.

## Higher-consequence checks with larger remaining packets

| Test | Concrete if/then test | Obtain before scaling | Cheap stopping rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| X01 / disclosure | If an article expressly denies relevant financial relationships, compare it with an independently identified author's direct payment within the applicable policy window. | Full declaration/supplements/corrections, dated journal policy, author-to-recipient identity, original payment category and payer/product. | Stop on namesakes, institutional research payments, excluded windows or an existing disclosure. Payment alone does not establish bias. |
| R05 / trial reporting | If a linked publication presents a consequential trial result, compare it with the prospectively specified outcome and analysis. | Pre-enrollment registry/protocol version, statistical analysis plan, full paper/supplements, amendments and explanation. | Stop if the paper is openly secondary, amendments explain the difference, or the prospective original cannot be dated. An NCT link is just plumbing. |
| X11 / evidence counted twice | If a synthesis treats two reports as independent populations, reconstruct whether the same people contribute twice to the same comparison. | Synthesis methods and study rows, both primary reports, cohort/arm/recruitment mapping, actual effect calculation. | Stop if cohorts are disjoint, outcomes differ appropriately, or dependence is handled correctly. [Cochrane's guidance](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-05) supports studying studies rather than merely counting reports. |
| R01 / sanctions and public money | If the recipient is genuinely the restricted entity, inspect individual dated contract actions under the applicable restriction. | Verified identity/ownership, designation history, action descriptions and amounts, underlying transaction and authorizations/exceptions. | Stop on a namesake, pre-restriction action, termination/correction, or authorized conduct. An award total or OFAC overlap is not a prohibited payment. |

These are small test designs, not six allegations or six case-ready leads. Original-source acquisition and subject explanations may still dominate effort. No claim is made that any can be finished in an hour, a day or at zero total cost.

## Which could make the biggest story?

The separate [news-potential view](strongest-news-potential.csv) prioritizes, conditionally: restricted actors receiving public money (R01), construction before a specific required approval (X07), a precise material issuer claim contradicted by a final facility finding (P05), changed quantities reversing a real bid comparison (Z01), relevant undisclosed payments (X01), materially double-counted medical evidence (X11), and departures from prospective trial outcomes (R05). The reason is the possible public consequence if the facts hold—not an estimated probability of wrongdoing or publication.

The [smallest-original-packet view](cheapest-meaningful-checks.csv) also lists exact assay attribution (X12), two authenticated releases of the same page (N06), and two copies claiming to be the same audited accounts (X14). These are conditional backups when those originals are already accessible; access to their paired originals has not been demonstrated. Their position is a structural comparison judgment, not measured practical cheapness. Do not spend days acquiring them merely because their arithmetic is simple.

## What the final retrieval checks changed

Using the USPTO's [current direct-PDF instructions](https://www.uspto.gov/sites/default/files/documents/PDF-links-QRG-Patent-Public-Search.pdf), a normal validated-HTTPS request retrieved an 18-page granted patent. Its cover and claims were visually inspected. The earlier guessed-host 404 did not demonstrate an access barrier. This is an unrelated apparatus patent used only as a retrieval control; it supplies no medical claim match or complete priority chain for J02.

A real GIF linked from a NASA page was retrieved through validated HTTPS, then queried in the archive availability service. The exact URL returned no archived snapshot. That confirms current bytes, not a historical capture or a general archive-access failure. J01 still needs the actual incident media's archived response and a falsifiable event-capture caption. No blocked interface was probed further.

**Security correction:** Five earlier AER/ClinicalTrials browser observations used disabled certificate verification after a warning. That was unauthorized; they are quarantined and excluded from verified counts. No credentials or sensitive user data were supplied; no live browser remains. Reusable probes were repaired. The earlier rendered well identity and trial-history fields must not be reused as verified evidence. [Exact incident and remediation](../consequential-expansion-08/evidence/certificate-incident.json).

## Where discovery now stands

All 344 explicitly labeled descriptions were normalized and reviewed; 117,992 directed pairs were enumerated for policy screening, not individually researched. The broad generation pass produced 2,535 candidate rows: 797 rejected, 8 deduplicated and 1,730 retained conditionally; later review left 1,715 active rows and 15 quarantines. Rows, detailed formulations, refinements, access checks and findings are different units.

The remaining productive step for this reviewed slate is mostly **case-specific original evidence acquisition**. More defensible mechanisms may still exist in these descriptions, and additional source families may improve existing tests. This is not universal exhaustion. But repeatedly recombining field names now produces more weak applications than new falsifiable ideas. Select one live public-interest question, secure its small evidence packet, and then decide whether scale is justified.

The model supplied source reasoning and editorial judgments. Code handled enumeration, semantic keys, duplicate screening, sorting and validation; it did not discover wrongdoing or prove novelty automatically. Raw consequence/testability scales from different batches are retained separately and never mixed. The two new views use explicit editorial slates and deterministic ordering. Method-development time—including dead ends, review and corrections—is not the per-test runtime a journalist would face. Neither that runtime nor weekly ChatGPT consumption has been measured. Latest user-reported allowance: 88% remaining; paid calls: zero.
