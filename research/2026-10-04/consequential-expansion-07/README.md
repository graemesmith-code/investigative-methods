## Detailed cards

### J01 — Media attributed to an incident but authenticated before that incident

Status: reviewed_new.

**Initial observation:** IF a public assessed-incident source explicitly presents a particular image or video as captured during a defined event, and the same original media payload is authenticated in an archive response captured strictly before the earliest possible event time.

**Expected observation:** THEN that particular attribution cannot be contemporaneous capture of that later event. Check the caption, chronology, identity and archive-response provenance before describing the limited evidentiary contradiction. Do not infer that the incident, casualties or other evidence are false.

**Basis:** This is a chronological incompatibility, not a count of independent reports. Material misattribution may distort accountability for civilian harm. Earlier media may legitimately be an illustration, portrait, retrospective comparison or background image; the explicit event-capture assertion is indispensable.

**Operational join:** Airwars public assessed incident ID/date → original public source and exact caption → original media URL → the media URL’s own archived response, capture timestamp, redirect chain, MIME and payload hash. An archived HTML wrapper, calendar date, unchanged URL, replay screenshot or perceptual resemblance is insufficient. Common Crawl SYS0134 is only an optional alternative if an actual historical payload and crawl provenance are obtained; not used here.

**Temporal geographic scope:** Compare authenticated capture-time upper bound with event-time lower bound, using explicit timezones and uncertainty. The caption must refer to that event/version. A parent-page capture date cannot date embedded media: Wayback can substitute other-date or live resources. Archive timestamp is evidence of existence by capture time, not the camera’s creation timestamp.

**Ordinary explanations and disqualifiers:** Illustrative, portrait, background or retrospective use; Event date wrong or imprecise; Caption updated or refers to a different event; Near-duplicate crop without established original identity; Old HTML replay pulls later/live media; Redirect resolves to different object; Archive timestamp or response authenticity unresolved; Hash computed from thumbnail or error page rather than original media

**Mandatory evidence:** Exact public source caption and event attribution; Reliable event-date bounds; Original current and archived media payloads with hashes; Media-response capture provenance and redirect chain; Payload type and authentication checks; Version history and benign use assessment

**Next cheapest check:** Read the exact caption first. If it explicitly attributes capture to the event, inspect one pre-event capture of the media URL itself. Stop if the payload is missing, identity uncertain or only an old wrapper is available. Use only publicly assessed Airwars sources, not restricted prepublication Source Library material.

**Novelty comparison:** REF-EVID examines claimed source independence; X11 examines overlapping evidence units. J01 tests an authenticated earlier existence that contradicts a specific later capture attribution. Independent review accepted this as a distinct formulation, not one new test per incident or media format.

**Access evidence:** Airwars methodology/access distinction and Wayback replay documentation inspected. No incident/media/archive pair retrieved; archived payload availability and attribution remain unverified. No restricted source library accessed.

### REF-AER — Fracturing after a scoped seismic stop trigger without required restart consent

Status: refinement.

**Initial observation:** IF a well and actual fracturing interval fall within the operative scope of AER Order2A, and an event meeting its local-magnitude and distance trigger was recorded by/on behalf of the licensee or another source available to it during that operation.

**Expected observation:** THEN inspect whether operations ceased and whether any later fracturing occurred only after applicable written restart consent. A completion date range overlapping an earthquake is a screening lead, not evidence of continued injection or absence of consent.

**Basis:** The publisher’s current index lists Order2A, effective2024-11-19, for its defined Duvernay-to-basement zone and AppendixA area. Its4.0ML/5km trigger and written-restart-consent provisions create a concrete safety sequence. Application to a particular well/date, actual knowledge/availability and any other governing instrument still require verification.

**Operational join:** AER well licence/UWI and dated licensee → fracture start/end, stage or event records and surface/bottomhole location/depth → authoritative seismic event ID/time/location and ML → operation cessation/restart logs and original consent. USGS SYS0205 is discovery only; preferred magnitude may be Mw. Supporting AGS catalog has distinct ML/MLy/M/Mw fields, which require definitions and revision checks rather than substitution.

**Temporal geographic scope:** Only the order’s mapped area, geological interval, relevant dates and affected well definition; not all Alberta. Surface/bottomhole and distance interpretation must follow the instrument and authoritative coordinates, including uncertainty. Align local/UTC times and actual injection stages. Current index checked2026-10-04; recheck amendments and well-specific permissions before execution.

**Ordinary explanations and disqualifiers:** Wrong formation or outside AppendixA; Mw or generic magnitude substituted for ML; Location uncertainty crosses threshold boundary; Event outside active fracturing interval; Multiday operation interval includes an actual stop; Source not shown available to licensee at relevant time; Written consent or other operative authorization exists; Completion/cleanup activity mistaken for injection; Revised earthquake solution or well coordinates

**Mandatory evidence:** Operative order and case-specific scope; Licence/UWI/operator history and mapped well/depth; Actual stage/day or finer injection and cessation/restart evidence; Applicable magnitude type, event time/location/uncertainty; Evidence of licensee recording or source availability; Original written consent or sufficiently complete consent record

**Next cheapest check:** Check one well’s record granularity and whether the original consent route is publicly usable before computing any broad distance join. If only start/end summaries exist, defer the continued-injection allegation. An unindexed consent is not proof of no consent.

**Novelty comparison:** Refinement of X07 required approval before action, applied to restart after a safety trigger. A better operational source route is useful but is not counted as a new non-refinement formulation.

**Access evidence:** AER product definitions, three-page order and current order index inspected. No well/event/consent pair acquired. AGS is an explicitly external supporting official dataset under AER, not fabricated as one of the344 descriptions. Free data listing does not establish unrestricted reuse; no CSV bulk download or permission request made.

### J02 — Historical trial disclosure as a claim-specific patent prior-art lead

Status: reviewed_new.

**Initial observation:** IF a public historical trial protocol or registry version describes a specific method before the legally effective filing date of a particular granted patent claim, and a documented technical comparison links that disclosure to the claim.

**Expected observation:** THEN build a claim chart testing every required element, including arrangement and relevant dependent-claim limitations, against one qualifying historical reference. Missing literal wording alone does not establish a missing limitation: specialist-reviewed inherent disclosure and explanatory evidence may apply, without assembling missing elements from separate references. Resolve enablement, public accessibility, priority entitlement and exceptions with a patent specialist before making any anticipation or invalidity assertion.

**Basis:** An improperly sustained exclusive right over a previously disclosed medical method could affect access and competition. US anticipation guidance requires the claim’s elements in a single qualifying reference; the applicable statutory regime and disclosure exceptions matter. A search hit or shared ingredient is not a legal conclusion.

**Operational join:** NCT number + exact archived/public version and first-public availability of the relied-on text → explicit method/regimen evidence → exact US publication/grant and claim number through PatentsView/EPO discovery → authoritative claim text, prosecution amendments and complete priority chain. There is no universal NCT-to-patent identifier bridge. A patent family groups documents but does not establish identical claims or priority entitlement.

**Temporal geographic scope:** US claim-specific screening only; determine AIA/pre-AIA applicability. Use the date the relied-on content became publicly accessible, not trial enrollment, submission, last update or patent grant date. Claim-specific effective filing date may precede visible filing and differ across claims. Do not transplant US rules to an EPO jurisdiction.

**Ordinary explanations and disqualifiers:** Earlier valid claim priority; Relied-on protocol text added later; Different dose, population, sequence or method limitation; One required element absent; Disclosure not enabling or publicly accessible; Applicable inventor-origin or other statutory exception; Claim amended or cancelled; Only abstract/ingredient similarity; Combination of several references silently substituted for single-reference anticipation

**Mandatory evidence:** Historically fixed trial text and public-access evidence; Exact current granted claim plus relevant amendment/status history; Complete priority chain and support for the particular claim; Element-by-element single-reference chart; Technical enablement analysis; Applicable legal regime and exception review by specialist

**Next cheapest check:** Choose one exact granted claim and one historical trial version with an explicit technical bridge. Check their dates and one discriminating limitation first. Stop if an element is absent or priority cannot be established; do not expand from ingredient similarity. Cheap triage is not cheap legal proof.

**Novelty comparison:** H03 concerns publicly funded inventions; Z04 conflicting exclusive licences. J02 concerns whether an earlier enabling public disclosure anticipates an exact later claim. It is a proposed distinct predicate, not a new test per patent family, claim or trial version.

**Access evidence:** USPTO anticipation/priority guidance and public-search documentation inspected; no trial/patent pair acquired. ClinicalTrials guide returned only a thin page through direct browsing; no historical text retrieved here. Patent Public Search announces an account requirement from2026-11-07; no account created. EPO credentials and full priority-file access remain unresolved.
