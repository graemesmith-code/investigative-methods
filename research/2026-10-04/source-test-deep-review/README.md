## Concrete opportunities

- **A production claim that exceeds a complete total (N09):** an issuer's claimed physical provincial production cannot exceed the province's complete total for the exact same commodity, unit and period. First rule out concentrate/contained-metal differences, purchased feed, toll processing and suppressed statistics. This is a conditional arithmetic bound, not a discovered discrepancy.
- **Different releases of the same document (N06):** match original document number and page across CIA and National Security Archive copies, then compare redactions and release markings. Later declassification is an ordinary explanation; a consequential newly readable passage can still be newsworthy.
- **Aid accounts that should reconcile (N01/N02):** compare the same project or award only after resolving transaction stage, reporting period, currency and amendments. Commitments, obligations and disbursements are not interchangeable.
- **A claimed law versus its operative text (N04):** trace the exact Congress/bill/version to the enacted instrument. A passed proposal or enrolled bill must not be casually described as an effective requirement.

The nine new cards have a separate [automatic triage ranking](new-formulations-ranked.csv). It combines explicitly authored consequence, testability, identifier-confidence and burden scores; it is not a calibrated probability of finding a story. The 18 inherited cards retain their ordinal editorial priorities, so the two scales are not mixed.

The original text is preserved. Official coverage evidence now overrides the coarse label in every affected emitted recipe: 31 receive explicit coverage gates, and 15 are quarantined. A documented US-covered Microsoft product is a possible alternative, not a silent substitution. See [Google's official list](https://sites.research.google/gr/open-buildings/) and [Microsoft's US product](https://github.com/microsoft/USBuildingFootprints).

Structured route evidence now changes production access ranking for Carbon Mapper, EIA and ENTSO-E. Manual/registration/API-key constraints also affect burden rather than merely adding a warning. Every route remains subject to actual terms and coverage verification.

The validation suite now checks IMO ship/company semantics through the production join function, reporter/partner/flow tuple reversal, and the actual saved linking values. It also preserves important negative results: the trial sample was registered after recorded completion; the OONI sample has anomaly=false and confirmed=false; RIPE returned stale historical information; the World Bank agreement text is dated 30-Dec-2024 while its index says 31-Dec-2026. A routine amendment does not establish project failure.

The adversarial ledger now separates a **proposed falsifier**, the **check actually performed**, and the **observed result**. R08 explicitly has no WorldPop raster/product probe. R07's coverage check does not validate a local overlay. An earlier note wrongly described Space-Track as radio allocation; that identity error is corrected and preserved in the correction ledger.

## What remains beyond this completed pass

The limiting work is evidence acquisition and source-pair verification: exact historical ownership, complete trial versions, source geometry, registry/award crosswalks, actual institutional orders, complete reporting coverage and current access terms. Paris MoU authorization, Equasis registration and several API credentials remain access gates. No new accounts, paid APIs or bulk investigative runs were used.

This pass is complete at the source-discovery level, not at the execution level. The next useful phase is to take selected cards into small, explicitly scoped pilot investigations after their gates are resolved—not to keep generating paraphrases.
