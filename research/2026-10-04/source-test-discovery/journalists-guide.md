# How to try one question cheaply

Pick a concrete public-interest event, entity, project or claim. Read the two source descriptions and their weaknesses. Verify the schema and one permissible record before collecting more. Save the raw record, source URL, retrieval date and effective period. Check the proposed identity or location independently. Read the disqualifiers before interpreting any result.

A failed access request belongs in the log. A failed join belongs in the rejected list. A missing search result is not absence evidence. Use agency records, legal text, full filings or a suitable measurement model to resolve the remaining question; offer affected parties the underlying evidence and seek their explanation before publication.

These sixteen examples are a varied reading queue, not an override of the full numeric ranking. Time estimates cover a first documentation check only; historical documents, data cleaning and expert interpretation may be expensive. None is a ready-to-run accusation or a validated executable test.

## 1. US OFAC Sanctions List Service → USAspending API

**T-S01-0024-0054 · sanctions**

IF US OFAC Sanctions List Service supplies this verified observation: A sanctions entry identifies a person, entity or vessel and the restriction was in force on the relevant date. THEN inspect USAspending API: A dated award or assistance record can establish a public financial relationship; inspect whether an action actually created obligations, deliveries or payments.

Why it matters: Public money reaching a restricted counterparty is consequential if the applicable restriction reaches the specific conduct. Award records alone are not payment evidence.

Interesting result: New positive action after the relevant restriction merits an underlying-document check. Zero-dollar closeouts and termination actions do not show continued payments.

Try first: First verify USAspending API's documented schema and one permissible record; then: Open one award action and read its description, amount type and effective date before expanding the match.

Join: Screen the named entity/person in its stated role, then confirm with jurisdiction-scoped registration identifiers or independent biographical evidence. Source-local IDs are not cross-source keys.

Ordinary explanations: Closeout; termination; refunds; pre-existing obligations; humanitarian or other authorization; same-name recipient.

Stop or qualify: First resolve actual identity and entity type; distinguish SDN blocking from non-SDN restrictions; establish the applicable jurisdiction, conduct, dates, program and authorizations/exceptions. A match alone establishes none of these.

Evidence needed: Designation history, complete award modifications, recipient registration, performance and payment records, program rules and licenses.

## 8. US EPA Greenhouse Gas Reporting Program → Carbon Mapper Data Platform API

**T-C01-0217-0214 · environment**

IF US EPA Greenhouse Gas Reporting Program supplies this verified observation: A facility report identifies pollutant emissions, location and reporting year. THEN inspect Carbon Mapper Data Platform API: Independent plume observations can be compared only for the same pollutant, operating period and physically attributable source.

Why it matters: Independent measurements may reveal reporting discrepancies; intermittent remote flux cannot be directly equated with annual reported mass.

Interesting result: A repeated material discrepancy surviving measurement and temporal reconciliation merits operator/regulator questions.

Try first: First verify Carbon Mapper Data Platform API's documented schema and one permissible record; then: Confirm common gas and an observed plume that can be attributed to the facility.

Join: Intersect verified footprints/time intervals in a common CRS. First confirm usable geometry, spatial resolution and effective dates in both schemas.

Ordinary explanations: Intermittent releases; neighboring source; wind; detection limits; different gases; model uncertainty.

Stop or qualify: Quarantine any comparison with no common gas, compatible units, usable observations or source attribution.

Evidence needed: Original report scope, plume retrieval quality, wind field, production history and uncertainty.

## 15. UN Comtrade → Eurostat Comext

**T-F02-0069-0070 · finance**

IF UN Comtrade supplies this verified observation: A bilateral commodity series records EU exports or imports for a specified product and period. THEN inspect Eurostat Comext: The corresponding partner-side trade series may differ after harmonizing direction, valuation, classification and timing.

Why it matters: Mirror gaps can identify measurement or trade-route questions; freight, re-exporting and valuation routinely create differences.

Interesting result: A persistent material residual after reconciliation merits inspecting customs methodology and specific routes.

Try first: First verify Eurostat Comext's documented schema and one permissible record; then: Select non-EU partner-reported Comtrade data against EU-reported Comext, not the same EU series redistributed in both; reconcile one product-month including valuation and re-export treatment.

Join: Harmonize destination/state codes, dates and category meanings; never infer entity identity from country totals.

Ordinary explanations: CIF versus FOB; lags; transit; re-exports; confidentiality; code revision; special trade systems.

Stop or qualify: No firm-level misinvoicing or sanctions-evasion claim from aggregate discrepancies.

Evidence needed: Both trade metadata, concordances, quantity/value units, partner definitions and revisions.
