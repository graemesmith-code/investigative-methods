# What the quality gates found

The sweep retains conditional designs only. It does not claim executable validation, complete coverage, current legal applicability or evidence of misconduct. All legal conclusions remain conditional; only general OFAC guidance was checked against current official pages. No other jurisdiction's rule was silently treated as established.

The early 79-design checkpoint reviewed 40 candidates and ran 16 automated tests before expansion. The first broader sweep generated 1,949 retained candidates, of which a new 40-row defect sample caught 13 failures. Those failures triggered rule-wide quarantine. Expansion into additional underrepresented families then yielded 1,738 designs, with a further 40-row review. Final source-semantic deduplication and activity checks removed eight more, leaving 1,730. There are 65 distinct investigative mechanisms; the recipes are source-specific implementations, not 1,730 independent theories.

Each review selected eight highest-ranked, eight from the largest near-duplicate clusters, eight risky joins/legal/temporal cases, eight sparse mechanisms and eight seeded-random remaining. Samples are deliberately adversarial, not a statistical accuracy estimate. The same model session designed and reviewed the work; no independent reviewer is claimed. Review-selection-provenance.json preserves snapshot timing rather than pretending later rank updates preceded review.

The main corrections:

- Corporate officers, leaked relationship labels and GLEIF accounting parents do not establish ownership shares. Affected ownership paths were quarantined; governance-role questions have a separate mechanism.
- Generic funding-to-asset combinations supplied hundreds of tenuous pairs. The rule now requires an identified project-funding pathway and a compatible project/asset source. Merely having the same organization in two catalogs does not suffice.
- OONI censorship observations do not establish general outages. Both affected mechanism paths were removed.
- Country-year refugee/displacement totals cannot test a local incident's effects. Those paths were removed rather than described as precise spatial joins.
- Permit polygons and geological/site entries without operator identifiers cannot support corporate attribution. Those paths require an additional source and were quarantined.
- Toxic-release or generic enforcement fields do not establish a common methane measure. Methane comparisons retain only candidate GHG/industrial reporting paths and require the same gas, period, units and source attribution. MethaneSAT's finite historical catalog is explicit.
- A dam inventory is not a flood footprint; an earthquake is not a weather forecast target. Wrong-observation paths were removed.
- EITI ownership-to-licence routes duplicated the ownership mechanism and were collapsed. A vessel registry with no established activity event was removed from activity testing while its ownership question remained.
- ENAAT's presentation of EU arms reports is an alternate access route, not independent evidence. Mirror trade comparison must use different reporting sides, not the same EU observations redistributed twice.
- Building footprints are not population counts. Local geometry missing from source fields is explicitly a required bridge. No dose, death count, wrongdoing or causality is inferred from spatial overlap.

Validation distinguishes schema/provenance checks, synthetic inference regressions, sampled conceptual review and external record access. Only the first three were performed across the design process. Three bounded access probes returned two records and one explicit failure; they did not execute any investigative test. CISA HTTP 403 is retained as a failure; Global Fishing Watch registration/key requirements and changing GHGRP reporting scope are recorded.

The last automated suite checks exact Rabbit source hashes/counts, preserved fields/AP identifiers, unique semantic fingerprints, required reasoning fields, safe provisional-join scores and quarantine regressions. Synthetic fixtures cover name collisions, namespace and entity-type mismatch, many-to-many inflation, pre-designation and delisted dates, non-SDN restrictions, ownership versus control/accounting consolidation, minority interests, termination actions and authorizations. These tests do not certify external APIs, legal analysis or statistical inference.
