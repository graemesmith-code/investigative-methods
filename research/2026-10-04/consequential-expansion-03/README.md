### Q01 — Corporate donor eligibility at the actual donation date

refinement; priority rank 1; consequence 5/5, testability 2/5, join route 3/3, cost tier 2/4.

**If:** IF a reported accepted corporate donation identifies an exact company registration number, and independent evidence suggests one of the applicable corporate-donor criteria was not satisfied at receipt.

**Then:** THEN check the exact donor entity, incorporation/registration and actual UK business activity at receipt, alongside acceptance, returns and amendments, before describing an unresolved eligibility problem.

**Basis:** Electoral Commission Great Britain guidance lists registration, UK incorporation and UK business activity as separate criteria. This is a conditional eligibility test; historical statutory scope, recipient type and disposition must be verified. The attempted current statutory page failed to render, so no universal current-law determination is claimed.

**Join:** EC donation record and company reg. no. → exact Companies House company number and incorporation record → receipt/accepted/returned/dealt-with dates → activity evidence for that entity/period. Brand, officer, parent or overseas branch identity cannot silently replace the actual donor.

**Scope:** Great Britain party-donation route described by the cited guidance; other recipient types, Northern Ireland provisions and historical eligibility must be checked separately. The relevant date is receipt under applicable rules, not publication or today.

**Ordinary explanations / disqualifiers:** Wrong donor or company number; UK-incorporated subsidiary confused with overseas parent; Dormant accounts yet actual qualifying business; Nonprofit business activity; Returned/corrected donation; Different historical or recipient-specific rule; Registered overseas establishment confused with UK incorporation

**Required original records:** Original donation and disposition/amendments; Official incorporation/company record at receipt; Affirmative period-specific business evidence if testing activity; Applicable recipient/date rule and exceptions

**Next cheapest check:** Read the reported company number and returned/accepted fields first. A foreign-incorporated donor record may provide a more decisive eligibility question than dormant accounts. Do not treat dormant status alone as proof of no business.

**Novelty:** S06 already explicitly requires donor eligibility, incorporation evidence and refund checks. This is a useful non-sanctions implementation refinement of that predicate, not a new mechanism.

**Access evidence:** Official guidance and a public donation-detail template confirm relevant fields. No donation/company pair retrieved. Current statutory direct-page access failed; dated 2024 text and current regulator guidance are not a case-law conclusion.

### Q03 — An express sovereign guarantee disclosure versus the operative instrument

proposed_new; priority rank 2; consequence 5/5, testability 2/5, join route 2/3, cost tier 3/4.

**If:** IF a sovereign report expressly states that no specified guarantees exist, or supplies a demonstrably complete guarantee schedule for a defined perimeter/date, while an original executed instrument establishes a potentially in-scope guarantee.

**Then:** THEN establish enforceability, guarantor, covered liability, outstanding exposure and dates, and reconcile the exact instrument with all incorporated schedules and amendments before identifying an omission.

**Basis:** A material unaccounted contingent public liability can affect public finance and investors. No blanket guarantee-reporting duty is inferred from Form 18-K: its categories and exceptions matter. A contract commitment, public-enterprise borrowing or government ownership alone does not establish sovereign guaranteed debt.

**Join:** AidData contract/document ID → original loan/guarantee number, signing parties and guarantee clause or separate instrument → sovereign legal guarantor → EDGAR sovereign CIK/accession/report and exact disclosure perimeter/date → liability status and reporting currency. IMF country-series code supplies context, not a loan-level identity join.

**Scope:** Same government/perimeter, report as-of date and operative guarantee interval; distinguish central/general government, SOE own debt, guaranteed debt, direct debt and undrawn commitment. Guarantee and loan effectiveness, release, refinancing and amendments need evidence.

**Ordinary explanations / disqualifiers:** Letter of support rather than enforceable guarantee; Guarantor is a different public body; Guarantee not effective, expired or released; Liability repaid or outside stated reporting perimeter; Disclosed in incorporated annex; No disbursement where report counts only outstanding drawn debt; Valuation/currency or contingent/direct-debt distinction

**Required original records:** Original executed guarantee and referenced loan; Effectiveness/signature authority and amendments; Evidence of covered outstanding obligation at report date; Exact report assertion and all incorporated schedules; Perimeter/valuation rules

**Next cheapest check:** Read the precise no-guarantees assertion or schedule definition before looking for a loan match. Then read the original guarantee; stop if there is only a financing announcement or an unverified database classification.

**Novelty:** N01 reconciles aid reporting, E09 compares public funding with company accounts, and X14 compares copies of the same audited statement. This tests an explicit contingent-liability disclosure against a separate operative guarantee instrument.

**Access evidence:** AidData documents access to original contracts; the current SEC Form 18-K was read as a blank form, with OMB expiry January 31, 2029. No contract-report pair acquired. Its external-funded-debt item excludes intergovernmental debt; completeness cannot be presumed.

**Provenance:** SYS-0064 AidData datasets (references/rabbit/originals/Rabbit2(1).txt:4401; AP-0065); SYS-0005 SEC EDGAR (references/rabbit/originals/Rabbit2(1).txt:588; AP-0006, AP-1148, AP-1149, AP-1150, AP-1151, AP-1152, AP-1153, AP-1154, AP-1155, AP-1156, AP-1157, AP-1158, AP-1159, AP-1160, AP-1161, AP-1162, AP-1163, AP-1164, AP-1165, AP-1166, AP-1167, AP-1168, AP-1169, AP-1170, AP-1171, AP-1172); SYS-0154 IMF Data APIs (references/rabbit/originals/Rabbit2(1).txt:10071; AP-0155)

### REF-OUT — A unit availability statement versus its contemporaneous output

refinement; priority rank 3; consequence 5/5, testability 1/5, join route 3/3, cost tier 3/4.

**If:** IF a precise contemporaneous disclosure says a particular generation unit has zero available capacity over a stated interval and a quality-controlled series appears to report actual output from that same unit during it.

**Then:** THEN reconcile EIC hierarchy, measurement interval, statement version and outage revisions before describing an unresolved status/output contradiction.

**Basis:** An inaccurate availability disclosure can matter to market transparency, but positive generation does not by itself prove manipulation, profits or a reporting violation.

**Join:** Issuer statement and unit designation → verified unit-level EIC → production/generation-unit hierarchy → actual generation interval/value/quality and outage record/version. Country or plant aggregate must not substitute for the specified generating unit.

**Scope:** Common time zone, interval convention and contemporary statement revision; distinguish production unit, generating unit, MW available capacity and MWh/average-MW actual generation.

**Ordinary explanations / disqualifiers:** Other unit within same plant; Partial derating rather than zero availability; Testing or permitted trial operation; Stale/cancelled notice or revision; Timestamp/interval error; Gross/net or telemetry correction; Statement described future planned outage

**Required original records:** Original timestamped statement; Unit identity/hierarchy; Historical notice revisions; Quality-controlled unit output and unit semantics; Relevant duty before any legal claim

**Next cheapest check:** Confirm exact unit-level identity and whether the statement actually says zero. Defer if only plant totals or a current revised page are available.

**Novelty:** R14 status-versus-operation refinement. Publication-delay compliance and operator trading profits are separately deferred/rejected, not inferred from this check.

**Access evidence:** ENTSO-E current documentation describes unit and outage products and platform migration; historical export/API access remains credentialed or unverified. No data or credentials requested.

### Q02 — Charity assets transferred as a candidate-campaign contribution

proposed_new; priority rank 4; consequence 5/5, testability 2/5, join route 1/3, cost tier 3/4.

**If:** IF original records attribute a direct campaign contribution to a specifically identified organization that was subject to section 501(c)(3) at the transfer date, and identify the candidate campaign receiving it. Distinguish a completed transfer from one never completed or misattributed; a later refund does not erase the original completed event.

**Then:** THEN reconcile the organization’s actual asset transfer with the campaign receipt and any refund, verifying historical tax status, legal entities, authorization, purpose and applicable rules.

**Basis:** IRS guidance distinguishes prohibited candidate-campaign intervention from permitted nonpartisan activity. The question is actual organizational assets supporting a campaign, not whether staff hold political views. No inference is made from worship communications, employment, an affiliate’s spending or an ordinary charitable grant.

**Join:** Form 990 EIN and legal name → verified donor legal entity and historical tax status → original FEC receipt transaction/report and committee ID → underlying organizational transfer/purpose. FEC employer text is not donor identity; FEC committee IDs and EINs are not a shared namespace.

**Scope:** US organization and candidate-campaign transaction date, historical tax classification and actual recipient role. A current IRS BMF status is not historical proof. Distinguish campaign contribution from commercial payment, refund, conduit or personal funds.

**Ordinary explanations / disqualifiers:** Individual donor merely lists charity as employer; Separate 501(c)(4) affiliate or different entity; Misreported receipt or return/refund; Commercial payment misclassified as contribution; Tax status different at transfer; Nonpartisan activity or restricted charitable grant; Unauthorized personal funds rather than charity assets

**Required original records:** Actual organization-origin transfer and authorization/purpose; Original campaign receipt, amendments and refunds; Organization EIN/legal-entity bridge; Applicable historical exemption evidence; Committee identity/type and contemporaneous legal scope

**Next cheapest check:** Open the original receipt and determine whether the donor is the organization or an individual. Only then verify EIN, tax classification and organizational funding; stop on employer-only matches.

**Novelty:** X05 checks a charity’s legislative-lobbying declaration. This tests a different underlying transaction and candidate-campaign asset restriction, without requiring a contradictory Form 990 answer. E08 related-party overlap alone does not establish this conduct.

**Access evidence:** IRS and FEC documentation inspected; no organization or transaction searched. Historical exemption evidence/underlying transfer can require additional records. IRS historical status/determination evidence is a supporting route, explicitly outside the Form 990 file alone.

**Provenance:** SYS-0045 IRS Form 990 bulk filings (references/rabbit/originals/Rabbit2(1).txt:2712; AP-0046); SYS-0116 OpenFEC API (references/rabbit/originals/Rabbit2(1).txt:7417; AP-0117, AP-1173, AP-1174, AP-1175, AP-1176, AP-1177, AP-1178, AP-1179, AP-1180, AP-1181, AP-1182, AP-1183, AP-1184, AP-1185, AP-1186, AP-1187, AP-1188, AP-1189, AP-1190, AP-1191, AP-1192, AP-1193, AP-1194, AP-1195, AP-1196, AP-1197)

refinement; priority rank 5; consequence 5/5, testability 1/5, join route 2/3, cost tier 3/4.

**Then:** THEN compare closing/effectiveness with the actual consent or valid deemed-consent process after resolving carve-outs, waivers and amendments.

**Basis:** The test concerns compliance with a particular prerequisite. An investor-name change or lack of a consent document in one archive does not establish an unauthorized transfer.

**Join:** Original contract/right/permit → exact covered legal entity and transaction definition → issuer filing/exhibit with closing date → consent authority/instrument and notice receipt chronology. Land Matrix deal ID is only a lead, not a contract-number crosswalk or closing proof.

**Scope:** Contract and amendments in force at actual legal transfer, with governing law, notice/deemed-consent periods and intra-group exemptions.

**Ordinary explanations / disqualifiers:** Share sale not covered assignment; Intra-group exemption; Condition precedent delays effectiveness; Consent in undiscovered amendment; Valid deemed consent; Transaction announced but never closed; Different licence or parcel

**Required original records:** Original contract and operative amendments; Executed transfer/closing evidence; Consent or deemed-consent notification records; Exact licence/party bridge

**Next cheapest check:** Read the full assignment/change-control clause before matching deal names. Reject the candidate if the transaction is outside its definition.

### REF-INS — A specific insurance risk answer versus contemporaneous site evidence
