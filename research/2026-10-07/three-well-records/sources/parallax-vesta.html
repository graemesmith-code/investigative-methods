Vesta Energy is Now Parallax Energy

Please be advised that Vesta Energy Ltd. has amalgamated with Parallax Energy Operating Inc., effective December 4, 2024, in accordance with the provisions of the Business Corporations Act (Alberta). The amalgamated corporation will continue under the name PARALLAX ENERGY OPERATING INC.

As a result of the foregoing, effective December 4, 2024, all notices, correspondence, invoices or payments formerly addressed to Vesta Energy Ltd. should now be issued in the name of Parallax Energy Operating Inc. Please update your records to the following mailing address:

Parallax Energy Operating Inc.
900, 520 – 3rd Avenue SW
Calgary, Alberta, T2P 0R3, Canada

Please circulate this notice to all departments within your organization and amend your records accordingly. Our business administration number and GST registration number remain unchanged. Should you have any questions regarding this Notice, or require any additional information, please contact our office at the address noted.
