## Exact records and why they matter

1. **Daily completion tour reports and service-company fracture job reports**, including all amendments, attachments and treatment/pressure/rate charts. Require stage IDs, well/UWI mapping, treatment dates, start/end/stop/restart timestamps, timezones/clock basis, pump rates/pressures and any recorded shutdown reason. Daily totals alone may still be insufficient to resolve the event instant. The downloaded Directive059 FAQ, bearing a March2018 date, identifies both service-company job reports and daily completion tour reports; it does not prove any particular report was submitted or included in the paid package.
2. **Perforation/treatment and completion-interval records**, stage top/base measured and true-vertical depths with units/reference datum, geological markers, and as-drilled directional/trajectory surveys. These distinguish actual stimulated formation and distance from licensed/planned Duvernay. Include corrections connecting the October2025 UWI/bottomhole updates to earlier submissions. Directional surveys/logs are not included in the standard tour catalogue description.
3. **Historical well-licence/licensee records**, including BA codes, effective transfer/amalgamation dates, status changes, and all old/new UWI or drilling-event relationships. Original Vesta licences plus a corporate successor notice do not alone establish the full well-specific history or operational contractor.
4. **Contemporaneous seismic reports and regulator response records**, including initial and revised magnitudes and magnitude types, event location/uncertainty, monitoring-station list, operator detection time, notification/call timestamps, incident reference, instructions to stop and acknowledged stop time. Current catalogue values and waveform availability are not proof of what an operator knew when.
5. **Conditional restart documents**: if a qualifying shutdown and subsequent restart occurred, written AER consent identifying the affected wells, issue/effective timestamp, conditions, the modified response/mitigation plan and its acceptance, and any record of actual restart. Also request any record identifying why a reported event did not trigger a shutdown for these operations. The request must not presume a violation or an unauthorized restart.
