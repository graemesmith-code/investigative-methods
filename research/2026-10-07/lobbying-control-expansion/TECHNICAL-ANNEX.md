## Legal and temporal rules

The [2024 edition of 2 USC 1603(b)(4)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title2/html/USCODE-2024-title2-chap26-sec1603.htm) supplies the foreign-entity identification rule used for the 2025 registrations: at least 20% equitable ownership, the activity-control alternative, or the interested-affiliate alternative. The contribution amount field does not make funding a prerequisite for the equity alternative. The website exception in the concluding text concerns paragraph (3)(B); it is not a general exemption for publicly known foreign parents. An LD-1 No response is distinct from an empty LD-2 additions field.

The [inflation-adjusted thresholds effective 1 January 2025](https://www.senate.gov/legislative/Public_Disclosure/new_thresholds.htm) were $3,500 quarterly income for outside firms and $16,000 quarterly in-house expenses, subject to the underlying lobbying/contact/time definitions. Earlier 2021–24 thresholds were $3,000/$14,000. The [2025 joint guidance](https://lda.senate.gov/ldaguidance.pdf) and [2 USC 1604](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title2/html/USCODE-2024-title2-chap26-sec1604.htm) govern changes and quarterly reporting. Historical registrations are used for chronology, not retroactive application of 2025 thresholds.

The [2025 annual CFR, 28 CFR Part 5](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title28-vol1/html/CFR-2025-title28-vol1-part5.htm), sections 5.201, 5.304 and 5.307, and [DOJ's FARA FAQ](https://www.justice.gov/nsd-fara/frequently-asked-questions) supply the exhibit and exemption framework. Commercial and LDA exemptions are activity-specific; foreign government/party principal-beneficiary questions are not answered by nationality alone. Section 5.201(b), not 5.208, addresses exhibit-change timing. Proposed regulations are not treated as enacted rules. The first checkpoint archives the primary legal texts and fuller temporal matrix. This research makes no liability determination.

## Reproduction and source handling

Run scripts from this directory's `scripts` folder or from the repository root with their full paths. `acquire.py` uses TLS verification, public URLs, a bounded download size, hashes, timestamps and immutable local cache files. LDA API consumers must run serially at a minimum 4.3 seconds between calls; original-form downloads are separately limited to four workers. `annual_histories.py` and `name_histories.py` pause/resume the RR scanner to avoid aggregate rate violations. Do not run multiple API scripts concurrently without that serialization.

Offline derivation: run `screen.py`, `loose_join.py`, `reconcile.py`, `verify_amounts.py`, then `finalize.py` and `package.py`.
