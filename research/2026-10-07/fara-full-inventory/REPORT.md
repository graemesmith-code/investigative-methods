# Foreign control and lobbying: what the records support

Private investigative pilot · 7 October 2026 · Final synthesis, superseding the checkpoint

The expanded search did **not establish that foreign control behind a major campaign was concealed**. It produced several reporting leads, substantial counterevidence and a reusable way to distinguish them. The strongest remaining major-campaign question concerns TransCanada PipeLines' historical registration. Its FARA relationship ended decades earlier, so it is not a contemporaneous conflict between the two regimes. The strongest recent cross-regime lead, Stark Aerospace, falls below this pilot's major-campaign threshold.

An important correction: Honda and Novartis quarterly filings expressly identify foreign parents. Earlier screening concentrated on registration checkboxes and missed those narratives. Their current campaigns should not be described as hiding foreign ownership. This correction is the most consequential result of the expanded audit.

## Ranked reporting opportunities

These ranks reflect the value of further document-based reporting, **not a probability of wrongdoing**. They are analyst judgments informed by the original records. Jev's legal rankings did not pass validation and are withheld. No case establishes intent.

## Actual coverage and materiality

The supplied DOJ inventories contain 7,090 registrant rows, 17,765 principal relationships and 154,309 document rows, with 126,480 distinct PDF URLs. Inventory rows are discovery records, not 154,309 documents read. The matched House source is the complete set of five official 2025 report-year XML archives: **109,389 records**, comprising 101,537 quarterly documents and 7,852 registrations/amendments. The quarterly records represent 97,567 relationship-quarters plus 3,970 additional versions. All carry report year 2025, but signing dates run through 2 October 2026. Signing is not filing receipt; this is not a census of documents received during 2025.

Client-name matching generated 598 discovery edges involving 222 House relationships; only 29 relationships had a principal-period overlap with 2025. The additional foreign-entity, affiliate and narrative screen generated 576 edges involving 149 relationships. Their union is **343 relationships**, of which **25** met the numeric major threshold. All 25 received original-based materiality and disclosure review; 318 did not qualify on acquired numeric data and are not cleared of disclosure concerns. This is a bounded review of discovered relationships, not exhaustive resolution of every corporate alias or all US lobbying.

“Major” means at least $1 million in the sum of selected 2025 quarterly income **or** in-house expenses for a registrant–client relationship after amendments. Historical leads use their explicitly named year. Income and expenses are not combined, nor are overlapping outside fees added to in-house spending. These figures measure reported lobbying scale, not foreign funding. Unknown/below-threshold values are not certified zeros. Across the major review and Gotion comparisons, 122 distinct 2025 original amount checks agreed with API amounts and posted-date amendment ordering. Three additional historical annual/partial-year checks are separately preserved.

The combined acquired original-form audit contains 4,143 distinct LDA documents: 3,658 HTML forms parsed without structural exceptions and 485 image/PDF forms on which automated narrative extraction abstained. There are 1,045 HTML forms with nonempty foreign-interest narratives. Five selected image pages were visually adjudicated; OCR remains a search aid. A Hyundai image demonstrated that an API foreign-entity array could actually reflect an affiliate section, so API role labels are not treated as original evidence.

In this continuation, eight modern DOJ PDF URLs were recognized by the browser reader, with varying excerpt coverage; ten selected historical Exhibits remained inaccessible. These counts do not imply full PDF downloads or complete page review. Direct DOJ downloads and the original attached guide transfer were blocked. The guide's matching repository card and the user's quoted item 10 supplied the task definition. None of the access failures was treated as an absent filing.

## Rules applied at the relevant time

The [2010 version of 2 USC 1603](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2010-title2/html/USCODE-2010-title2-chap26-sec1603.htm) required registration within 45 days and identified foreign entities through at least 20% ownership, specified control or planning of lobbying activity, or qualifying affiliate interests. A foreign owner's zero contribution does not remove an otherwise applicable ownership/control condition. The inflation-adjusted thresholds for 2009–2012 were $3,000 in outside quarterly income and $11,500 in in-house expenses, documented in [GAO-10-499, physical page 8](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-10-499.pdf) and [GAO-12-492, physical page 9](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-12-492.pdf). The unadjusted statute dollar amounts are not substituted for those thresholds.

The saved February 2025 House/Senate guidance supplies the contemporary thresholds: $3,500 outside income and $16,000 in-house expenses; the prior 2021–2024 values were $3,000 and $14,000. Registration thresholds and the separate 20% lobbying-time definition are not inferred solely from campaign size. Quarterly amendment duties and the particular form's instructions must be applied to the actual registrant and activity.

FARA is activity- and relationship-dependent. Foreign corporate ownership alone does not establish a FARA obligation. The commercial exemption and the LDA exemption have different conditions; foreign-government/political-party principal-beneficiary issues require separate examination. FARA Exhibit A changes are governed by 28 CFR 5.201(b)'s ten-day provision; 5.208 is not the correct general citation for that claim. No database mismatch, later acquisition or move between regimes establishes concealment. The preceding-stage legal annex preserves the primary statutes, regulations, guidance and their retrieval records.
