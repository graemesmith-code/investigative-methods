## Investigation priority, not a legal risk score

1. **TransCanada PipeLines Limited: a historical No response and contemporary parent disclosure.** Its [original registration, section 14](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/c7bf0737-bf48-4517-83ad-48cf98787b3f/print/), filed 16 December 2010 and effective 5 November, answers No. It expressly gives a Calgary, Canada address and describes US/Canada natural-gas and climate-legislation lobbying, so Canadian identity is not hidden. The issuer's [2009 annual information form](https://www.tcenergy.com/siteassets/pdfs/investors/shareholders/historical-information/tcpl-regulatory-filings/2009/tcpl-2009-annual-information-form.pdf), physical pages 4 and 22, identifies the same address, the 2003 arrangement and TransCanada Corporation's ownership of all common shares. Its [2010 annual financial report](https://www.tcenergy.com/siteassets/pdfs/investors/shareholders/historical-information/tcpl-financial-statements/2010/tcpl-2010-annual-financial-report.pdf), physical page 96, Note 1, calls TCPL a wholly owned subsidiary of that parent. These bracket the registration, but are not a certified daily share register. The 2025 relationship reports $2.72 million in expenses; that establishes present cohort materiality, not the size of the 2010 campaign. The strongest innocent explanation is filing interpretation/error or a separate disclosure not yet reconciled, especially given the openly Canadian registrant. The decisive missing records are the contemporaneous complete registration context, ownership effective at the filing, and the inaccessible [historical FARA Exhibit](https://efile.fara.gov/docs/3076-Exhibit-AB-19811101-D0K92502.pdf). This is a sourced inconsistency for review, not intentional concealment.

2. **Honda: exact employer identity and a historical amendment need reconciliation.** The current API label is American Honda Motor Co., Inc.; the [20 April 2012 original amendment](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/2d5100df-3cf8-4403-a8cf-b246f9489d57/print/) actually names Honda North America and answers No at section 14. It describes greenhouse-gas/CARB work. Honda Motor Co., Ltd.'s [2012 Form 20-F, printed page 25, Organizational Structure](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/715153/000119312512277747/d231149d20f.htm) lists Honda North America, Inc. and American Honda Motor Co., Inc. separately, each at 100% ownership/voting interest as of 31 March 2012. This supports a parent-ownership question but does not license substituting one subsidiary for another. The current relationship reports $3,763,079 in 2025 expenses. Its FARA name match refers to American Honda in 1990, not necessarily the 2012 printed client. Strong alternative: amendments, employer changes or separate registrations explain the apparent inconsistency. Missing: the initial registration, exact legal-entity/employer continuity, contemporary amendment instructions and an original historical FARA filing. Do not call the 2012 document a 2025 nondisclosure.

3. **Novartis: historical No responses, unresolved exact legal client.** The [2003 amendment](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/385f8307-470a-4d16-ab4c-e13ca730376a/print/), physical page 3, visually checks No; the [2011 amendment](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/304479ad-e757-465f-96c9-78ff1bd4b9d1/print/) does likewise. Current relationship expenses are $7.788 million. A different legal-client identity is the strongest innocent explanation. Missing: original initial registration, exact employing entity and the inaccessible 1997 Ruder Finn Exhibit A. Remains an identity lead, not a documented ownership omission.

4. **Gotion: major activity, but explicit foreign-ownership disclosure.** The [in-house registration](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/8061d143-bb77-4069-9be5-67336c3f1f76/print/), [Mercury registration](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/5e15761c-3ffc-4b93-af85-b0406f036c68/print/) and [Vogel registration](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/34c85c11-abda-41da-8159-4c49d9af2a9e/print/) all answer Yes and name a Chinese parent at 100%.
