These are the original discovery and audit utilities, preserved without rewriting their historical path assumptions. They expected `research/raw`, `output/starter-kit/sources`, `output/comparability-audit` and the report layout in the original working directory. They are not runnable from this relocated directory without recreating that layout and installing their dependencies.
