This archive saves the research, evidence locations and checked extracts. It does not bundle the full third-party report collection. Public source documents can be retrieved using the new downloader; a changed or missing edition requires review. Historical discovery manifests retain their original path fields and must not be interpreted as an inventory of files bundled here.

The four observed agreements do not independently verify either company's underlying facts. The host-country EITI records are company-attributed disclosures in national transparency processes, not uniformly original tax returns. No interviews or regulator contacts were used to resolve the parked cases.
