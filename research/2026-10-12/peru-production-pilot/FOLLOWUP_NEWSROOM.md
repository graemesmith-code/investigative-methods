# Peru mining disclosures: follow-up for a newsroom editor

12 October 2026. Read alongside the original [100-record newsroom brief](NEWSROOM_BRIEF.md).

## Summary for an editor

We tested whether a company could be caught reporting two incompatible production figures by comparing public documents in English and Spanish. The original sample contained 100 annual records, including 35 gold records. This follow-up examined the four recurring leads more closely.

**We have not established a false company statement.** We found a stronger example of the method working: Nexa's English SEC filings and Spanish annual reports on Peru's securities regulator's website give exactly the same quantities for five metals, including gold, for two sampled years. Both Spanish source reports are explicitly preliminary versions, pending shareholder approval. Other English/Spanish company comparisons also agree at their published precision.

The four numerical leads remain differences between company reports and Peru's mining ministry's compilation. Public documents do not yet establish that these sources count precisely the same metal at the same processing stage and cutoff. We have parked those cases rather than label the differences fraud.

**Editorial recommendation:** retain the evidence, automate the SEC–Peru securities-report comparisons with explicit version status, and resume the parked cases only when a new public original document closes the specific definition gap. Cheaper AI alone will not supply a missing filing or assay reconciliation.

## What changed for the four leads?

| Operation | New check | Current decision |
|---|---|---|
| La Arena, gold | Quarterly 2023 comparisons show differences throughout the year, with the ministry higher in Q1 and lower in Q2–Q4. Spanish news coverage already repeated the company's annual quantity. | Park. No explicit bridge between the two sources' production, assay and cutoff definitions. |
| Shahuindo, gold | The ministry is higher in all four 2023 quarters. The technical report describes doré testing and overseas refining, but does not reconcile the annual numbers. Spanish news coverage repeated the company's quantity. | Park. The gap is real arithmetic, but not an established incompatible claim. |
| Antamina, copper/zinc | The operator's own English and Spanish reports agree. Its quantities differ from both Teck's and the ministry's. Different concentrate products may explain some of the gap. | Park. We lack the actual product-by-product assay bridge. |
| Cerro Lindo, Nexa, zinc | Five years of company language comparisons agree at published precision. SEC and Peru securities-regulator documents agree exactly for five metals in 2021 and 2023. | Use as a clean comparison example. Park the separate ministry gap. |

For the two gold mines, 2019 is not a full-year comparison: Pan American's figures begin with its February acquisition. Its La Arena 2024 output also stops before December following the sale. Neither belongs in an unadjusted calendar-year test.

In 2023, the original diagnostic ministry-minus-company gold differences were about **−39.4 kg at La Arena** and **+86.3 kg at Shahuindo**. Their opposite directions and their small combined total do not explain Peru's national gold-export gap. Neither is proof of incorrect reporting.

## What does the CooperAcción reporting mean for this experiment?

The linked [CooperAcción article](https://cooperaccion.org.pe/la-brecha-entre-la-produccion-y-la-exportacion-de-oro-en-el-peru/), published 11 May 2026, describes a national production/export gap exceeding 100 tonnes for 2025 and associates the gap with informal and illegal mining. It motivates investigating gold. It does not identify these companies as responsible or establish matching company-level production and exports. Our frozen production sample covers 2019–2023, not the article's latest year.

## Could exports identify a company that misstated output?

Peru's free customs service returned named La Arena export declarations. However, the retrieved response does not identify a matching foreign consignee, and the description, weights and values are not reconciled into reliable fine-gold quantities. The tested Canadian and British public sources did not provide the same named shipment's quantity. China access failed in the original test.

We have no matched company-level export/import pair. The trade case is parked. Exports can also include inventory or purchased material, so a shipment match would still need a separate bridge to mine production.

## Has someone already reported the story?

See [MEDIA_NOVELTY.md](MEDIA_NOVELTY.md) for specific articles. Environmental disputes, community conflicts and company operating results have already been reported. We did not locate an investigation of our exact production-number pairings. That bounded search result does not establish novelty. Crucially, the company quantities already circulate in Spanish, and ministry quantities also appear in English industry reporting. The evidence does not support a simple claim that language concealed these numbers.

## What can scale now?

The free document acquisition and arithmetic work. We tested a Nexa document registry returning 45 document entries across six year buckets and packaged a reusable client. The difficult part is proving that a pair means the same thing and preserving the original submission and corrections.

This follow-up's 43 control checks cover 33 distinct operation/metal/year combinations: some compare the same quantity through an additional source. They are not 43 independent experiments or discoveries. No token-cost benchmark, detection accuracy or unattended system throughput has been established.
