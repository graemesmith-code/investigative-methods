# Peru production pilot: fixed protocol

Question: can company production statements in English be compared cheaply and reliably with Spanish-language declarations compiled by Peru's mining ministry?

The fixed batch contains 100 operator–metal–year records, including 35 gold records, across 11 operator groups and 2019–2023. Twenty 2023 records are used to develop the extraction and comparison rules; the remaining 80 test reuse in earlier years. This is a purposive feasibility sample, not a random prevalence study or a statistically blind test. Southern Copper's 2023 molybdenum value was already inspected in the prior audit. Records with absent data or unresolved meaning remain in the denominator.

A candidate requires the same company/mine population, full or attributable ownership basis, calendar period, substance, output stage and source version. Convert pounds or troy ounces exactly; allow one displayed reporting unit for each rounded component unless the document proves a narrower convention. A numerical gap does not establish a company contradiction until attribution, revisions and definitions survive review. Ministry compilation errors remain an alternative until company-submitted values are established.

Do not compare national production/export totals to identify a company. Pursue trade records only if exporter/importer and product/quantity/period can be matched at company level; genuine shipment matching is preferable. Gross shipping weight is not fine metal content. Production is not automatically equal to exports because stocks, purchases and processing can differ.

Review every numerical flag and a deterministic random sample of apparent agreements. Add artificial differences and scope errors to test the comparison rules. Save blocked cases, reasons, reviewed evidence locations, source retrieval/fingerprints and any rule changes. Report elapsed work and any unmeasured manual effort without inventing cost or time savings.

Continuation target: at least 80 of 100 cases support a reusable, defensible comparison with low subsequent review effort. This target is an engineering decision, not evidence of statistical validity. A reliable batch with no contradictions passes feasibility but leaves investigative yield unproven. No paid model calls or outreach are authorized or required for this pilot.
