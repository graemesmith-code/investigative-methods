# Existing coverage and novelty check — 12 October 2026

## Finding

A bounded search of English and Spanish reporting did not locate a published comparison identifying our exact recurring company-versus-MINEM production differences for La Arena, Shahuindo, Antamina or Cerro Lindo. This is evidence of a possible reporting gap, not proof of originality. Paywalled, poorly indexed and local archives may contain relevant work. None of these four discrepancies has yet passed the pilot's strict same-assertion/provenance gates.

Existing coverage establishes extensive scrutiny of the mines, particularly environmental and community issues. It does not substantiate a production-misstatement allegation. A new story would need a verified incompatible quantity, its reporting provenance and its consequence, rather than a repetition of existing controversy.

## La Arena — gold, La Libertad

The pilot found Peru's figures lower than Pan American's full-year disclosures in every year from 2020 to 2023; the 2023 difference is about 39.4 kg. Existing coverage includes:

- [CooperAcción, 25 September 2018](https://cooperaccion.org.pe/derrame-de-cianuro-en-la-libertad-boletin-amp-231-setiembre-2018/): official reporting of a 600 cubic metre cyanide-solution spill at the then-Tahoe operation.
- [RPP, 17 October 2018](https://rpp.pe/peru/la-libertad/denuncian-contaminacion-de-piscigranjas-con-cianuro-y-muerte-de-truchas-noticia-1157327): residents alleged contamination and fish deaths and sought compensation. These are reported allegations, not an independent attribution finding in our audit.
- [Reuters, 28 April 2026, syndicated by Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/commodities/articles/chinas-zijin-plans-1-5-205434788.html): Zijin's proposed $1.5 billion expansion and its acquisition of the mine at the end of 2024.

Novelty assessment: environmental controversy and the Chinese purchase are already covered. No located article identifies our four-year production discrepancy. Historical comparisons concern Pan American's ownership period; the later Zijin transaction supplies context and does not transfer responsibility for historical representations. Leached/poured/refined gold and assay/cutoff definitions remain the main explanation to test.

## Shahuindo — gold, Cajamarca

The pilot found Peru's figures higher than the company's disclosure in all four full years; about 86.3 kg higher in 2023. That direction cannot by itself support under-reporting to Peru.

- [Mongabay, March 2020](https://es.mongabay.com/2020/03/mapa-conflictos-mineras-america-latina/): reporting on a Latin American mining-conflict map includes Shahuindo and environmental concerns. Search-indexed article content was available, but direct page retrieval failed in this check.
- [Tahoe statement, 19 January 2018](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/tahoe-corrects-inaccurate-reports-regarding-shahuindo-mine-300585349.html): the then-owner denied reports that rainfall-related overflow had contaminated process water. This is a company response, not independent journalism or a final regulator determination.
- [ProActivo, 12 December 2024](https://proactivo.com.pe/shahuindo-contratos-explotacion-mineria-artesanal/): agreements with two members of the AMASBA artisanal-miner association to operate within a defined area. The article is an industry account of a public signing, not an independent investigation of gold origin.

Novelty assessment: conflict, water concerns and nearby artisanal mining are already public. No located story compares these annual production figures. The 2024 artisanal agreements do not explain a 2020–2023 discrepancy or prove that artisanal output was included in Shahuindo's production. A claim about purchased or mixed gold needs direct evidence of the physical flow and reporting scope.

## Antamina — copper/zinc, Áncash

All five sampled years differ between MINEM and Teck's stated 100% quantities. For 2023 zinc, the diagnostic figures are approximately 527,979 tonnes versus 463,100 tonnes. Exact coverage of byproduct metal across concentrate streams is unresolved.

- [The Guardian, 4 February 2025](https://www.theguardian.com/global-development/2025/feb/04/the-last-drops-of-our-water-how-a-mine-left-some-of-perus-poorest-high-and-dry): investigated water shortages, arsenic exposure and disputed environmental impacts around Antamina.
- [Convoca, 18 August 2023](https://convoca.pe/agenda-propia/ancash-antamina-decide-recortar-inversiones-en-obras-por-impuestos-y-se-niega): investigated the dispute over commitments to fund a Huaraz hospital through the public works for taxes mechanism, including the company's position.
- [Convoca's financial-transparency investigation](https://ilusionfiscal.convoca.pe/como-se-oculto-el-movimiento-financiero-de-mas-de-4-mil-empresas-en-peru.php): names Antamina while describing the removal of an audited-financial-disclosure obligation affecting large unlisted companies. This demonstrates prior reporting on disclosure opacity, not an identified production falsity.

Novelty assessment: a broad environmental, transparency or unfulfilled-community-promises story would overlap with substantial prior journalism. No located article reconciles or alleges falsity in our specific production pairing. Product definitions could still dissolve this lead. Cross-language deception is not established.

## Cerro Lindo — zinc, Ica; Nexa, historically Milpo

The pilot found Peru's zinc totals 4.66%–8.31% higher across the five sampled years; about 84,710 tonnes versus 78,209 tonnes in 2023.

- [Reuters, 14 December 2021, syndicated by Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/miner-nexa-suspends-production-in-peruvian-zinc-mine-due-to-road-blockade): reported a temporary suspension following a local road blockade. Reuters also reported the restart on 16 December. These events explain output interruption, not a disagreement between two full-year quantities for the same output.
- [Rumbo Minero, production/operating review](https://www.rumbominero.com/peru/nexa-mina-cerro-lindo-planes/): covers the 2023 operating performance and future production guidance. Search-indexed content was accessible; direct retrieval returned HTTP 403.
- [Mining Technology/GlobalData production ranking](https://www.mining-technology.com/marketdata/five-largest-zinc-mines-peru/): already reports an estimated 78.2 thousand tonnes in 2023. Direct page retrieval failed; search-indexed content was available. This is publication of one number, not a check against MINEM. Its other mine estimates should not be assumed independently verified.
- **New language control:** [Nexa's Spanish production table](https://www.nexaresources.com/es/indicadores/producao-de-minerios-metalicos-e-produtos-metalicos-acabados/) explicitly shows Cerro Lindo zinc at **78.2 thousand tonnes in 2023**, consistent with the rounded English company figure. The page is live and includes later years; its old template date is not a reliable historical publication date for the current table.

Novelty assessment: the company's lower quantity is public in Spanish as well as English. This materially weakens the theory that a difference is hidden behind language in this case. The ministry-versus-company numerical gap still exists and was not explained by the reporting located. Concentrate-product coverage remains the strongest public-document question.

## Editorial decision

No case was eliminated by finding a previous investigation of the exact comparison. All four still require semantic/evidence work before becoming publishable findings. Prior scandals are useful context but provide no evidence that a current numerical difference is false reporting. The meaningful possible novelty is a documented inconsistency in a precisely defined quantity, with original submission and amendment provenance.

This check used both available web search engines, English and Spanish mine/company names, production-discrepancy and under-reporting terminology, selected exact quantities, and site searches for OjoPúblico, Convoca, Reuters and mining outlets. It included historical names Tahoe/Rio Alto for the gold operations and Milpo for Nexa where relevant to background. It was not an exhaustive paid media-archive search. Some searches returned unrelated entities with similar names; those were excluded. No inference that absence of a search result proves absence of coverage is made.

## Follow-up: quantities already cross the language boundary

Further primary-document work and media checks materially weaken the language-concealment premise for all four leads:

- [Gestión, 17 January 2024](https://gestion.pe/economia/empresas/pan-american-planea-invertir-hasta-us-31-millones-en-mina-huaron-el-2024-mineria-pasco-plata-empresas-canada-noticia/) reports Pan American's preliminary 2023 output in Spanish: 140,100 oz Shahuindo and 97,100 oz La Arena. This is reporting of the company quantities, not a ministry reconciliation.
- [Global Business Reports, Peru production 2023](https://www.gbreports.com/contents/peruproduction2023/) has search-indexed English content repeating ministry-based Antamina zinc 527,979 t and Nexa 84,710 t. Direct retrieval failed in this follow-up; indexed contents are not independently validated company submissions.
- Antamina's own English and Spanish reports agree for the eight Cu/Zn controls reviewed. Nexa's bilingual disclosures agree for 25 metal-year controls, and exact SEC–SMV agreement was established for five metals in two years.

These findings do not show that the ministry differences have been reported as a mismatch. They show that a numerical series is not confined to one language or audience. A future story would need to establish incompatible definitions and submission provenance before arguing false disclosure. See [FOLLOWUP_TECHNICAL.md](FOLLOWUP_TECHNICAL.md).
