# Prior-reporting and media corpus

**Scope for this investigation:** internationally directed US national-security capabilities across AI, quantum, drones/autonomy, cyber, electronic warfare, space, sensing, communications and enabling technologies. The inventory below is deliberately broader than the final inclusion boundary. Domestic-only policing, administrative AI and unrelated civilian projects are peripheral or out of scope unless a documented international/overseas security mission connects them. Source availability is not a decision to include its whole dataset.

Checked 16 October 2026. This layer establishes what has already been publicly described. It is separate from evidence that a government actually committed money. Publisher pages and documentation were checked; no subscription login, authenticated archive query or bulk news-content download was performed.

## 1. Defense and space trade reporting

Official publisher entry points: [Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/), [Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/), [C4ISRNET](https://www.c4isrnet.com/), [Defense One](https://www.defenseone.com/) and [SpaceNews](https://spacenews.com/). The first four homepages were retrieved. SpaceNews' [newsletter page](https://spacenews.com/newsletters-2/) and [academic subscriptions page](https://spacenews.com/academic-subscriptions/) were search-indexed; its homepage was not retrievable in this tool.

The unit is an article, interview, newsletter item or published investigation. Retain original URL, title, publisher, byline, publication/update date, mentioned program/company and any award number. These are important named outlets for defense technology and space coverage; no complete historical export, unrestricted full-text feed or uniform archive start date was verified. Separate original reporting, opinion, sponsored content and republished press releases. Shared ownership or syndication can create duplicate articles.

## 2. Procurement, general and local reporting

[GovExec](https://www.govexec.com/) and [FedScoop](https://fedscoop.com/) homepages were retrieved. They add procurement, government IT and agency-use reporting that may be absent from weapons-focused outlets. General-interest investigations, newswires and local reporting near company offices, laboratories and military installations are also necessary coverage categories, but no complete all-outlet corpus was identified in this pass. This is a source-selection gap, not a verified searchable national/local archive.

Use article URL, publication date, program aliases and company/legal-entity names as join candidates. Article text often lacks government identifiers. Search-engine results are discovery indexes with changing coverage, ranking and historical visibility, not a reproducible census of prior reporting. A story about a company may already disclose the investment without using the agency's later program name.

## 3. Media Cloud

Official [documentation](https://www.mediacloud.org/documentation), [FAQ](https://www.mediacloud.org/documentation/faqs), [source guide](https://www.mediacloud.org/documentation/source-guide) and [search guide](https://www.mediacloud.org/documentation/search-tool-guide) describe searchable news sources/collections, dates and keyword queries, with CSV exports of matching article URLs and aggregate results. Source metadata includes domain, source ID and first story collected.

Documentation was retrieved or search-indexed; a logged-in query/API response was not tested. Coverage depends on tracked sources and when collection began, primarily through RSS and sitemaps. Paywalled sources may contribute only titles/summaries. The service explicitly does not provide article full text for download because of copyright. Quotas apply. Treat this as a potentially repeatable discovery/metadata layer whose source and time coverage must be inspected before use.

## 4. GDELT

The official [data and documentation index](https://gdeltproject.org/data.html) was retrieved. It documents raw event/knowledge-graph datasets and live APIs, including article discovery. Article URLs, times, source domains and extracted entities/themes can connect coverage to program/company names; an extracted event is not the same unit as an article or government award.

No API query or bulk download was tested. Historic [DOC API documentation](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/gdelt-doc-2-0-api-debuts//) and [2018 update](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/doc-2-0-updates-1-5-year-searching-and-updated-mobile-interface/) describe different date-window behavior by output mode. Current usable date ranges and result caps require an endpoint test; do not promise full historical article retrieval from these old pages. Extracted entities, translation and changing source coverage introduce errors. Metadata discovery does not confer rights to republish publishers' full text.

## 5. Licensed news archives: Nexis and Factiva

[Nexis' official product page](https://www.lexisnexis.com/en-us/products/nexis.page), [journalist offering](https://www.lexisnexis.com/en-us/professional/research/media.page) and [news-search documentation](https://supportcenter.lexisnexis.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/1099993/~/how-do-i-search-news-on-nexis%3F) describe licensed historical news, source selection and searchable text. Product documentation was retrieved/search-indexed. No account, subscription or API entitlement was checked.

[Factiva's official service entry](https://www.dowjones.com/professional/factiva/) was not retrievable through this tool. Its [official terms](https://global.factiva.com/FactivaLogin/tou/default.aspx?fcpil=en) were search-indexed, and the [ProQuest distributor guide](https://proquest.libguides.com/factiva/about) documents the product. Treat it as a licensed candidate, not access already available to this project. Exact archive, export, machine-processing and reuse rights depend on the active agreement. The older indexed terms are not a substitute for that agreement.

For either service, the unit is an indexed licensed article/document. Publisher, date, title, document identifier and entity/program mentions are useful keys. Source availability and date coverage differ by subscription and outlet. A user-interface subscription does not establish bulk/API or AI-processing permission. No copyrighted corpus was copied in this inventory.

## What an absence means

“No match in the searched sources, dates and aliases” is supportable after a logged search. “Previously unreported” requires a broader editorial check and remains a qualified judgment. A previously reported contract can still belong to an original, previously undescribed spending pattern. Record those two kinds of novelty separately when analysis begins; this inventory makes no novelty claim about any investment.
