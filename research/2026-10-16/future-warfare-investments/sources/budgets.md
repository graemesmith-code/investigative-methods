# Budget, congressional and oversight data universe

**Scope for this investigation:** internationally directed US national-security capabilities across AI, quantum, drones/autonomy, cyber, electronic warfare, space, sensing, communications and enabling technologies. The inventory below is deliberately broader than the final inclusion boundary. Domestic-only policing, administrative AI and unrelated civilian projects are peripheral or out of scope unless a documented international/overseas security mission connects them. Source availability is not a decision to include its whole dataset.

Checked 16 October 2026. Scope: public US military/security investment records, including AI and emerging capabilities. This is a source map, not a claim that every listed corpus is complete or that any keyword-defined total is comprehensive.

## What is most useful

- **For small, overlooked projects:** RDT&E R-2/R-2A project narratives, R-3 cost/performer tables, procurement P-40/P-5/P-5a exhibits, congressional adjustment tables and reprogramming explanations have much finer detail than headline budget totals.
- **For repeatable portfolio coverage:** start with R-1/P-1 summaries, DD 1416 Excel files, OMB account-level spreadsheets and NCSES R&D tables. The first two preserve defense program/account structure; the latter two provide cross-government context.
- **For money actually available:** appropriations laws and their associated tables, DD 1414 enacted baselines and implemented reprogrammings are different records from the President’s request. None is a substitute for contract obligations or payments.
- **Current-vintage caution:** FY2027 request materials are publicly indexed. OMB’s FY2027 budget database includes historical actuals through FY2025 and estimates thereafter. The retrieved FY2026 DD 1416 page lists June 30, 2026 workbooks and explicitly labels September 30, 2026 “Coming soon!”

### Access labels

**Retrieved** means the official page or document actually opened in this test. **Listed** means a retrieved official page exposes the download, but the file/API response was not successfully parsed. **Indexed only** means an official source was found in search results, but direct retrieval failed. No authenticated API key was created and no bulk corpus was downloaded.

## Read the financial measure before using a number

1. **Requested/proposed:** an administration’s plan. Its future-year projections are not enacted commitments.
2. **Authorized:** permission/policy and often an authorized appropriations level. An ordinary NDAA does not itself make the matching money available.
3. **Appropriated budget authority:** authority supplied by law to incur obligations, subject to purpose/time/amount restrictions. Supplemental, reconciliation and continuing-resolution funding must be identified separately.
4. **Program allocation/current program:** agency/congressional distribution after transfers and reprogramming. DD 1414/1416 live principally here.
5. **Obligation:** a binding commitment such as an award or order. It may use authority enacted in an earlier year. **Outlay:** payment liquidating an obligation, potentially years later. “Actual” is only meaningful with the measure, period and vintage attached. See [CRS budget-process explanation](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R46240).
6. **Ceiling/target/lifecycle cost:** neither annual appropriation nor money paid. J-book target contract values, acquisition totals and contract ceilings cannot be summed with obligations as though they were equivalent.

## The usable joining boundary

Budget records commonly join to one another with component, appropriation, PE/project or procurement line-item, fiscal-year program, report date and budget vintage. **They generally do not supply a complete PIID/UEI contract crosswalk.** R-3/P-5a names, locations and dates can assist matching, but a name match is evidence to validate, not a deterministic key. Account-level OMB/Treasury joins are more mechanical but much coarser. Program renaming, transfers, splits, classification, differing dollar units and replenishment/reconciliation funding are persistent structural gaps.
