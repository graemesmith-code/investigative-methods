# Technical annex: acquisition, validation and reuse

This is an acquisition checkpoint for later investigation, not an estimate of total US investment or a list of unreported stories. The original 60-entry register, consolidated 89-entry register, 37-route expansion access register and source notes informed selection. [SOURCE_DECISIONS.csv](SOURCE_DECISIONS.csv) accounts for every consolidated source entry; untested/deferred sources retain their earlier access status rather than receiving a new availability claim.

## Scope and acquisition boundaries

The principal fiscal window is **FY2019–FY2026**, 2018-10-01–2026-09-30 for spending actions. Most recent public DoD contract dates are constrained by the 90-day release delay. A snapshot refreshed in October 2026 is not complete FY2026 defense procurement. Assistance reporting has different timing; the observed dates, not a blanket procurement-delay rule, describe its coverage.

**USAspending core:** public anonymous transaction-download endpoint, CSV ZIPs. Contract types A/B/C/D exclude IDV summaries; orders and modifications remain transaction records. Selection uses **awarding** agency, not all awards funded by DoD through another agency. Four bounded extracts have exact [request, response, status and count evidence](evidence/): all DARPA-awarded contracts; DoD contracts in R&D PSC category A; DoD contracts matching quantum, autonomous, unmanned, drone, hypersonic, electronic warfare, cyber, satellite, sensor or contested logistics; and DoD research assistance in 12.300, 12.431, 12.800, 12.910, 12.630 and 12.615. This is not the entire DoD procurement universe. The PSC slice reduces text-selection bias; the keyword slice adds relevant equipment and services outside R&D classifications. Do not assume either finds every capability. Grants and cooperative-agreement types 02/03/04/05 are separate from contracts.

The endpoint also generated reported first-tier subaward files. Parse and count them separately; the generator's `total_rows` is the combined CSV count. It is not the prime-transaction count. The requested 500,000-row cap is checked against actual output, and independent transaction count responses are retained. Any disagreement is reported rather than silently asserting completeness. The completed keyword export contains 305,768 prime actions versus 324,990 in the count endpoint, confirmed again after download; it is not represented as a complete match to that count. All 305,768 exported rows identify awarding agency 097/Department of Defense. There are 121 actions after 2026-07-18 (119 DISA, two Navy), and the maximum action date is 2026-09-30. Those observed dates do not establish complete recent-quarter coverage or explain exceptions to the general delay. The cause of the count/export discrepancy remains unestablished; smaller FY slices are a validation alternative, not assumed repairs. Empty/missing fields and signed monetary values are profiled for every CSV column. [Source-table profiles](validation/) include numeric parse and sign counts. Whole source CSVs also become lossless string-column Parquet tables.

**SBA SBIR/STTR:** two different official links resolve to different CSVs. The data-resources link (`awarddatapublic`) has a January 2026 server timestamp but much older defense coverage; the awards-page link (`mod_awarddatapublic`) has an October 2026 server timestamp, UEIs and more recent awards. The latter is primary. Preserve the older snapshot for comparison, never union both as independent awards. Main selection: reported `Award Year` 2019–2026; defense/war agencies, plus DOE records with transparent capability regex hits. No unrelated NIH award corpus is acquired. DOE candidates still require actual security-mission evidence.

SBA defines `Award Year` as federal FY and `Proposal Award Date` as the first day of contract performance. Ten retained records' reported award FY differs from the FY derived from performance start; one start is before 2018-10-01. They remain in the award-year slice, with dates preserved. All 25,770 retained rows have parseable start dates, spanning 2018-07-27–2026-01-23; only 156 are labeled FY2026. This does **not** establish complete FY2026 coverage. The complete raw file includes older malformed/implausible dates, including 1905; its historical date range must not be confused with the retained window. One exact duplicate remains in the source-preserving canonical file; an exact-deduplicated view removes it. Different-content repeated source composites remain separate records for review.

**Grants.gov:** one complete daily XML ZIP for 2026-10-16, all 83,576 synopsis/forecast records parsed. Select FY2019–FY2026 posting dates and DoD/war agencies, plus DOE/DHS/State capability-text candidates. Repeated XML fields are preserved as arrays in selected-source JSONL and joined values in flattened source Parquet/evidence envelopes; raw XML is authoritative. Posting date, forecast, amendment/version, closing date and award date are different. The snapshot is not a complete history of every previous opportunity version. It does not substitute for SAM solicitations or attachments.

**OMB:** complete PB2025 and PB2027 budget-authority and outlay XLSX files, each opened and parsed. Retain complete source tables and long FY2019–FY2026 security-account cells across Defense/War, Energy, Homeland Security, State and International Assistance agency labels. These include broad/routine accounts and are context until capability/mission review. The newer PB2027 vintage labels FY2019–FY2025 actuals and FY2026 estimated; PB2025 has its own actual/estimate boundary. Historical classifications are recast into each vintage's account structure. The full dimension tuple, measure and vintage identify a row; agency/bureau/account alone can repeat across BEA category, subfunction and grant splits. Missing account codes on receipts are retained. No universal budget-project-to-contract crosswalk is inferred.

**Project and institutional evidence:** all six linked Microelectronics Commons technology-area PDFs; the March 2023 APFIT implementation report's ten FY2022 projects; the full DARPA PB2025 justification book; the GovInfo FY2026 DoD appendix; DOE FY2019–FY2025 annual LDRD financial reports; the full Sandia FY2025 LDRD narrative report; complete finite FFRDC FY2025 microdata and Federal Funds table bundles; and the April 2025 PSC workbook. The current APFIT index has images rather than a parsed all-year project table, so no current-complete portfolio is claimed. Sandia is a narrative/highlights report, not a project census.

## File lineage, keys and record units

[http_manifest.jsonl](validation/http_manifest.jsonl) records exact requested/final URLs, UTC retrieval time, HTTP status, media type, ETag/last-modified where supplied, relative task-data path, size and SHA-256 for every completed download. Failures are recorded without inventing a checksum. A successful HTML response at a presumed data URL is not accepted as a spreadsheet/CSV; parsers and signatures, not HTTP status alone, establish usable data. [normalized_manifest.json](validation/normalized_manifest.json) records hashes, sizes and Parquet row counts for derived files.

Canonical evidence fields are documented in [canonical_record_schema.json](design/canonical_record_schema.json). IDs include a source identity and raw-row content hash; PDF records use file SHA-256 plus physical page. This preserves different versions while identifying exact repeated content. Source record keys can collide; duplicate raw rows remain in source-preserving files. `raw_locator` provides the source member and row/ID/page. CSV row numbers are data rows excluding header. PDFs use physical page numbering, which can differ from the printed page label.

UEIs are preferred legal-entity identifiers, with DUNS as historical fallback. They are not interchangeable corporate-group identifiers. PIIDs need agency and parent-IDV context. SBIR tracking number/contract/agency/program/phase/year form a composite, not a guaranteed global unique key. The CSV lacks direct SBA record URLs, so portal link, exact identifiers, title and raw locator are retained. USAspending record permalinks and Grants.gov opportunity URLs are kept where available. Budget PE/project references, laboratory question keys and project title/lead combinations have their own grains.

The canonical Parquet schema intentionally uses strings/nulls, with JSON strings for monetary fields, identifier bundles and regex tags. Decimal amounts are not coerced through binary floating-point for award analysis. Full source-table Parquet preserves all original fields as strings, including agency, appropriation/account, classification and recipient attributes. OMB scaled dollar cells, LDRD financial rows and project extracts have separate source-specific schemas. Code and source dictionaries explain them.

## Money and date semantics

The [monetary-field dictionary](validation/monetary_fields.csv) enumerates monetary fields in downloaded transaction/subaward schemas, plus all normalized supplemental measures. [source_fields.csv](validation/source_fields.csv) carries Treasury's current official field definitions and exact download-field names. Treasury's dictionary and disclosure/guide PDFs are themselves downloaded and hashed. The official transaction-search mapping is pinned to a repository commit to document the derived `generated_pragmatic_obligations` field.

- **Obligation change:** `federal_action_obligation` is signed action-level federal commitment/decommitment. Only unique transactions in a defined action-date scope can support a transaction sum. No such investment total is asserted here.
- **Cumulative obligations:** `total_dollars_obligated`/`total_obligated_amount` refer to the overall award and repeat across actions. Never sum those rows. `generated_pragmatic_obligations` duplicates action obligations in this grant slice; do not add both.
- **Contract values and options:** base/exercised and base/all-options fields describe contract value and can report changes on modifications; current/potential award-value fields are separate from action obligations and may vary by action/vintage. Unexercised options and potential ceilings are not paid expenditure. Treasury's current-value wording uses “obligated”; preserve that official definition while keeping the distinct fields separate.
- **Outlays:** overall award-level payment measures and supplemental subsets, repeated on actions/subawards. They are not payments made on the row's action date. Pre-FY2022 coverage can be incomplete. No separate monthly File C corpus is acquired.
- **Subawards:** reported first-tier values, possibly revised/cumulative, not an additional federal spending layer. Parent-award amounts/outlays repeat on subaward rows. Executive compensation fields describe pay, not investment.
- **Assistance:** nonfederal contributions are not federal spending. Indirect costs are a component of approved action funding. Loan principal and loan subsidy cost are distinct; loan types are outside this selected grant scope.
- **Budget authority, outlays and requests:** OMB source units are thousands of dollars and values are net of offsetting collections. Negative receipts remain signed. Authority and outlays, actuals and estimates, and different budget vintages never become a single additive measure. Authorization is permission/policy, not appropriation or payment. No new authorization corpus is acquired.
- **DARPA R-2/R-2A:** program amounts in millions, including FY2025 requests and prior/current-year exhibit amounts. No claim that every earlier column is an award obligation or outlay. Program, project and PE totals nest; cross-PE references can describe the same effort. The 256 title-row extraction is not a complete financial-table reconstruction; 47 rows lack a parsed project ID, while PE IDs are present.
- **Commons/APFIT:** disclosed project amounts and selected funding are kept with exact labels. One Commons amount has unspecified scale and is deliberately null in `amount_usd`. These can overlap later awards and are not assumed action obligations. APFIT production quantities/delivery dates are narrative evidence, not dollars.
- **Institutional research:** FFRDC sections 1–3 report separately accounted R&D expenditure in thousands of dollars for the organization's FY2025, not necessarily federal FY. Source/agency/type breakdowns overlap. Federal Funds tables instead report federal obligations/outlays or percentages, with units varying by table; FY2025 is preliminary. DOE LDRD costs are institutional expenditures funded through overhead, not new prime awards; broad laboratory totals include the LDRD component. SPP includes non-DOE federal and nonfederal work, so it is not wholly national-security investment.

[The Sandia example](evidence/financial_basis_examples.json) shows why these distinctions matter: $260.6M excluding program-management costs in Sandia's report and $265.38M LDRD total costs in DOE's report both accompany 536 projects. They are not two investments or necessarily a contradiction. The difference has not been causally reconciled.

## Parsing and verification limits

All ZIP members are inventoried and CRC-tested; every downloaded payload is SHA-256 checked. Counts, date ranges, year distributions, raw/retained identifier missingness, duplicate identities and numeric monetary parse/sign checks are saved in per-source profiles. [checks.json](validation/checks.json) contains machine-readable acceptance results; [canonical_profiles.json](validation/canonical_profiles.json) audits lineage and document-key duplication. Samples are representative inspection aids, not statistical samples or separately acquired token corpora.

All selected PDFs are converted to full page text. FY2019 and FY2021 DOE LDRD reports are scanned: full OCR is supplementary and flagged, while their main Table 1 is manually transcribed and visually checked against physical page 8. The small [transcription CSV](evidence/ldrd_scanned_table1_transcription.csv) is reviewable on GitHub. The seven-year financial table has 123 rows including seven totals; project counts reconcile for every year, and small monetary rounding differences remain as reported. OCR text must never be treated as a verified monetary extraction merely because it contains digits.

No assertions cover classified data, unrevealed procurement, all OT ultimate performers, all subcontract tiers, every agency, all opportunities/versions or complete current-year reporting. Public availability does not establish an unrestricted redistribution license for every credited image/third-party component. Preserve original attribution and notices. GitHub holds modest code/metadata/text samples, not entire illustrated reports or large corpora.

## Deduplication and later pipeline

Exact transaction overlap is measured in [overlap.json](validation/overlap.json). The unique-transaction index chooses one preferred representation while retaining alternate source-document references; different-content variants are flagged. It does not collapse different transactions on one award. The newer SBIR exact-deduplicated view removes only exact identity/content repetition. SBIR contract-to-USAspending links are candidates requiring agency/parent context, not automatic merges or extra dollars. Announcements, grants, subawards, budget lines, laboratory expenditure and project disclosures can all describe overlapping investments.

[PIPELINE_INTERFACES.md](design/PIPELINE_INTERFACES.md) and the example request specify later novelty/capability scoring, uncertain capability relationships, cluster newsworthiness, Exa checks and stronger-model dossiers. The included [dry run](validation/dry_run.json) uses deterministic regex/identifier/readiness checks only. Scores remain null, execution/cost limits are zero, and no model/Exa classifier is called. A security-agency sponsor or regex hit is a candidate flag, not a finding of novelty, international relevance or wrongdoing.

## Storage and reproduction

The task-data root is `/workspace/task-data/future-warfare-investments/`, configurable with `ACQUISITION_DATA_DIR`. Raw files and normalized datasets remain outside Git. The initial overlay had about 30 GB free; download budget is 15 GB, with at least 6 GB headroom enforced. Workspace persistence is not independently guaranteed. The final README/STORAGE record states the actual archive-save outcome; an intention to upload is not retention evidence.

From the repository root:

```bash
python3 -m venv /workspace/acquisition-venv
/workspace/acquisition-venv/bin/pip install -r research/2026-10-16/future-warfare-investments/acquisition/code/requirements.lock.txt
export ACQUISITION_DATA_DIR=/workspace/task-data/future-warfare-investments
/workspace/acquisition-venv/bin/python research/2026-10-16/future-warfare-investments/acquisition/code/retrieve.py --verify-only
```

Restore the durable archive first when available. `retrieve.py --source SOURCE` downloads an absent manifest payload and verifies its original checksum; omit `--source` to retrieve all completed payloads. A mutable source can legitimately differ: the script reports the mismatch rather than claiming it restored the old snapshot. USAspending generated URLs can expire. `retrieve.py --source usaspending_dod_rd_contracts --regenerate` submits the exact bounded request again and records a **new** snapshot; its checksum and rows need fresh validation.

Run `normalize.py SOURCE` and `parse_auxiliary.py SOURCE` for each USAspending extract; `normalize.py sbir_current` for the primary SBA file; `parse_supplements.py grants pdfs ffrdc html`; `parse_budget.py`; `parse_capabilities.py`; `parse_ldrd.py`; `parse_auxiliary.py federal_funds psc`; `build_dictionary.py`; `supplement_dictionaries.py`; `deduplicate.py`; `dry_run.py`; then `validate.py`. OCR regeneration uses `ocr_ldrd.py`, local Poppler and Tesseract, not a paid service. The pinned Python dependency file and runtime record support reruns; PDF/OCR engine versions can affect derived bytes, so raw hashes and source lineage remain the primary reproducibility anchors.
