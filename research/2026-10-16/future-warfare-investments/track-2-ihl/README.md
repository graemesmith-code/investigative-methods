# Track 2: civilian protection, IHL and the funded research network

**Updated 16 October 2026.** This track follows people, products and funding separately. It asks what survived the Civilian Protection Center of Excellence's contraction and what public records establish about attempts to model legal constraints, train personnel or evaluate military AI. It does not treat these different activities as one deployed “IHL targeting algorithm.”

## What the expanded evidence establishes

### The centre contracted; no complete institutional successor is established

A directly readable **13 October congressional letter reports seven centre staff**. It supplies a firmer source for the latest reported count than the inaccessible October New York Times article used as an initial lead. It remains a congressional statement rather than an audited roster. The centre's statutory mandate still exists. A proposed move from the Army to the Joint Staff has not been verified as implemented.

Public records place former officials at NYU's Reiss Center, the Institute for Defense Analyses, independent civilian-protection work and investigations, and, in one case, a different government role. These document careers and continuing expertise. They do not establish transfer of the centre's government data, software, staff billets or authority. [Full people and institutional history](TEAM_CONTINUITY.md)

### Some products and analytical work have identifiable continuations

- **Maven civilian-environment work:** the FY2024 report's indexed text identifies XVIII Airborne Corps' Warfighter Exercise 24-5. That is a specific technical and institutional trail; the present owner and deployment status remain unknown.
- **AskSage-based prototype:** the FY2025 report is described in credible reporting, but its complete PDF could not be retrieved. The reported exercise-time saving is not an independently validated protection or legal-compliance result.
- **Army CHMR Data Management Platform:** a separate disrupted system. QBE is tied to CHMR by its own announcement, and a civil-society report links it to DMP development. This does not identify QBE as builder of the Maven or AskSage features.
- **CNA work:** a public analytical report preserves a CP CoE/INDOPACOM collaboration; a separate CDAO-funded CNA task records $500,000 in obligations for civilian harm and responsible AI. Neither proves that CNA inherited the centre's programme. [Sources and unresolved ownership questions](TEAM_CONTINUITY.md)

### The funding trail is substantially more concrete

The [contract investigation](CONTRACT_BREADCRUMBS.md) connects four research lines to awards, investigators, collaborators and outputs:

1. **Perceptronics / CETLAWS → TEELAWS:** the primary record confirms $750,000 obligated on the Air Force CETLAWS award and $1,899,998.92 on its Army training successor. SBA explicitly identifies the succession; West Point documented classroom testing in April 2026. The earlier $49,998 Air Force project is a plausible predecessor whose formal Phase I linkage remains unverified. This is evidence of funded legal/ethical modeling and training, with no verified operational targeting deployment.
2. **ASIMOV:** all seven prime awards are identified. Together they show **$20,495,054 in obligations** and **$39,475,202 in base-plus-all-options values**. These are different measures. The programme is marked complete; potential end dates and unexercised options do not establish ongoing work. Sixteen reported subaward rows reveal a wider company/university network. Their values are inside the prime awards and are not additional federal spending.
3. **CoVar continuation:** a 2026 company announcement explicitly connects a new PPADM/VectoredAI effort with ASIMOV-derived GEARS software. This is an announced adjacent research continuation; its award number/amount and an IHL-specific successor mandate remain unresolved.
4. **CODORD:** official programme and university/company records identify USC, RTX BBN and SRI branches, plus public legal-reasoning outputs. A separate primary Johns Hopkins APL task records $2,481,841 obligated. It must not be conflated with Johns Hopkins' academic role on BBN's team. Primary OT transactions for the other branches remain missing. Civilian legal-reasoning benchmarks cannot be relabeled IHL-compliance tests.

## What changed from the initial notes

The [initial funding note](../IHL_TARGETING_FUNDING.md) and [initial centre history](../IHL_CENTRE_HISTORY.md) remain as dated records of the first pass. The current reports supersede their uncertainty about the CETLAWS award amount, resolve all seven ASIMOV prime awards, add reported subaward/continuation trails, and improve attribution for the latest seven-person staffing claim. The inaccessible annual-report PDFs and missing software ownership/transfer records remain real limitations.

Public records do not establish an aggregate “IHL algorithm” budget, universal legal compliance, or a clean transfer of the centre's mission. Obligations are commitments, not cash payments. DoD procurement publication has a 90-day delay. This is an evidence map and deep dive, with no claim that every finding is previously unreported.
