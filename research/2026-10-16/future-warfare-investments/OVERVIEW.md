## 1. The expanding data universe

[Read the sweeping overview](track-1-data-universe/README.md) or open the [consolidated source register](track-1-data-universe/SOURCE_REGISTER.csv).

The universe now spans public money, research institutions and people, technical requirements and outputs, prototypes and industrial capacity, physical infrastructure, international transfers, and previous reporting. It includes quantum, drones/autonomy, cyber, electronic warfare, communications, space, sensing, advanced materials and enabling power/logistics alongside AI.

The [bulk-access register](track-1-data-universe/BULK_ACCESS_REGISTER.csv) identifies free downloads and collection routes, while separating successful samples from publisher-listed offers, account requirements, paid products and access failures. The source register consolidates **89 entries**: the initial 60 plus 29 distinct additions. Refinements and overlapping source views are reconciled explicitly; this is not a count of independent spending datasets.

The [historical extension](history/README.md) is now published: **18,819 substantive paragraphs from 1,245 in-period pages**, covering **12 September 2018–11 September 2023**, alongside the unchanged original September 2023–September 2026 export. The [combined deduplicated index](history/combined_deduplicated_index.csv) contains **28,818 unique paragraph keys**. These are archived copies of official pages; live listings returned 403. Coverage remains partial: **59 weekdays have no recovered page**, which does not establish that an announcement was missing. The original 10,000-row export also cuts off partway through its oldest day. Read the [coverage and comparability limits](history/COVERAGE.md) before comparing periods.

## 2. IHL, civilian protection and the funded research network

[Read the deep-dive overview](track-2-ihl/README.md), [team and product continuity](track-2-ihl/TEAM_CONTINUITY.md), or [contract and research trails](track-2-ihl/CONTRACT_BREADCRUMBS.md).

The centre's public record points to severe contraction and dispersed expertise; no clean handover to a successor is established. Named professionals, residual institutional work and specific technical products have different trajectories. The contractual investigation now connects legal/ethical modeling, training, autonomy benchmarking and broader rules-to-logic research to primary procurement records and reported subawards.

The evidence package contains a [14-award ledger](track-2-ihl/ihl_prime_awards.csv), [16 reported subaward rows](track-2-ihl/ihl_reported_subawards.csv), and [42 primary API responses plus a retrieval manifest](track-2-ihl/EVIDENCE_README.md). Source access and unresolved links remain explicit. A research grant, training demonstrator or proposed continuation is not evidence of a fielded, legally reliable targeting system.
