Completed 16 October 2026. Scope includes externally authored material hosted by Afghan government bodies. Public universities are reported separately. Language labels were candidate filters, not accepted findings.

These are unique recovered PDFs, including mixed-language works with substantial target-language content. Arabic counts include five school textbooks and one calendar; they are not eleven diplomatic documents.

## Start with these

- **Russian transport protocol:** four-page signed English/Russian protocol for Afghanistan joining a cross-border transport agreement involving Tajikistan and Kyrgyzstan. Russian on pages 3–4. Signature blocks carry dates in 2011 and 2013. It extends the corridor from Nizhni Pyanj / Sher Khan Bandar to Torkham.
- **Egyptian Embassy / Al-Azhar form:** Arabic/English/Dari blank scholarship registration form, republished by Kabul's Higher Education Ministry. An outside-authored item, not a completed application or proof of an award.
- **Indonesian scholarship announcement:** Arabic poster from Maulana Malik Ibrahim State Islamic University, Malang, with registration dates 24 February–24 March 2020. Also hosted by the Higher Education Ministry.
- **Emirate Friday sermon booklet:** Pashto introduction and Arabic sermons on PDF pages 8–12. Page 11 contains a prayer naming Hibatullah Akhundzada. This is domestic religious/governance material; a foreign audience is not established.
- **Turkish academic article:** a 31-page study of Jamal al-Din Afghani in Diwan (2023), Jawzjan University's international journal. It is the only verified Turkish result and belongs in the university appendix.

The other Russian PDF is a Russian version of the 2015 regional cooperation conference statement, corresponding to the earlier Chinese find. The remaining Arabic material is routine education, academic or administrative publishing. No claim of novelty or newsworthiness is made.

## Coverage and limits

Scanned all 136 selected FinePDFs parquet files: **20,775,502 records**, with no remaining file/row download gaps. Broad host filtering produced 135 candidate records, followed by ownership and language screening. Fifteen retained records reduce to fourteen unique PDF files because the Russian conference statement appears twice.

Three source records remain unresolved because their original PDFs were unusable or unavailable: a customs procedure, a Higher Education development-budget file and a Commerce Ministry price table. None is counted as verified Arabic. Some other exclusions rely on readable extracted Dari/Pashto/English text rather than full page-by-page inspection; the audit records review basis and recovery state.

This is exhaustive for the selected dataset language partitions and host rule, **not an exhaustive inventory of all foreign-language pages on Afghan websites**. Mixed-language PDFs assigned to English, Dari, Pashto or another partition can be missed. The earlier Russian waybill extraction from the separate unknown-language screen is a prior lead, not a newly recovered PDF here. Archive dates are crawl dates, not proof of publication dates. Current-site replacements may differ from the historical captured PDF; recovery source and hashes are recorded.

## Extraction failures

Several Arabic-labelled records contain invented or unrelated Arabic extraction over rotated or faint Dari originals. Examples include an Afghan Telecom internet contract and mining/procurement material. Merely running a better language classifier over those extracted strings would not solve the problem. Positive findings were checked against actual PDF pages. Damaged/unavailable originals remain uncertain rather than being declared Arabic.

## Separate company-hosted by-product

A document on **dawioil.af**, outside the government scope, is headed as an Emirates NBD statement for Dawi Oil, covering 30 December 2014–22 November 2017 and printed 12 March 2020. It is mostly English with Arabic bank headings, not an Arabic prose publication. First-page labels refer to airline fuel payments and the United Nations. It has not been authenticated with the bank and establishes no misconduct. The PDF is supplied separately to the researcher, not republished in this repository.
