## Verdict

The evidence establishes real contractual restrictions, investor consequences and corporate legal/governance processes. It does not establish that company-survival incentives reliably preserve an independent veto over autonomous targeting. The clearest historically applicable signed restriction coexists with broad negotiated permissions and a deployment environment in which the provider says it cannot technically enforce its contract. Government riders can preserve licence duties while qualifying precedence and remedies. Other provisions primarily allocate losses, protect intellectual property or govern exports.

No reviewed record establishes a named General Counsel stopping a particular autonomous operational release. This is a bounded finding, not proof that no such decision exists. An applicable contractual restriction remains meaningful evidence without a public enforcement event. A legal officer's signature, public policy or possible suspension is not proof of an exercised veto.

The governing question is **who can require what, for which product/version and customer, under which agreement, with what enforcement and exceptions?** Four source domains completed three actual independent editorial rounds. That clearance concerns their frozen evidence, not this integration or publication; no qualified-lawyer review is claimed.
