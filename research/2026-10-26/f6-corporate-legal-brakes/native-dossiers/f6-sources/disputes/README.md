## Files

- `candidate-register.csv/json`: normalized legal entity, product/version, customer/contract, decision-maker, trigger, exception, consequence, novelty and gaps.
- `source-register.csv/json`: stable public source links, dates, hashes, access and rights dispositions.
- `version-incorporation-chains.json`: separate historical contracts, public terms, proposed amendments and judicial tracks.
- `reference-occurrences.csv/json`: all retained lexical hits plus manual semantic additions. A hit is **not** an IHL safeguard. Generic candidates remain explicitly unadjudicated.
- `human-control-context-review.csv/json`: 71 human-control/civilian/kinetic contexts individually classified.
- `nonhit-review-sample.csv/json`: 16 exact sampled pages, selection bias and discovered misses.
- `coverage-summary.json`, `page-coverage.csv/json`, `panel-coverage.csv/json`: denominators and omissions.
- `lexicon-v1.json`, `screening-code-v1.py`, `context-review-code-v1.py`: reproducible authored extraction logic; private source inputs are intentionally not published. Subsequent remedy-context/date corrections are described in memo/editor records; code alone does not recreate all final hand edits.
- `query-log.csv/json`, `access-log.json`, `cost-log.json`: bounded searches, access failures and zero paid spend.
- `editor-decisions.json`: three review rounds and correction decisions.
- `publication-whitelist.json`: only authorized authored derivatives proposed for publication by the coordinator.

## Counts and limits

28 source entries; 25 locally extracted sources; 817 physical pages (including duplicated opinion attachments and broad GSAR material). 1,697 lexical matches plus five manual semantic additions produce 1,702 occurrence rows. There are nine promoted findings. Manual review count is recorded in `coverage-summary.json`; this is not exhaustive semantic review of all pages.

**No raw source PDFs, HTML, text, signatures or full proprietary context files are publication-approved.** Public availability—including a court exhibit bearing an old Confidential marking—does not equal redistribution permission. `private/` is working material only. This package supplies links, hashes, minimal extracts and authored context instead.
