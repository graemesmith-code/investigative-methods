# Legal claim index

- **LRE01** — Boyle military-equipment design-defect defence requires government-approved reasonably precise specs, conformity, supplier warning of known dangers unknown to US. Vacated/remanded. Source **LR01**, 487 US 500,512–514. [Boyle](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-487/pdf/USREPORTS-487-500.pdf).
- **LRE02** — Summary judgment affirmed for named manufacturers; substantial Army review and warnings record supported claim-specific defence. Software presence did not itself defeat defence. ATEC dismissed for lack of personal jurisdiction. Source **LR33**, opinion printed 9966–9967,9972–9976; disposition 9957. [Getz v Boeing Co Ninth Circuit No 10-15284](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2011/08/02/10-15284.pdf).
- **LRE03** — Reversed remand because contractor alleged colorable federal defence sufficient for federal-officer removal; not final merits adjudication. Source **LR02**, opinion pp 12–17. [Sawyer](https://www.ca4.uscourts.gov/Opinions/Published/161530.P.pdf).
- **LRE04** — 25 February 2026: Yearsley protection is merits defence; denial not immediately appealable under collateral-order doctrine. Affirmed/remanded. Source **LR34**, majority slip pp 1,7–12; footnote 5. [GEO Group v Menocal SCOTUS No 24-758 slip opinion](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/25pdf/24-758_2dp3.pdf).
- **LRE05** — Federal contractor does not receive government’s unqualified immunity; alleged violation of federal law and express government instructions outside Yearsley protection. Read with Menocal 2026 terminology. Source **LR32**, 577 US 153,166–169. [Campbell-Ewald Co v Gomez](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-577/pdf/USREPORTS-577-153.pdf).
- **LRE06** — FAR>$150 m congressional 60-day threshold/wait expressly excepts 50.104-3 indemnity. Other duties remain. FAR $90 k delegation rule not statutory $500 k; nuclear§ 876(c) newer exception qualifies head-only rule. Source **LR03**, 50.102-3(b)(4);50.102-1(b), (d). [FAR Subpart 50.1](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/subpart-50.1).
- **LRE07** — Contracting officer assisted by counsel/program staff reviews insurance, defined risk and national-defence justification; signed memorandum and clause incorporation needed. Source **LR16**, 50.104-3(a), (b). [FAR 50.104-3](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/50.104-3).
- **LRE08** — Coverage only for defined risk and uncompensated loss; no insurance deductibles. Principal-official misconduct exception concerns govt claims except subrogation and contractor property, not blanket third-party bar; govt may control defence. Source **LR04**, 52.250-1(c), (d), (g), (h). [FAR 52.250-1](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.250-1).
- **LRE09** — Practicable DCMA insurance-market input; final decision and executed memo within 90 days. Military secretaries must delegate advanced-nuclear-system/component requests to appropriate subordinates. Source **LR47**, PL119-60§ 876(a)–(c),139 Stat 1005. [FY 2026 NDAA PL119-60 scoped sections 801 and 876 only](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ60/html/PLAW-119publ60.htm).
- **LRE10** — Contractor not required to assume specified work-in-process loss uninsurable or unclaimable because classified; excludes managerial misconduct/lack good faith and workmanship error. Source **LR47**, PL119-60§ 801/new 10 USC 3864,139 Stat 939–940. [FY 2026 NDAA PL119-60 scoped sections 801 and 876 only](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ60/html/PLAW-119publ60.htm).
- **LRE11** — War-hazard employee compensation context disproves a categorical all-war-risk-uninsurable claim, but says nothing conclusive about AI product-liability insurance. Source **LR24**, 28.305. [FAR 28.305](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/28.305).
- **LRE12** — Mixed R&D/other work may involve separate 10 USC 3861 and 85-804 authorities; latter only for work not indemnifiable under 3861. Source **LR25**, 250.104-3-70. [DFARS 250.104-3-70](https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars/250.104-3-70-indemnification-under-contracts-involving-both-research-and-development-and-other-work.).
- **LRE13** — Exact consent requires legal support across divisions, independent compliance oversight, CEO/board escalation and records access; privilege/workproduct waiver not compelled. Source **LR06**, §§ 9(b), (e)–(h),22; printed pp 4–6,14. [RTX Consent Agreement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101829/000010182924000031/a2024-08x30exhibit991.htm).
- **LRE14** — Consent requires jurisdiction review before specified supplier-item exports, automated export decision tracking and external audit. $200 m total comprises $100 m cash and $100 m conditionally suspended compliance expenditure. No automatic debarment imposed. Source **LR06**, §§ 12–14,18–21,24,28–29. [RTX Consent Agreement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101829/000010182924000031/a2024-08x30exhibit991.htm).
- **LRE15** — Changing specified end user, use or destination ordinarily requires DDTC written approval, subject to express exceptions. Source **LR30**, 123.9(a), (c). [22 CFR 123.9](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/chapter-I/subchapter-M/part-123/section-123.9).
- **LRE16** — EAR General Prohibition 9 addresses violation of licence/order/authorization terms; distinct from ITAR. Source **LR31**, 736.2(b)(9). [EAR 736.2 general prohibitions](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/subtitle-B/chapter-VII/subchapter-C/part-736/section-736.2).
- **LRE17** — Blue Lantern covers direct-commercial channels and brokering with pre/post licence and shipment checks. Monitoring report not substantive licence itself. Source **LR35**, pp 1–2. [Blue Lantern end-use monitoring report FY 2025](https://www.pmddtc.state.gov/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=f7c320619774cb14fe5c39b0f053af72).
- **LRE18** — Golden Sentry is distinct government-to-government regime; additional use restrictions may be in LOA notes. C8.T2 links UAS Principles-of-Use written agreement, followed to 2020 template. Source **LR15**, C8.2.2–3;C8.3.2–4;C8.T2. [DSCA chapter 8](https://samm.dsca.mil/chapter/chapter-8).
- **LRE19** — Historical incorporated memo explicitly required IHL/lawful-use assurances, consent for modification/integration, and appropriate training. Recipient duty, not independent vendor veto. Source **LR44**, Attachment FMS/BPC note§§ 1–5. [DSCA military UAS principles policy 20-31](https://samm.dsca.mil/policy-memoranda/dsca-20-31).
- **LRE20** — ICC natural-person jurisdiction is not corporate jurisdiction; territorial/nationality/accepted/referral gateways, admissibility, participation/mental elements and arrest/surrender procedure separate. Source **LR17**, arts 12–13,17,25,30,58,89,98. [Rome Statute core original text](https://legal.un.org/icc/statute/english/rome_statute%28e%29.pdf).
- **LRE21** — VStGB 6–12 can apply abroad without German nexus; aggression 13 narrower. Intentional assistance and prosecutorial discretion matter; corporate administrative fine under OWiG 30 is not ICC corporate criminal liability. Source **LR43**, §§ 1–5; see LR12 StPO 153f, LR22 StGB 27, LR23 OWiG 30. [German VStGB current German consolidation](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/vstgb/BJNR225410002.html).
- **LRE22** — England/Wales territory or overseas UK national/resident/service links; ancillary conduct separately defined; Attorney General consent required under 53. Source **LR13**, § 51(1)–(2);LR14§ 52;LR42§ 53(3). [UK ICC Act 2001 s 51](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/17/section/51).
- **LRE23** — 2023 amendment includes offender-presence jurisdiction regardless nationality; prosecutorial certification required; presence-only route requires AG/Deputy AG and additional considerations. No automatic arrest. Source **LR37**, 2441(b), (e)–(i). [18 USC 2441 War Crimes](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/2441).
- **LRE24** — Trial ended 28 May 2026; judgment scheduled 3 December 2026 after cutoff. Indictment and corporate financial claims not conviction. Source **LR09**, Lundin case paragraph Swedish and English. [Lundin court status](https://www.domstol.se/stockholms-tingsratt/om-tingsratten/aktuellt/uppmarksammade-mal/).

[Lafarge US plea and attachments](https://www.justice.gov/d9/press-releases/attachments/2022/10/19/us_v._lafarge_-_plea_agreement_and_attachments_ecf_0.pdf).
