# Western Europe: verified lidar sources (checked 14 October 2026)

The strongest options in this regional review are the Netherlands, England and Switzerland. All offer structures in actual airborne laser point clouds; all now have concrete evidence of repeat coverage. England adds a particularly long history of dated surface rasters. France is a strong high-density baseline, but this review did not verify a second national lidar acquisition of the same footprint. Germany has good free laser data, but retained historical point clouds remain a separate question. These are selective findings, not an exhaustive European survey.

## 1. Netherlands — AHN: recommended

**Access and products.** [Official dataroom](https://www.ahn.nl/dataroom) links to the [download index](https://basisdata.nl/hwh-ahn/AUX/bladwijzer/index.html), [original AHN2/3/4/5 tile index GeoPackage](https://basisdata.nl/hwh-ahn/AUX/bladwijzer.gpkg), LAZ point clouds and GeoTIFF DSM/DTM at 0.5 m and 5 m. Original tiles are 5 × 6.25 km; newer kilometre tiles include reprocessed old data. This is airborne laser acquisition, not stereophotography: see [acquisition explanation](https://www.ahn.nl/hoe-werkt-het-inwinnen-van-hoogtegegevens). Raw/classified returns support investigating industrial roofs, buildings, earthworks, demolition and reclamation.

**Coverage and repeat evidence.** AHN is repeated countrywide coverage, not a single national patchwork spanning different dates. [AHN4 documentation](https://www.ahn.nl/ahn-4) describes 2020–2022 acquisition following AHN3 (2014–2019), with a changed allocation of survey regions. The review verified actual LAZ headers for **C_37HN1.LAZ** in AHN3 and AHN4: matching RD footprint x = 90000–95000, y = 431250–437500; both HTTP 206, only 4096 bytes per file sampled. Header file-creation years are not flight dates. Specific acquisition-date metadata still needs spatial intersection with [AHN3 flight lines](https://basisdata.nl/hwh-ahn/AUX/vlieglijnen/AHN3_clip.gpkg) and [AHN4 flight lines](https://basisdata.nl/hwh-ahn/AUX/vlieglijnen/AHN4.gpkg). No claim of measured change has been made.

**License.** The official linked [download index](https://basisdata.nl/hwh-ahn/AUX/bladwijzer/index.html) explicitly distinguishes **AHN1/2/3/4: CC0 1.0** from **AHN5/6: CC BY 4.0**. Do not apply older unrestricted open-data descriptions indiscriminately to newer editions. CC0 has no mandatory credit; retain AHN/provider/version/date credit as good journalism practice. CC BY requires appropriate attribution, license link and change indication; use the exact dataset rights holder from its metadata when publishing AHN5/6. [CC0 text](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) and [CC BY 4.0 text](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The service is anonymous and free; no citizenship limitation was found. Large original cloud tiles can be multi-GB: sampled tile catalog sizes approximately 2.0 GB AHN3 and 9.38 GB AHN4. Prefer small reprocessed raster tiles or selective cloud reads where supported; do not start with a whole original cloud download.

**Comparability warning.** The index's own notes describe changed raster derivation: AHN2/3/4 half-metre DSMs used inverse-distance weighting; AHN5/6 use the highest point. New kilometre tiles of old editions are rederived to largely match AHN6 specifications. Some power-line/tower classes are excluded differently between editions. Point-cloud classification also changes. A large apparent difference can therefore be processing, not construction. All coordinates are RD New + NAP height (EPSG:7415; NAP vertical EPSG:5709), per dataroom. Use matched processing on classified clouds or explicitly matched raster recipes.

## 2. England — Environment Agency archive: recommended

**Access.** [Official point-cloud dataset](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/977a4ca4-1759-4f26-baa7-b566bd7ca7bf/lidar-time-stamped-point-cloud), [Defra point-cloud documentation](https://dsp.environment.data.gov.uk/dataset/094d4ec8-4c21-4aa6-817f-b7e45843c5e0), [dated DSM documentation](https://dsp.environment.data.gov.uk/dataset/1021ecff-6549-4dbe-b8a0-48ae72a3c698), [download selector](https://environment.data.gov.uk/DefraDataDownload/?Mode=survey). The current official [WFS capabilities](https://environment.data.gov.uk/spatialdata/survey-index-files/wfs?request=GetCapabilities&service=WFS) work anonymously. Old indexed ArcGIS service URLs returned a JSON `Invalid URL` error (HTTP application error 400); use current WFS instead. No accounts or payments were used.

**Coverage/products.** England, site-specific dated lidar raster archive since 1998 and classified LAZ point-cloud archive back to 2006, with holes in historical retention. Dated DSMs are **last-return** surfaces, not automatically identical to modern first-return DSMs. Raster resolution varies by survey (0.25–2 m). The latest composite is a mosaic chosen from various acquisition dates, not one survey date. Do not compare two composite publication years as if they were two repeat flights. The point-cloud archive does not necessarily retain a corresponding cloud for every old raster.

**Concrete repeat check: London Docklands.** A 10 × 10 m metadata query at OSGB x=537600–537610, y=180200–180210 intersected:

- National_LIDAR_Programme_Index_Catalogue: **TQ3580 / P_10768**, flown **7 Dec 2017–24 Jan 2018**, with `TQ3580_P_10768_20171207_20180124.laz`, DTM, last-return DSM and first-return DSM names; and **same TQ3580 / P_12151**, flown **12 Dec 2020**, with `TQ3580_P_12151_20201212_20201212.laz` and the same product families. Both recorded raster resolution **1 m**. This is verified metadata for an overlapping footprint, not a download or change measurement.
- Dated last-return DSM layer: 1999 March (2 m), 22 Feb 2003 (1 m, plus a 0.5 m sub-tile), 7–11 March 2007 (0.5 m), 2–16 April 2012 (0.5 m) over the queried footprint. A 2012 cloud record for TQ3780 has 4,514,713 points, approximate spacing 0.460 m, classified=YES and intensity=YES. Historic raster/point-cloud products are not equally complete.

[Reproduce national repeat WFS query](https://environment.data.gov.uk/spatialdata/survey-index-files/wfs?service=WFS&version=2.0.0&request=GetFeature&typeNames=dataset-9f0fa3fc-a860-4729-adc9-47fe53f658d0%3ANational_LIDAR_Programme_Index_Catalogue&outputFormat=application%2Fjson&count=20&srsName=EPSG%3A27700&bbox=537600%2C180200%2C537610%2C180210%2CEPSG%3A27700). Corresponding JSON downloaded successfully (2,014 bytes); dated DSM check 203,618 bytes; cloud check 1,007 bytes. The [reproducible checker](check_western_europe.py) and [published results](evidence/western-europe-checks-results.json) retain the national repeat query; other dated DSM/cloud responses were reviewed in working files.

**License/cost/limits.** Dataset metadata specifies Open Government Licence v3 and the EA attribution statement. [OGL v3 terms](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) permit copying, publishing, redistribution and commercial reuse, with acknowledgment and the required source attribution; do not imply official endorsement. The [point-cloud record](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/977a4ca4-1759-4f26-baa7-b566bd7ca7bf/lidar-time-stamped-point-cloud) specifies: “© Environment Agency copyright and/or database right 2019. All rights reserved.” Retain that dataset statement and the OGL v3 link; use each raster’s own attribution rather than silently changing the year. No public access constraints; no nationality limit found. Anonymous downloads are free. Delivery bundles are 5 km ZIPs with tiled LAZ or GeoTIFF inside; size depends on survey. WFS is for survey metadata, not streaming arbitrary point-cloud subsets. Query small areas and paginate if necessary; no measured global service rate limit established.

**Datum/quality.** OSGB EPSG:27700; heights in metres relative to Ordnance Datum Newlyn. Historical datum transformations differ. This check explicitly found 1999 DSM **OSTN97/OSGM91**, later historic DSMs/cloud **OSTN02/OSGM02**. Modern files need individual transform/geoid metadata checked, not assumed. Official DSM documentation advertises ±15 cm RMSE vertical accuracy; this is not a universal guaranteed change-detection threshold. Stable-surface alignment, residuals, and changed sampling must still be assessed.

**Pilot.** The Docklands repeat is a practical construction/demolition screening pilot. Compare 2017/18 and 2020 first-return DSMs or regrid matching point classes, identify a few major candidates, then inspect planning decisions, building records, dated photography and ownership documents. No conclusion about permissions or legality follows from lidar alone.

## 3. Switzerland — swissSURFACE3D: recommended, current repeat metadata verified

**Access/products.** [Official product page](https://www.swisstopo.admin.ch/en/height-model-swisssurface3d), [STAC collection](https://data.geo.admin.ch/api/stac/v1/collections/ch.swisstopo.swisssurface3d), [browser](https://data.geo.admin.ch/browser/index.html#/collections/ch.swisstopo.swisssurface3d). Classified airborne laser returns represent natural and built objects. 1 km² tiles; older LAS ZIP, newer COPC LAZ. Product page instructs users to uncheck `current` and select LIDAR ZIP to reveal older nationwide data, so a latest-only filter can hide the archive. No account/citizenship restriction encountered.

**Repeat example, sampled.** [Zurich metadata query](https://data.geo.admin.ch/api/stac/v1/collections/ch.swisstopo.swisssurface3d/items?bbox=8.537,47.372,8.54,47.374&limit=100) returned **2682-1247 and 2683-1247 at both 2018 and 2024**, older LAS ZIP and newer COPC. Anonymous HTTP 200; 6,647 bytes. [St Gallen query](https://data.geo.admin.ch/api/stac/v1/collections/ch.swisstopo.swisssurface3d/items?bbox=9.375,47.42,9.38,47.423&limit=100) similarly returned tiles 2746-1253/1254 at 2018 and 2024. Conversely [Chur query](https://data.geo.admin.ch/api/stac/v1/collections/ch.swisstopo.swisssurface3d/items?bbox=9.53,46.85,9.533,46.853&limit=100) returned only 2020 tiles, demonstrating that national update plans cannot be generalized to every place.

For 2024 Zurich tile 2682-1247, [actual COPC asset](https://data.geo.admin.ch/ch.swisstopo.swisssurface3d/swisssurface3d_2024_2682-1247/swisssurface3d_2024_2682-1247_2056_5728.copc.laz) served HTTP206 **bytes0–4095/155767533**; LASF signature, LAS1.4; header bounds x2682000–2682999.99, y1247000–1247999.99. Only 4,096 bytes were read. Compressed point-format flag 134 corresponds to format6 with compression flag; this is not full point decoding. The [checker results](evidence/western-europe-checks-results.json) retain the Zurich pair and bounded header check.

**Date caveat.** STAC datetimes are `2018-01-01`/`2024-01-01`, representing acquisition-year granularity; do not call them exact January1 flights. Created/updated dates are publication/processing dates. Retrieve acquisition information within clouds/documentation before timing a change. National first coverage was acquired in stages, with a 2024–2029 update program; exact years vary by tile and canton. Historical STAC data includes some pre-national cantonal acquisitions (initial collection response returned a 2015 item).

**Rights/cost.** [Official free-data terms](https://www.swisstopo.admin.ch/en/terms-of-use-free-geodata-and-geoservices) permit use, dissemination, transformation and commercial reuse; mandatory credit **“Federal Office of Topography swisstopo”** or **“©swisstopo”**. Anonymous standard downloads free; excessive service use may be restricted. Special delivery/processing may incur fees, excluded from this project. IMPORTANT: product page's separate *sample data* are test-only and cannot be incorporated into products; the checks above used ordinary STAC production assets. No foreign-user bar found.

**Comparison caveats.** Current product page reports LV95/LN02, nominal ±20 cm horizontal and ±10 cm vertical (1 sigma). Older typical mean density 15–20 pts/m², minimum5; new eastern coverage typical25–40, minimum15. Classification changes add roofs/facades, bridge piers and utilities after2024. Regrid consistent classes and inspect density effects; 2018 vs2024 cannot be assumed processing-equivalent. Zurich redevelopment, demolition or industrial roof change is a sensible pilot, supported by permits and dated imagery.

## 4. France — IGN LiDAR HD: strong baseline; repeat not verified

[IGN program](https://www.ign.fr/institut/programme-lidar-hd-vers-une-nouvelle-cartographie-3d-du-territoire) documents aerial high-density laser acquisition begun2021, covering metropolitan France and overseas departments except Guyane. It supplies classified clouds and derived MNT/DTM, MNS/DSM and MNH/height models. Official [old entrypoint](https://geoservices.ign.fr/lidarhd) now redirects to [new cartes.gouv.fr point-cloud catalog](https://cartes.gouv.fr/rechercher-une-donnee/dataset/IGNF_NUAGES-DE-POINTS-LIDAR-HD?redirected_from=geoservices.ign.fr). Dynamic catalog content was not extractable in this browsing run: avoid claiming a tested cloud download. Nationwide completion statements/plans are not evidence of repeat national surveys.

[IGN-published official DSM record](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/mns-lidar-hd) verifies **Licence Ouverte v2**, buildings/vegetation/bridge-deck/ground/water classes; derived 0.5 m GeoTIFFs in1 km tiles (indicative24 MB), advertised ≥10 pulses/m² except5 above3200 m. [DTM record](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/mnt-lidar-hd) confirms ground/water/virtual points, not building heights. Restricted ZICAD areas are omitted/nodata; do not attempt to bypass omissions. These records still describe geographic completion targets, not actual full coverage everywhere.

[Etalab Licence Ouverte v2 text](https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/) permits commercial and editorial reuse/redistribution with source and last-update attribution; cite IGN and the acquired dataset/version/date and distinguish modifications. The DSM's specific license is verified; point-cloud record's exact current terms should be saved from the new catalog before publication of clouds. Official program calls all products free/open; access appears public, with no nationality limit identified, but a point-cloud asset request was not tested here. Vertical references and exact flight dates must be taken from each local acquisition; do not assume a single nationwide datum applies overseas. Older regional lidar may supply repeats, but none was verified in this review. France is a baseline/site-specific follow-up candidate, below verified repeat archives.

## 5. Germany — regional, not a single national repeat archive

**North Rhine-Westphalia.** [3D laser documentation](https://www.bezreg-koeln.nrw.de/system/files/media/document/file/geobasis_hm_3dm_nutzerinfo_3d-messdaten_aus_dem_laserscanning.pdf) confirms classified ALS point clouds, LAZ distribution, LAS1.2/PDRF1 and changes since2017 full-wave acquisition. [Direct free download index](https://www.opengeodata.nrw.de/produkte/geobasis/hm/3dm_l_las/) returnedHTTP200. [April2026 official update](https://www.bezreg-koeln.nrw.de/aktualisierung-der-3d-messdaten) says new2024–2025 laser data cover one fifth of the state. [Official license PDF](https://www.bezreg-koeln.nrw.de/system/files/media/document/file/lizenzbedingungen_geobasis_nrw.pdf) specifies **Datenlizenz Deutschland Zero2.0**, unrestricted use without conditions ([license](https://www.govdata.de/dl-de/zero-2-0)). This validates current point-cloud availability and a rolling update program; **retained historical clouds and a concrete same-footprint pair were not verified**. Anonymous/free; no foreign-user restriction found. Density, exact dates, vertical reference and tile sizes require selected-tile metadata. Beware [bDOM and 3D meshes](https://www.bezreg-koeln.nrw.de/geobasis-nrw/produkte-und-dienste/hoehenmodelle): some are image correlation/photogrammetry, not lidar, despite being in the same height-model family.

**Bavaria.** [Official Laserdaten portal](https://www.geodaten.bayern.de/opengeodata/OpenDataDetail.html?pn=laserdaten) offers airborne LAZ in1 km² tiles, UTM32(EPSG25832), updates by acquisition block, tiles up to1 GB. Polygon download supports metalink, max selection2000 km², direct ZIP up to25 tiles; do not interpret portal “max point count20000” as lidar point-density constraint (likely selection geometry parameter). [Official terms](https://www.geodaten.bayern.de/odd/m/3/html/nutzungsbedingungen.html) specify **CC BY4.0**, credit “Bayerische Vermessungsverwaltung – www.geodaten.bayern.de”, license link, change indication, no endorsement. Free/no license-request needed. No foreign-user limit found; retained historic airborne clouds/repeated dates not established. Vertical datum and classifications remain selected-tile checks. Current baseline promising for extraction or restoration screening, but a repeat pilot should wait until two actual retained epochs are located.

## Evidence boundaries / costs

Small metadata and byte ranges establish that catalogs, overlapping dated asset names and LAS headers are accessible; they do **not** validate classification, full cloud integrity, alignment, exact change magnitudes or complete repeated survey footprints. No paid external API calls, no accounts, no outreach and no giant lidar downloads were used. ChatGPT token attribution is unknown. Failed routes (legacy EA ArcGIS, outdated Swiss api3 STAC path404, occasional swisstopo/Defra503 and a timed-out Defra documentation fetch, IGN dynamic migration) were recorded and alternate official public routes used. Certificate validation stayed enabled.
