# Asia–Pacific and selected African lidar leads

Checked 2026-10-14. Selective primary-source research; not exhaustive. Grades are independent: **V** official documentation verified; **S** small public metadata successfully sampled; **P** promising but unresolved; **N** unsuitable for this no-account, no-payment task. Repeat grade refers to documented same-area surveys, not proof of valid pixel-level change. No paid external calls; token costs unknown.

## Newsroom recommendation

**Start with Auckland, New Zealand.** Its 2013 and 2024 surveys overlap in built Auckland, with actual lidar point clouds and 1m surface models that retain above-ground structures. Licensing is unusually clear on LINZ, and anonymous cloud metadata works. Buildings and excavation can be compared, but a height-datum transformation, classification checks and an unchanged-ground alignment test must precede any change estimate. Hong Kong is a credible second-stage historical archive with two territory-wide surveys, subject to resolving the portal and derivative-publication conditions. Shizuoka is a good open single-survey reference, not yet a verified repeat pair. Australian access varies sharply by state; do not equate ELVIS visibility with commercial reuse permission.

## Auckland: concrete historical comparison and reproducible access

**Official products:** [2013 DSM](https://data.linz.govt.nz/layer/53406-auckland-lidar-1m-dsm-2013/), [2013 index](https://data.linz.govt.nz/layer/53407-auckland-lidar-index-tiles-2013/), [2024 DSM](https://data.linz.govt.nz/layer/121992-auckland-part-1-lidar-1m-dsm-2024/), [2024 index](https://data.linz.govt.nz/layer/121993-auckland-part-1-lidar-index-tiles-2024/), [2024 point cloud](https://data.linz.govt.nz/layer/d3VcCb5rKzNsNGk/auckland-part-1-lidar-point-cloud-2024/).

The 2013 urban-and-adjacent-rural survey covered approximately 2,250km²; LINZ gives acquisition 17 July–23 November 2013, >1.5 points/m² specification, 1m DSM/DEM, classified LAS with intensity/return/GPS time, NZTM2000 horizontal and NZVD2009 vertical coordinates. Specifications: vertical ±0.2m and horizontal ±0.6m at 95% confidence. The 2013 tile index carries `date_flown`; use that per tile. LINZ layers show CC BY 4.0. [2013 official metadata](https://data.linz.govt.nz/layer/53406-auckland-lidar-1m-dsm-2013/).

2024 Part 1 was acquired 30 April–27 June 2024; 1m DSM/DEM and classified point cloud are separately available. Minimum specification: 8 pulses/m², vertical ±0.2m and horizontal ±1m at 95%; vertical NZVD2016, horizontal NZTM2000. Catalog reports 16.93 points/m², LAS 1.4 PDRF6 stored as LAZ COPC, including ground and vegetation classifications; the page exposes additional classifications. Dataset is being refined, so freeze the version. [2024 cloud metadata](https://data.linz.govt.nz/layer/d3VcCb5rKzNsNGk/auckland-part-1-lidar-point-cloud-2024/).

**License and attribution:** LINZ pages explicitly label CC BY 4.0, allow commercial use and require creator attribution. [Legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en): retain attribution/license notices and identify changes; no additional restrictions on licensed rights. AWS survey collection identifies **Regional Software Holdings Limited** as licensor, **Woolpert** producer and LINZ host/processor. Cite these dataset-specific roles, not only the portal. [AWS documentation and attribution format](https://registry.opendata.aws/nz-elevation/) gives anonymous no-account access and says copyright varies by survey. Its elevation bucket contains DSM/DEM COGs and STAC, not point clouds in the documented initial release. Foreign-user restrictions were not found; global CC licensing and anonymous access support use abroad, but do not imply a service guarantee.

**Older point-cloud archive caveat:** [OpenTopography Auckland 2013](https://portal.opentopography.org/datasetMetadata?otCollectionID=OT.062016.2193.1) publishes 4.76 points/m² and building class 6, but records CC BY 3.0 New Zealand in acknowledgement while its separate use-license field is empty. This differs from current LINZ raster/index CC BY 4.0. Preserve the older point-cloud terms for that route, cite Auckland Council and OpenTopography/DOI [10.5069/G9KW5CZ5](https://doi.org/10.5069/G9KW5CZ5), and check archive notices before redistributing. Metadata date starts 29 July, unlike LINZ's 17 July start: project dates are not a tile flight date. [CC BY 3.0 NZ legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/nz/legalcode).

**Small checks actually made:** Anonymous HTTPS listing of `nz-elevation` succeeded without account or API key. It lists Auckland North 2016–2018, South 2016–2017, Part 1 and Part 2 2024; no Auckland 2013 prefix under this region listing, so do not promise AWS has the older epoch. Downloaded only XML listings, 40,860-byte 2013 GeoJSON boundary, 1,491-byte 2024 tile STAC and 24,242-byte collection metadata. A ray-casting point-in-polygon check at longitude 174.72, latitude −36.85 returns true for both 2013 survey polygon and 2024 `BA31_10000_0405` tile geometry. This verifies shared documented footprint in Auckland's western urban area; no pixels or LAS points were tested and a tile polygon can include no-data.

**Pilot:** Take a small western Auckland industrial/urban tile overlap, transform NZVD2009 to NZVD2016 using an official applicable transformation grid, compare DSM first, then inspect building-class points. Confirm old/new classification and overlap-return handling. Pair candidate construction/demolition/earthworks with historical aerial photos, planning consents and ownership documents. A lidar height change alone does not establish illegality or production. [LINZ survey index](https://data.linz.govt.nz/layer/104252-nz-elevation-survey-index/) warns raster survey extents are only approximate indicators of point-cloud coverage; intersect actual cloud extents too. [LINZ access guidance](https://www.linz.govt.nz/products-services/data/types-linz-data/elevation-data/access-elevation-data) confirms free elevation/cloud downloads and streams. LDS export/API account requirements and cloud tile size limits were not tested; anonymous AWS COG access is the verified no-account raster route.

## Hong Kong: valuable repeat archive, unresolved publication/access details

[CEDD official archive](https://www.ginfo.cedd.gov.hk/GEOOpenData/eng/LIDAR.aspx) directs the public to [Spatial Data Portal](https://sdportal.cedd.gov.hk/#/en/). [Official CSDI metadata](https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/csdi-webpage/dataset/cedd_rcd_1629267205233_87895) confirms both surveys cover Hong Kong Island, Kowloon, New Territories and Lantau: fixed-wing 1 December 2010–8 January 2011; helicopter 20 December 2019–2 February 2020. This is genuine territory-wide repeat acquisition, not separate adjacent regional dates. Reclamation and major construction could be historical newsroom applications.

The public portal HTML and 99,359-byte public application JS were retrieved successfully. Product menus distinguish LAS all points/ground and raster DSM/DTM for both years; 2020 also offers building-class LAS. These are actual airborne lidar products. Two metadata map URLs inferred from relative public JS returned HTTP 404; no download was completed. Density, vertical datum, per-tile resolution/accuracy, account requirement, foreign eligibility and order/size limits remain unverified. Do not promote this to a runnable pilot yet.

**Terms:** [GEO Open Data terms](https://www.ginfo.cedd.gov.hk/GEOOpenData/Disclaimer_en.html) permit free commercial/noncommercial browsing, download, distribution and reproduction **in original format**, require source/government ownership attribution, and contain indemnity and disclaimer clauses. [DATA.GOV.HK terms](https://data.gov.hk/en/terms-and-conditions) permit commercial copies too but direct users to external-site terms; [actual lidar listing](https://data.gov.hk/en-data/dataset/hk-cedd-csu-lidar/resource/628b8d02-b688-4f2a-88af-003efadaee15) points onward. It is unclear whether a transformed point-cloud subset or derived change map falls within these grants; don't assert legal certainty. Public JS also contains contractor-order terms for separate digital-map/UAV products, so do not assume those restrictions apply identically to open lidar, or ignore lidar's own terms. No generic CC license was verified.

## Japan Virtual Shizuoka: open points, repeat not established

Publisher [2019 southeast Fuji/eastern Izu](https://www.geospatial.jp/ckan/dataset/shizuoka-2019-pointcloud), [central/west](https://www.geospatial.jp/ckan/dataset/virtual-shizuoka-mw) and [northwest 2025](https://www.geospatial.jp/ckan/dataset/shizuoka-2025-pointcloud) pages label **CC BY 4.0 / ODbL dual licensing**. [CC BY conditions](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) require attribution/link/change disclosure; [ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) can add database share-alike obligations when that route is used. Confirm the dual-license choice for any resource before publication.

Pages separately offer airborne laser LP original points, ground points and 0.5m terrain grids; also ALB bathymetric and MMS vehicle data. Do not call MMS airborne or the ground grid DSM. LAS ZIP/7z tiles average hundreds of MB and can exceed 5GB; choose metadata first. JGD2011 / Japan plane rectangular zone VIII is stated; vertical height reference and class schema require specifications. Acquisition-date spreadsheets/CSV exist. Updates include new 2024–25 central/west meshes and replacement color data; neither addition nor reprocessing establishes new surveys of the same footprint. No repeated tile pair verified. No account or nationality limit is described, but download success was not verified. The direct public CKAN API returned 403; no bypass attempted. Good industrial/urban baseline, conditional future repeat search.

## Australia: search ELVIS carefully, no nationwide time series

[ELVIS](https://elevation.fsdf.org.au/) offers no-charge point/elevation downloads, **15GB per request**, using emailed ZIP links. No order submitted: this task forbids outreach/accounts and should not invent or publish an email. [Geoscience Australia documentation](https://www.ga.gov.au/scientific-topics/national-location-information/digital-elevation-data) describes multi-jurisdiction holdings; the 5m national lidar DEM is bare earth from many 2001–2015 projects, not a repeated nationwide building surface. National SRTM products are satellite radar, not airborne lidar.

One specific verified CC BY 4.0 point-cloud record is [L0106 Sydney North](https://ecat.ga.gov.au/geonetwork/srv/api/records/0cb98f82-d542-97de-e053-12a3070a69bc): LAS 1.2 airborne ALS50, minimum one pulse/m² and 0.3m horizontal 95% accuracy. Its metadata publication year 2013 is not an independently established flight date. The record's lineage is unknown; no newer matching footprint was confirmed and height datum needs the actual tile/project metadata. Attribute the stated custodian and retain source/license links before redistribution; creator information needs careful checking. Foreign restrictions not found in this unclassified CC-licensed record. Actual tiles not downloaded.

[NSW official theme](https://data.nsw.gov.au/data/dataset/1-73a47df530fe42a0a1d3eabb43defcf1) explicitly mixes lidar and photogrammetric point clouds and gives 1–2m lidar-derived bare-earth DEMs; the theme's license is **not specified**. Select actual lidar projects, never infer sensor/license from the theme label. Datum and flight dates must be read per dataset. [Victoria official catalogue](https://www.land.vic.gov.au/maps-and-spatial/spatial-data/vicmap-catalogue/vicmap-elevation) states point/DEM archives require licensed use and coverage-based fees; exceptions on ELVIS are CC BY-NC. Archive spans 2007–present, 2–24 points/m², GDA94/GDA2020 and ellipsoidal/AHD heights. CC BY-NC should not be assumed safe for commercially funded journalism. Exclude under this zero-payment task unless a specific suitable grant exists.

## Africa and other weak leads

**Cape Town:** [2019 ground-level-map contours](https://odp-cctegis.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/cctegis%3A%3Acity-of-cape-town-ground-level-map-2m-contours-cct-glm-2019/about) derive from 2018–19 lidar, 2–3 points/m², 0.1m vertical accuracy (95%); 5m bare-earth DEM accuracy 0.3m, height SA Land Levelling Datum with SAGEOID2010. [Newer municipal contours](https://cdn.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=d179f9e0faf145a2957617da1b231892) document northern flights December 2021 and southern January 2023, 10 points/m², 0.1m vertical accuracy and same named levelling datum. This documents repeated municipal acquisition but publicly retrieved products were **contours**, not archived LAS/DSM pairs. Live-service metadata dates do not equal survey dates. [REST route](https://citymaps.capetown.gov.za/agsext/rest/services/Theme_Based/ODP_SPLIT_2/FeatureServer/0) is identified by the official item; not sampled here. [Open-data terms](https://www.capetown.gov.za/general/city-of-cape-town-open-data-terms-of-use/) say no remuneration and disclaim warranties but a clear point-cloud derivative/redistribution license was not verified. Foreign restrictions and API quotas unknown. Useful earthworks lead; not a validated structural comparison source.

**Philippines:** [NAMRIA free-products workflow](https://namria.gov.ph/kiosk/namria02.htm) requires ID, request letter and processing/outreach for requested products, while some digital downloads are free. No unattended licensed repeat airborne point-cloud pair was verified. Do not claim absence of national lidar, only absence of a suitable verified route in this selective search. Taiwan's [NLSC open-information statement](https://www.nlsc.gov.tw/en/cp.aspx?n=2173) is worldwide OGDL with attribution and exceptions; it does not prove any specific publicly downloadable repeated airborne cloud is covered. No recommendation follows from a generic website license.

All failed routes were left as failures. No certificate warning was bypassed, no access control circumvented, no account created and no giant tile downloaded.
