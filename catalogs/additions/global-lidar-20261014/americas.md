# Americas research notes — checked 14 October 2026

## Newsroom lead

**Vancouver is the best verified North American city pilot found in this pass:** public LAS archives for 2013, 2018 and 2022; permissive worldwide commercial reuse; an actual matching tile verified across all three survey footprints using tiny public API responses. New York City has a strong 2013/14→2017 archive pair, with a more complicated processing history and licence trail for the city-funded 2017 source. USGS 3DEP and NOAA Digital Coast are excellent free discovery/bulk gateways, but neither guarantees repeated surveys at every site. Brazil, Mexico and Uruguay offer worthwhile additional leads; a second matching epoch was not verified for them. Bogotá illustrates why “open data” alone is insufficient: its lidar-derived DSM record carries a noncommercial licence.

Evidence labels below distinguish **R1** (same footprint repeat established by matching metadata polygons), **R2** (repeat city/program coverage documented; exact selected-tile overlap not sampled), **R0** (no repeat established), and **L-clear/L-qualified/L-hold** (reuse evidence). These are research grades, not legal opinions. No accounts, outreach, paid services, giant downloads or certificate bypasses were used.

## 1. Vancouver, Canada — R1 / L-clear / recommended first pilot

Official archive entries: [2013](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/lidar-2013/), [2018](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/lidar-2018/), [2022](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/assets/lidar-2022/).

- 2013: city jurisdiction, 168 tiles, acquired 7/15/19 February; classified LAS and GeoTIFF ground/upper-surface products. At least 12 points/m², reported vertical accuracy 0.18m at 95%. Class list includes ground, vegetation, water, buildings.
- 2018: city plus UBC Endowment Lands, 134km², 181 tiles, acquired 27–28 August; classified LAS, average 30 points/m², reported vertical accuracy 0.18m at 95%. Classes include buildings, ground, vegetation, water, noise and other/unclassified.
- 2022: same stated 134km² and 181-tile extent, acquired 7/9 September; LAS, mean about 49 points/m², reported vertical accuracy 0.081m at 95%. Same broad classes as 2018. Portal reports ZIP sizes 16.45MB–2.74GB; download only selected tiles.
- Coordinates: LAS uses UTM10 / NAD83(CSRS), CGVD28 and Metro Vancouver geoid. 2018/2022 specify NAD83(CSRS) 4.0.0.BC.1.GVRD. Map index is WGS84; do not treat map coordinates as LAS CRS. Compare epoch/geoid/header details before differencing.

**Concrete repeat check:** 2013/2018 tile `4830E_54540N` and 2022 tile `483000_5454000` cover the same square around -123.226692,49.243052. The 2018/2022 API polygon vertices match exactly up to rotation; 2013 differs by tiny coordinate rounding/transformation. This establishes footprint overlap, not valid surface-change measurements.

Reproducible metadata endpoints (anonymous; no key used):

- [2013 single record](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/lidar-2013/records?where=name%3D%224830E_54540N%22&limit=1)
- [2018 single record](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/lidar-2018/records?where=name%3D%224830E_54540N%22&limit=1)
- [2022 single record](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/lidar-2022/records?where=name%3D%22483000_5454000%22&limit=1)

Direct tile routes: [2013 ZIP](https://webtransfer.vancouver.ca/opendata/2013LiDAR/COV_4830E_54540N.zip), [2018 ZIP](https://webtransfer.vancouver.ca/opendata/2018LiDAR/4830E_54540N.zip), [2022 ZIP](https://webtransfer.vancouver.ca/opendata/2022LiDAR/483000_5454000.zip). Only metadata downloaded; these ZIP payloads were not tested.

[Open Government Licence–Vancouver v1.0](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/pages/licence/) grants worldwide, royalty-free commercial use, copying, adaptation, publication and distribution. Credit the source and link the licence where possible; default attribution: “Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Vancouver.” No endorsement; excludes personal information, unauthorized third-party rights, official marks and other protected intellectual property. Free anonymous portal/API observed, foreign eligibility explicitly supported by worldwide grant. Numerical API throttling/quota was not established. API responses were 512–590 bytes for sampled one-record requests. Licence content is embedded in HTML and poorly extracted by the browser; Python HTTPS retrieval verified it.

Pilot: choose an industrial/redevelopment block with documentary significance; compare 2018→2022 first (both late-summer acquisitions), then add 2013 after vegetation/season checks. LAS building classes can assist, but verify class consistency and compute comparable upper-surface DSMs. The matched example is a technical footprint check, not an identified story.

## 2. New York City, United States — R2 / L-qualified

**Recommended working pair:** [2013–2014 USGS Post-Sandy point clouds, InPort 49891](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/49891) and [2017 NYC topobathymetric point clouds, InPort 64728](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/64728). Both explicitly cover the five borough counties. Download [2013/14 bulk tile index](https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/laz/geoid18/4920/index.html) and [2017 bulk tile index](https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/laz/geoid18/9306/index.html); indexes successfully retrieved, about 49KB/135KB. No tile payload retrieved.

2013/14 includes 2013 Manhattan cross flights and March–April 2014 acquisition, nominal pulse spacing 0.7m; classified LAS (NOAA LAZ), plus original project bare-earth DEM/intensity/breakline products. NOAA says finalized DEMs were not received. It explicitly lists no use constraints, appreciates USGS acknowledgement, and warns about tide/hydrologic interpolation artifacts. Use actual LAS times to separate mixed epochs. The programme purpose included storm damage and erosion.

2017: topographic acquisition May, bathymetry July; LAS1.4, ground/water/noise/bridge-deck and custom classes, with building returns potentially in unclassified rather than a building class. Ground accuracy reported 0.074m at 95%; this is not roof-edge accuracy. The [2017 DEM](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/64732/) is bare earth with human features removed—unsuitable alone for demolition/expansion. Do not confuse it with a highest-hit DSM.

**Datum warning:** both fetched bulk indexes state NAD83(2011)/UTM18N and NAVD88/GEOID18 metres. InPort's generic distribution wording says geographic coordinates; actual index differs. Original municipal products can use state-plane US survey feet and another geoid. Read LAS headers/metadata rather than assuming all routes deliver the same datum.

Cost/access: NOAA direct HTTPS bulk tiles free, anonymous; no foreign restriction documented. [Digital Coast](https://coast.noaa.gov/digitalcoast/data/coastallidar.html) offers customization up to 1.5 billion points with results emailed; the direct indexes avoid email submission. Current numerical direct-download quota unknown. NOAA collects heterogeneous-provider data, not a universal licence grant. 2017 metadata records “no access constraints” and only accuracy/time disclaimers for use, credits NYC OTI predecessor. **Affirmative city raw-data redistribution licence remains a check before republication**; do not equate public access with public domain. 2013/14 is stronger via explicit no-use-constraints and USGS source.

**2010 caveat:** [NYC's own lidar summary](https://github.com/CityOfNewYork/nyc-geo-metadata/blob/main/Metadata/Metadata_LiDAR_Summary.md) documents April14–May1 2010 and May/July2017 city surveys and calls out 2010 classified LAS1.2, but does not provide a functioning direct point-cloud link in that row. Its separately public 2010 1-foot DEM is bare-earth. Thus 2010→2017 historical city repeat is documented; **public 2010 point-cloud download was not verified**. NOAA's [2010 USACE New York source](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/54358) is a coastal strip, not the municipal full-city dataset. Do not substitute them.

## 3. United States national gateways — R0 at any unspecified site / L-clear for 3DEP

[USGS 3DEP](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program) says products are free without use restrictions. Its [official point-cloud collection](https://data.usgs.gov/datacatalog/data/USGS%3Ab7e353d2-325f-4fc6-8d95-01254705638a) explicitly says all 3DEP products are public domain, original points/reference systems preserved, LAZ. [National Map downloader](https://apps.nationalmap.gov/downloader/) and [TNM API](https://tnmaccess.nationalmap.gov/api/v1/docs) provide project/tile discovery. Building analysis needs points/DSMs, not its bare-earth DEMs. Accuracy/density/date/classification are per project; USGS's national baseline includes IfSAR rather than lidar in much of Alaska. USGS's [next-generation plan](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/cir1553/full) describes improved refresh as future/program design, not already complete repeats everywhere. No account/foreign restriction apparent; sampled national API not tested in this worker.

[NOAA coastal archive](https://coast.noaa.gov/digitalcoast/data/coastallidar.html) spans 1996 onward, heterogeneous projects, some many epochs and some no coverage. Point clouds, DEMs, contours, original bulk LAZ. Its page says lidar storage may involve datum/projection normalization and bare-earth processing. Validate source-specific terms and processing. Helpful for land reclamation, storm reconstruction and excavation; select footprint intersection before comparing years. Preserve the Washington source guide already committed by the parent.

## 4. Canada beyond Vancouver — R0 / L-clear; useful discovery

[NRCan CanElevation point clouds](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/7069387e-9986-4297-9f55-0288e9676947) are actual airborne lidar LAZ/COPC, organized by project without integration; points standardized to NAD83(CSRS) epoch2010 / UTM and CGVD2013. Public [tile GPKG](https://canelevation-lidar-point-clouds.s3-ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/pointclouds_nuagespoints/Index_LiDARtiles_tuileslidar.gpkg) and [product specification PDF](https://canelevation-lidar-point-clouds.s3-ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/pointclouds_nuagespoints/CanElevation-LiDARPointClouds_products_specs_EN.pdf) link from the record. HTTP COPC enables selective access. Regional dates/density/classes must be queried; no concrete repeat pair established. [Open Government Licence–Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada) permits worldwide commercial publication/distribution with source/licence attribution, no endorsement, exclusions for protected third-party material. Free; no account requirement recorded; exact throttle limits not verified. Browser opening licence once failed, official search retried successfully.

[Québec airborne LAZ/MNT](https://www.donneesquebec.ca/recherche/fr/dataset/donnees-lidar-du-quebec) lists CC-BY4.0 and Forêt ouverte/map-download routes. Coverage described as all Québec; acquisition footprint/date must come from index, not catalogue update. MTM / NAD83(SCRS) epoch1997; majority CGVD28/HTv2.0. It is irregular rolling/partner coverage, not verified repeat coverage. Attribution, licence link and changes must accompany redistribution under [CC-BY4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); worldwide. No download/account/cost barriers indicated; payload not sampled. Potential industrial/forestry/land-use applications only after establishing second date.

## 5. Latin America — selective inventory and holds

**São Paulo, Brazil — R0 / L-qualified:** [official 2020 city announcement](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/se/w/noticias/104836) documents 2017 aerial laser acquisition, LAZ MDS/MDT access, roughly10pts/m² and10cm stated precision. [City metadata](https://metadados.geosampa.prefeitura.sp.gov.br/geonetwork/srv/api/records/03944df3-cd3c-4ea1-bbe8-44732d6f8be4) instead gives Feb–June2017, nominal8pts/m²; retain disagreement, inspect flight/tile report. GeoSampa provides surface and ground point products; some normalized MDS−MDT products express height above ground, not absolute elevation.

[Provider-submitted AWS registry](https://registry.opendata.aws/pmsp-lidar/) lists anonymous LAZ `s3://laz-m3dc-pmsp/` and EPT `s3://ept-m3dc-pmsp/` routes using `--no-sign-request`, GNU GPLv3 and citation wording. A [current official viewer](https://novogeosampa.prefeitura.sp.gov.br/) footer says CC-BY-SA, while index metadata flags intellectual-property rights. **Do not silently substitute CC-BY4.0 or a software licence for data reuse certainty.** GNU GPLv3 is unusual for data; confirm exact distribution/derivative obligations before publishing raw subsets. Current catalogue date is not a second acquisition: repeat NOT verified. Free foreign anonymous access supported for AWS route; exact rate limits unknown; payload not sampled. Useful built-environment baseline, not a ready change pilot.

**Mexico INEGI — R0 / L-clear for identified INEGI products:** [MDE programme](https://www.inegi.org.mx/programas/mde/) mixes lidar, photogrammetry, satellite and radar; its catalogue's “LiDAR” collection can include products titled satellite/airborne remote-sensor-derived. [Lidar-specific documentation](https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/programas/mde/doc/lidar_alta_resolucion.pdf) describes 5m surface and terrain rasters; DTM removes buildings, DSM retains surface objects. Do not claim raw publicly downloaded lidar points or same-footprint repeat. [Map downloads](https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/mapas/?tg=1016) show acquisition versus edition dates separately. [Free-use terms](https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/inegi/doc/terminos_info.pdf) permit copying, publication, adaptation and commercial products; preserve metadata, credit INEGI/product/update, identify modifications and avoid endorsement. No nationality condition found; free anonymous maps; quota not established. Resolution may support large excavation/land reclamation but not subtle building change.

**Minas, Uruguay — R0 / L-clear / small built-heritage lead:** [official December2025 flight resource](https://catalogodatos.gub.uy/dataset/ide-vuelo-fotogrametrico-minas-diciembre-2025/resource/55df73b0-3411-4c28-8436-d229e756392e) links lidar points for four heritage blocks separately from photorealistic3D products. The 1,666-byte GeoJSON download succeeded and contains [classified LAS](https://visualizador.ide.uy/descargas/datos/Vuelo_Minas_2025/LiDAR/nube_LiDAR_clasificada.las) and [metadata](https://visualizador.ide.uy/geonetwork/srv/api/records/ba943752-022c-44f7-81a2-af08dbd6c337/formatters/xml). XML fetched (30,939bytes); says IDE holds rights and grants Uruguay open-data licence. [Exact licence v0.1 PDF](https://www.gub.uy/agencia-gobierno-electronico-sociedad-informacion-conocimiento/sites/agencia-gobierno-electronico-sociedad-informacion-conocimiento/files/documentos/publicaciones/licencia_de_datos_abiertos_0.pdf) allows commercial/noncommercial reproduction, distribution, publication, transformation and combination; cite provider, licence and dataset, disclose changes. No citizenship restriction stated. Catalog EPSG4326 applies to grid, not necessarily LAS. Density, LAS vertical datum, airborne sensor/platform and repeat epoch not verified; no payload downloaded. Free public route; quota unknown. Do not promote to airborne repeat shortlist without technical metadata completion.

**Bogotá, Colombia — R0 / L-hold for commercial newsroom:** [official lidar-derived urban DSM](https://www.ideca.gov.co/recursos/mapas/modelo-digital-de-superficie-zona-urbana-de-bogota), [dataset/access services](https://datosabiertos.bogota.gov.co/dataset/modelo-digital-de-superficie-zona-urbana-de-bogota) list CC-BY-NC-SA4.0 and REST/WMS. [Rural DTM](https://www.ideca.gov.co/recursos/mapas/modelo-digital-de-terreno-zona-rural) describes 2020 lidar,0.5m raster. Latest “2022” DSM date is update, not independently verified flight. Repeat/raw points unknown; datums/resolution/exports need inspection. **Noncommercial restriction makes commercial newspaper redistribution a no-go without separate permission**; this pass involved no contact. Newer general reference-map CC-BY record does not automatically relicense this historical DSM.

## Reproducibility and cost

The [Vancouver checker](check_vancouver_repeat.py) and [results](evidence/vancouver-repeat-check.json) preserve exact named-tile queries. Other working payloads were not published because official HTML/XML can include contact addresses; the exact public routes and retrieval limits are documented above. No LAZ/LAS ZIP payload downloaded, so headers/class counts/cloud alignment not yet validated. Zero paid external calls; ChatGPT token attribution unknown/unmeasured. No legal advice, no exhaustiveness claim, no current military monitoring, no account creation or outreach.
