# Additional UK archives

Reviewed 14 October 2026. These supplement the verified England pilot; no matching historical tile payloads were tested here.

**Scotland — project archive, product-specific reuse.** The [Scottish Government catalog](https://remotesensingdata.gov.scot/data) lists project LAS/LAZ, DSM and DTM, including 2011–12 and later power-network surveys. Successive phase dates do not establish overlapping usable points at a chosen site. The publisher-managed [anonymous distribution registry](https://registry.opendata.aws/scottish-lidar/) documents `s3://srsp-open-data/` with `--no-sign-request`, OGL v3 **except phase 2 LAZ, which is noncommercial**. Check that exact project/product's terms. Numerical throttles and foreign-user eligibility were not separately tested; the anonymous route needs no AWS account. OGL allows commercial derivatives and redistribution with source attribution; [OGL v3 terms](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) exclude third-party rights and endorsement. Do not apply raster permission automatically to raw clouds. Datum, density, classification and flight dates are project-specific; no pair was selected.

The [2025 capture LAS catalog](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/80187dd8-6cae-47fb-b712-8afd98b7b3c9/scottish-land-lidar-programme-2025-capture-las), updated September 2026, describes the 2025–27 national programme, 10 points/m², LAZ and DSM/DTM. It lists Crown copyright attribution but a blank license field. This programme is a baseline/update lead, not proof of complete published repeat coverage or a substitute for product terms. No sample downloaded.

**Wales — useful dated surface rasters.** The [official viewer](https://datamap.gov.wales/maps/lidar-viewer/) exposes both Historic LiDAR Archive and Welsh Government survey tile links, plus DSM/DTM COGs and OWS routes. The map text says 2020–22 while the tile catalog says 2020–23; use flight metadata rather than resolving that difference by assumption. [Download metadata](https://datamap.gov.wales/maps/lidar-data-download/metadata_detail) explicitly marks OGL and EPSG:27700; vertical datum remains a tile/project check. Current products include 1m DSMs suitable for large buildings and earthworks, whereas DTM removes above-ground objects. Clouds and exact repeated tile intersections were not verified. Some [tile metadata](https://datamap.gov.wales/layers/geonode%3Awelsh_government_lidar_tile_catalogue_2020_2023/metadata_detail) omit a license: preserve product-level terms before raw redistribution. No fee/account is described for linked public COGs; foreign conditions and numeric limits unverified. Stream a small COG window instead of downloading the whole national mosaic.
