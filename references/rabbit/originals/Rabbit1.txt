# Reconciled Investigative Public-Record API Catalogue

NotebookLM-optimized edition | Generated 2026-08-19

## Scope and provenance

This document converts the intact `13aug2026_reconciled_investigative_api_catalogue.xlsx` into one searchable Markdown source. The later file `14aug26v1.xlsx` was not incorporated because its ZIP container is truncated and cannot be read as a complete workbook. The source workbook itself was not altered.

The document preserves the catalogue's stable relational identifiers: source systems (`SYS`), access points (`AP`), system-to-jurisdiction and record-family mappings (`MAP`), jurisdictions (`JUR`), and record families (`RF`). Each source-system section brings the related access, coverage, ranking, verification, and evidence records together.

### Interpretation rules

- `PARTIAL` means that active source presence was evidenced. It does not mean complete fields, complete history, permission to automate collection, or permission to republish raw records.
- `DISCOVERY_ONLY` means that a source is retained as a lead but excluded from active coverage, usually because access, price, credentials, licensing, or technical operation remains conditional.
- HTTP success proves reachability only. It does not prove dataset completeness, authenticity of every record, legality of a proposed use, or permission to scrape.
- Endpoint counts are informational. Multiple endpoints do not increase a system's coverage score.
- A missing source means missing from this catalogue, not necessarily absent from the public record.

## Methodology

### Scope
- **Rule or definition:** Prompt 2 restructures and scores the Prompt 1 catalogue; it performs no source discovery.
- **Implementation:** 484 source systems and 1245 principal access-point rows were used.
- **Caution or source:** A missing source means missing from this workbook, not necessarily missing from public records.

### Jurisdiction universe
- **Rule or definition:** All ISO-style country/territory codes, Kosovo as a practical separate jurisdiction, selected decentralized first-level units, and explicit local-layer placeholders.
- **Implementation:** 643 jurisdiction/control rows.
- **Caution or source:** UN members: https://www.un.org/en/about-us/member-states | UN M49: https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/ | ISO 3166: https://www.iso.org/iso-3166-country-codes.html

### Subnational scope
- **Rule or definition:** United States states, Canadian provinces/territories, Australian states/territories, UK constituent nations and selected comparable decentralized systems are individually represented.
- **Implementation:** Local units remain labelled placeholders unless enumerated.
- **Caution or source:** Do not infer county or municipal coverage from a state or national source.

### Record families
- **Rule or definition:** Thirty-three controlled families separate direct records from contextual data.
- **Implementation:** Weights and definitions are editable in RECORD_FAMILIES.
- **Caution or source:** One source system may map to several families; endpoint counts never increase system counts.

### Evidence map
- **Rule or definition:** SYSTEM_COVERAGE_MAP links each unique source system to normalized jurisdiction and record family.
- **Implementation:** Family mappings use Prompt 1 system names, publishers and coarse families; jurisdiction and access mappings use the normalized system fields.
- **Caution or source:** Query wording and endpoint counts are excluded from family classification so a search topic cannot masquerade as primary-record coverage.

### Complete
- **Rule or definition:** Requires a manual audit of the statutory universe, exclusions, history and access route.
- **Implementation:** No cell is automatically marked Complete.
- **Caution or source:** Presence of one official system is not proof of completeness.

### Partial
- **Rule or definition:** At least one direct system maps to the exact jurisdiction and family.
- **Implementation:** Exact evidence only; global or parent systems do not qualify.
- **Caution or source:** Partial means presence, not breadth, accuracy or legal reuse permission.

### Unknown
- **Rule or definition:** Used when only global, regional, EU/EEA, parent-jurisdiction or local-placeholder evidence might apply.
- **Implementation:** Uncertainty is preserved explicitly.
- **Caution or source:** Unknown must not be counted as coverage.

### Absent
- **Rule or definition:** No exact, parent or plausible aggregate system-family evidence appears in this catalogue.
- **Implementation:** Derived from the evidence map.
- **Caution or source:** Absent is a catalogue gap, not proof that no external source exists.

### Discovery-only / inaccessible
- **Rule or definition:** Discovery-only lacks an underlying direct source; inaccessible records only a restricted/manual/gap route.
- **Implementation:** Classified conservatively from Prompt 1 access and review text.
- **Caution or source:** Prompt 9 must verify access and legal reality.

### Evidence-quality score
- **Rule or definition:** 0–100 score from public availability, source authority, historic depth and person/entity linkage.
- **Implementation:** 20 × (0.30×availability + 0.25×authority + 0.20×history + 0.25×linkage).
- **Caution or source:** Availability, authority and history are conservative rule-based values from workbook metadata.

### Gap score
- **Rule or definition:** 0–100 triage score combining HNWI relevance, status deficiency and evidence weaknesses.
- **Implementation:** 100 × (relevance/5) × status factor × [0.25 + 0.20(1−availability/5) + 0.20(1−authority/5) + 0.15(1−history/5) + 0.20(linkage/5)].
- **Caution or source:** Weights are editable assumptions, not empirical probabilities.

### Priority tiers
- **Rule or definition:** Critical ≥75; High ≥55; Medium ≥35; Low <35.
- **Implementation:** Formula-driven in COVERAGE_MATRIX.
- **Caution or source:** Use filters and editorial judgment; tied scores are common.

### Source hierarchy
- **Rule or definition:** Official sources are preferred; authoritative nonprofit sources supplement; aggregators do not substitute for official records.
- **Implementation:** Authority score ranges 1–5.
- **Caution or source:** Prompt 1 publisher fields are incomplete, so authority confidence is limited.

### Historic depth
- **Rule or definition:** Score 4 when metadata signals archives, historic series, annual records or backfiles; 2 for mainly current periodic data; 1 when unstated.
- **Implementation:** Rule-based from source names and access/review text.
- **Caution or source:** Prompt 9 must verify actual start dates and retention.

### Person/entity linkage
- **Rule or definition:** Family-level assumption measuring whether records commonly support named-person or named-entity joins.
- **Implementation:** Editable in RECORD_FAMILIES.
- **Caution or source:** It does not claim that every source exposes personal data.

## Reconciliation method and limitations

### Input control
- **Rule:** Compare shared sheets before merge
- **Implementation:** Twelve substantive shared sheets matched exactly; START HERE differed only because each team linked its own additions.
- **Residual limitation:** Formatting hashes were not used as source identity.

### Source identity
- **Rule:** Merge only an established system match or a same documented platform
- **Implementation:** Seven candidates merged into six Prompt 10 systems. Six duplicate-URL pairs became six new systems with separate access rows.
- **Residual limitation:** A publisher may operate several distinct systems; shared publisher or domain alone was not merged.

### Endpoint retention
- **Rule:** Keep one candidate-level access and evidence row
- **Implementation:** All 300 alternate records received stable AP identifiers even where the system was merged.
- **Residual limitation:** Endpoint counts are informational and do not increase coverage or source rank.

### Jurisdiction
- **Rule:** Use existing exact IDs; otherwise use parent or aggregate
- **Implementation:** All 300 candidates resolved. City and local sources use an existing subnational ID when available, otherwise their country.
- **Residual limitation:** Parent-country mapping does not claim national completeness.

### Record families
- **Rule:** Prefer the existing controlled family; add only genuine omissions
- **Implementation:** Five families added: policing/criminal justice; social care; migration/borders; municipal services; health-care delivery/ownership.
- **Residual limitation:** Public-official assets, property, professional licensing, platforms and crypto map to existing specific families.

### Verification
- **Rule:** Do not promote the alternate team's labels beyond their evidence
- **Implementation:** PASS → PARTIAL active presence. CONDITIONAL → DISCOVERY_ONLY and no active coverage.
- **Residual limitation:** External checks and operative legal terms were not repeated during this reconciliation.

### Coverage
- **Rule:** Rank unique systems and exact jurisdiction × family cells
- **Implementation:** Mappings are deduplicated before the matrix is updated. New endpoints cannot inflate coverage.
- **Residual limitation:** PARTIAL does not mean complete fields, full history or legal permission to automate.

### Legal use
- **Rule:** Public access is separate from automation and republication rights
- **Implementation:** Authentication, rate limits, terms, copyright, database rights and privacy cautions remain in verification and access rows.
- **Residual limitation:** Researchers must recheck current operative terms and must not bypass controls.

### Updating
- **Rule:** Edit stable IDs rather than append a replacement record
- **Implementation:** Use RECONCILIATION LOG to trace GAP IDs to final SYS and AP IDs.
- **Residual limitation:** Create a new system only when source identity—not merely its URL—has changed.

## Legal and methodological guardrails

### Tier A
- **Meaning:** Direct public or official interface/bulk release with comparatively clear reuse route.
- **Required practice:** Archive terms/licence, query, source file and hash; respect attribution and personal-data law.

### Tier B
- **Meaning:** Free registration, token, quota or bounded platform terms; still reproducible by peers.
- **Required practice:** Use the documented interface; do not evade limits; record account class, quota and terms version.

### Tier C
- **Meaning:** Research approval, non-commercial licence, manual access or material permission uncertainty.
- **Required practice:** Obtain written permission or legal/editorial review before automated collection or publication reuse.

### Tier D
- **Meaning:** Leak/publisher-mediated or otherwise high-sensitivity route from the original catalogue.
- **Required practice:** Use through an established publisher and counsel; do not redistribute raw personal or restricted data.

### Modelled spatial data
- **Meaning:** Building, population, wealth, land-cover, emissions and hazard layers are estimates.
- **Required practice:** Retain confidence/uncertainty, reference year, processing version and original image or record used to validate.

### Non-detection
- **Meaning:** Clouds, sensor thresholds, orbit, sampling or platform coverage can produce missing observations.
- **Required practice:** State 'not observed in this dataset/time window', never 'did not happen'.

### Entity resolution
- **Meaning:** Names, addresses and coordinates are not stable identities.
- **Required practice:** Prefer legal-entity, facility, instrument, vessel, patent and dataset identifiers; log all fuzzy-match decisions.

### Personal data
- **Meaning:** Small-area results, mobility, imagery and platform metrics can expose vulnerable people.
- **Required practice:** Minimise, aggregate, encrypt, restrict access and perform a public-interest/proportionality review.

### Automation
- **Meaning:** A public webpage is not permission for high-volume scraping.
- **Required practice:** Prefer APIs/bulk downloads; obey robots and terms where applicable; do not bypass authentication or technical controls.

### Reproducibility packet
- **Meaning:** Every publishable collision needs a frozen evidence bundle.
- **Required practice:** Save query/code, exact URL, UTC retrieval time, terms version, raw hash, transformations, join table and human-review notes.

### Statistical screening
- **Meaning:** Running many spatial joins produces apparently striking coincidences by chance.
- **Required practice:** Define hypotheses, comparison groups and thresholds before looking; correct for multiple testing and report nulls.

### Right of reply
- **Meaning:** Machine-derived leads can misidentify operators, sites or causal links.
- **Required practice:** Resolve the legal entity and evidence chain before contact; provide the material facts and time to answer.

## Glossary

### API
Application programming interface: a documented machine-to-machine query route.

### AIS
Automatic Identification System: vessel broadcasts of identity, position and motion; reception is incomplete and messages can be wrong or manipulated.

### ASN
Autonomous system number: identifier for a network that participates in internet routing.

### BGP
Border Gateway Protocol: the protocol autonomous systems use to announce reachable internet address prefixes.

### Cloud-optimised
A file layout that allows remote software to read only needed portions, reducing download and compute cost.

### Confidence / uncertainty
A quantitative or qualitative statement about model reliability; it must travel with the result.

### FDSN
Federation of Digital Seismograph Networks web-service standard used for earthquake and station data.

### GeoParquet
Columnar Parquet files with standard geospatial metadata; efficient for large spatial joins.

### GeoZarr
Chunked cloud-native array storage suited to large multidimensional environmental rasters.

### Grid cell / pixel
A spatial unit in a raster; its value represents an area, not necessarily a precise point.

### Ground truth
Independent observation or record used to validate a model or remote-sensing interpretation.

### Join key
A field used to connect records. Stable source identifiers are safer than names or addresses.

### Mascon
A large mass-concentration cell used in GRACE satellite gravity solutions.

### Modelled estimate
A value inferred from observations plus assumptions; it is not the same as a direct measurement.

### OData
Open Data Protocol, a standard for querying structured web data.

### Parquet
Compressed columnar data format designed for efficient analytical queries.

### RPKI
Resource Public Key Infrastructure: cryptographic statements that help validate which network may originate an internet prefix.

### SAR
Synthetic-aperture radar: active satellite imaging that works through cloud and darkness but needs specialised interpretation.

### SDMX
Statistical Data and Metadata eXchange: a standard used by international statistical organisations.

### STAC
SpatioTemporal Asset Catalog: a standard API/schema for discovering imagery and other geospatial assets by place and time.

### Vector / raster
Vector data represent discrete points, lines or polygons; raster data represent a grid of cells.

### Version pinning
Recording the exact dataset release, processing baseline, model version and retrieval date so another reporter can reproduce the result.

### CKAN
Open-source data-catalogue software used by Canada and several provinces/territories; its API usually exposes metadata and resource links, not automatically the data itself.

### ESTMA
Extractive Sector Transparency Measures Act: Canadian law requiring covered extractive entities to report specified payments to governments.

### OSFI
Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions: Canada's federal prudential regulator for banks, insurers and federally regulated pension plans.

### OSC
Ontario Securities Commission: Ontario's capital-markets regulator.

### SEDAR+
System for Electronic Document Analysis and Retrieval: the Canadian Securities Administrators' public-company and investment-fund filing system.

### SEDI
System for Electronic Disclosure by Insiders: Canada's browser-based insider-reporting system.

### NRD
National Registration Database: the Canadian securities registration system for firms and individuals.

### WFS
Web Feature Service: a standards-based route for querying vector features and attributes.

### WMS
Web Map Service: a standards-based route primarily for rendered map images; use a feature service or bulk vector file when attribute analysis is required.

### ArcGIS REST
A machine interface exposed by Esri ArcGIS servers for querying layers, attributes and geometry; observe record limits and layer-specific licences.
