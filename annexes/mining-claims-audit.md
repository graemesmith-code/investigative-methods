# Rabbit mining comparisons: four survive the audit

Audit completed 12 October 2026. **Four of the ten project pairings survive for narrowly specified comparisons; six are parked. No proven cross-government contradiction was found.** All four retained specimens are consistent. A surviving comparison means that its specified claims can be compared, not that every field in the two documents is comparable or that either document proves the underlying fact independently.

This follows the [original ten-candidate research](../research/2026-10-12/mining-claims/discovery/README.md). I inspected the available original filings, company-labelled host-country declarations, workbook cells, embedded forms, reporting definitions, dates, adjustments and currency notes. Unresolved cases were parked without contacting anyone. The original API/download checks establish access; this audit tests meaning and attribution.

| Original pair | Decision | Exact outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Kinross / Paracatu, Brazil | Park | Construction-stage alignment is not established by the registry's last-raise date. |
| 2. Southern Copper / Toquepala + Cuajone, Peru | Keep, changed year | Historical **2023 molybdenum output**, using the later ministry annual workbook. Exclude preliminary 2024. |
| 3. Endeavour / Ity, Côte d’Ivoire | Keep, royalties only | Aggregate all three project companies and both royalty components for 2024. |
| 4. Endeavour / Sabodala-Massawa, Senegal | Park | The Western project label and the host company's legal identity have not been decisively bridged. |
| 5. B2Gold / Fekola, Mali | Park | Dividend gross/net and category boundaries remain unresolved; royalty-category mapping is not sufficiently explicit. |
| 6. Rio Tinto / QMM, Madagascar | Park | Currency methodology and the exact royalty basket are not sufficiently aligned. |
| 7. Barrick / Kibali, DRC | Keep, ownership only | Barrick group's **45% economic interest in 2023**, not its direct registered holding. |

| 10. Galiano / Asanko, Ghana | Park | Royalty recipients and conversion bases do not yet define the same complete basket. |

The host side of eight candidates is national EITI reporting: company declarations made through a government-linked, multistakeholder disclosure process. These are not all original tax returns filed directly with a tax authority. The retained EITI comparisons use affirmative company-attributed information, rather than treating a government's receipt figure as the company's statement.

## Retained comparisons

### 2. Peru: retain 2023 molybdenum, exclude preliminary 2024

**Approved claim:** annual fine molybdenum output of Southern Peru Copper Corporation's Peruvian branch, at 100% project scope, combining Toquepala and Cuajone.

The newly downloaded [MINEM 2024 annual annex workbook](https://cdn.www.gob.pe/uploads/document/file/8166327/6827926-am-2024-excel-de-anexos.xlsx?v=1749051484) provides the crucial qualification. In `Mo-Empresas`, K4 marks 2024 with footnote 1; A16 identifies it as preliminary. J4/J7 provide historical 2023 without that qualification. J7 is **10,055.339772 fine metric tonnes**. The workbook identifies the ministry's mining statistics office; its source explanation links Peruvian production to company ESTAMIN declarations.

The [SEC-filed 2024 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1001838/000155837025002017/scco-20241231x10k.htm), printed p. 37, reports 2023 molybdenum mine production of **13,916 + 8,252 thousand pounds** for those two mines. Mexico's operations are excluded. The company's [Spanish annual report](https://southerncoppercorp.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/m2024e.pdf), p. 12, provides a cross-check of the mine-production/concentrate terminology and metric presentation.

Calculation: `(13,916 + 8,252) × 0.45359237 = 10,055.23565816 tonnes`. Allowing a full reporting unit for each displayed mine figure, conservatively covering rounding or truncation, gives **10,054.32847342–10,056.14284290 tonnes**. The ministry's 2023 value lies inside that interval, and also inside the narrower nearest-rounding interval. **Consistent.**

The original 2024 numbers also fit their rounding interval, but the explicit preliminary qualification prevents their use as two final factual assertions. Do not use copper, sales, smelter/refinery output or production denominators from cash-cost calculations under this approval. A later corrected version supersedes the specimen for future comparisons.

### 3. Côte d’Ivoire: retain the Ity royalty basket

**Approved claim:** total 2024 government mining-royalty payments for the complete Ity complex, at full project scope.

The downloaded [Endeavour 2024 AIF](https://edv-14806-s3.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/files/5617/4558/2776/EDV_2024_AIF.pdf), p. 128, explicitly maps the three exploitation permits: PE26 to SMI, PE49 to SMD, and PE53 to SMF. This closes the project/entity gap; SMI alone is not Ity's complete payment population.

In the [host EITI workbook](https://eiti.org/document/26394), sheets `34.1`, `34.5`, `34.8` identify those companies. Company-initial column E and company-final column G agree; adjustment column F is zero for the six selected cells. Rows 27 and 42 are explicitly labelled the **85% and 15% royalty components**. Sum G27 and G42 across all three sheets: **XOF 27,368,514,903**. The [host report](https://eiti.org/document/26393), section 4.8/p. 207, defines the period as flows occurring during calendar 2024; the workbook groups the selected lines under monetary payments.

The [2024 ESTMA return](https://edv-14806-s3.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/files/4717/4886/2253/Endeavour_Mining_ESTMA_2024.pdf), project table, reports **USD 45,145,203** in royalties and gives payment-date conversion rates of **595.72–631.40 XOF/USD**. The host amount permits approximately **USD 43.346m–45.942m** under those rates, before a conservative reporting-precision allowance. The Western amount lies inside it. **Consistent.**

Retain the interval comparison, not equality after conversion at an annual average. Dividends, SODEMI distributions, rehabilitation deposits and the host report's modelled tax expectations are excluded. Future corrections, refunds, negative components or changes to the project company set require a fresh check.

### 7. DRC: retain Barrick's group interest in Kibali

**Approved claim:** Barrick group's 45% economic interest in Kibali during 2023.

The [DRC annex workbook](https://eiti.org/document/25572), `Annexe 10!A6`, identifies the declaration period as January–December 2023. `Annexe 11 !C29` affirmatively records receipt of the company's capital declaration; F29 identifies **A0702049L**. The same record lists Barrick 45%, AngloGold 45% and SOKIMO 10%. `Annexe 13 !D27` explicitly attributes the two 45% interests to the company's own declaration. The [main report](https://eiti.org/document/25573), ownership/state-participation sections, explains the company-form collection and 2023 scope.

Barrick's [SEC-filed 2023 AIF](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/756894/000119312524068583/d643214dex991.htm), pp. 30 and 92, applies a 45% interest to its 2023 Kibali operations and explains the acquisition history. **45% versus 45%: consistent.**

The crosswalk is deliberately at group/economic-interest level. It does not assert that Barrick itself is the immediate registered shareholder. The host's other shareholder percentages corroborate the project mapping, but this approval does not claim a separately audited AngloGold SEC comparison or a signed certification that was not inspected.

### 8. Burkina Faso: retain Essakane ownership by bracketing the dates

**Approved claim:** IAMGOLD group's 90% interest and the state's 10% interest during the admissible 2023/2024 period.

The [host annex workbook](https://eiti.org/document/25565), Annexe 10 row 9, links Essakane's supplied capital information to embedded `xl/embeddings/Microsoft_Excel_Worksheet3.xlsx`. Its sheet `17.Structure de Capital ` names IAMGOLD in C15, with D15 = 0.90 and state participation D13 = 0.10. The form names a finance director. I am not treating its attestation wording as proof of a verified signature.

A6 says reporting period 2024; A12 says capital at 31 December 2023. I preserved both labels. The [2023 SEC AIF](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1203464/000106299324006457/exhibit99-1.htm) and [2024 SEC MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1203464/000162828025006844/q42024managementdiscussion.htm) support 90/10. A further [SEC-filed 2025 MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1203464/000106299326001022/exhibit99-2.htm), p. 21, dates the change to 85/15 to **20 June 2025** and identifies 90% for the earlier period.

**Reasoned resolution:** the ownership result is unchanged under either date interpretation. Therefore the narrow ownership comparison survives without silently correcting the form. **Consistent.** A future alert must remain incompatible under every admissible date, or be parked. Production, direct legal shareholding chains and current 85/15 ownership are excluded from this historical comparison.

## Parked comparisons and the decisive blockers

### 1. Brazil: construction stage and source timing

The [ANM bulk CSV](https://dadosabertos.anm.gov.br/SIGBM/Barragens.csv) contains Kinross CNPJ **20346524000146** and specific dam IDs. Tank XII, ID8438, says downstream raises and a liner, matching [SEC technical report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/701818/000110465926035270/tm269365d1_ex99-1.htm) section 18.4, pp. 158–159. But the SEC effective date is 31 December 2025 and the CSV is a current 2026 retrieval.

I tested whether the CSV's last-completed-raise date could resolve that timing gap. It cannot safely do so: Eustáquio ID8431 shows **25 October 2017**, while SEC p. 160 describes Stage 15 completed in late 2025. This demonstrates a stale field or a scope/definition problem that the public extracts do not settle. It does not establish deliberate misreporting. The [ANM user manual](https://www.gov.br/anm/pt-br/assuntos/acesso-a-sistemas/sistema-integrado-de-gestao-de-barragens-de-mineracao-sigbm-versao-minerador/manual-do-usuario-externo-sigbm/@@download/file) also describes controlled changes to construction-method fields. **Parked: no validated same-stage, same-effective-time pairing.**

### 4. Senegal: project label is not a legal-entity crosswalk

The [host workbook](https://eiti.org/document/25614), sheet `11`, attributes **XOF 12,982,245,458** of 2024 royalty payments to **Sabodala Gold Operations (SGO)** at G33/I33, with no company adjustment. [Endeavour's ESTMA return](https://edv-14806-s3.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/files/4717/4886/2253/Endeavour_Mining_ESTMA_2024.pdf) uses **Sabodala Mining Company SARL** as the project name. SMC is also a distinct company in the host reporting population. The AIF proves SGO holds the merged mining licence, but does not explicitly establish that the ESTMA row includes precisely SGO's payments and no different entity/project allocation. A name similarity or plausible numeric match is insufficient.

The [host report](https://eiti.org/document/25525), p. 203, also explains the removal of **XOF 3,620,127,355** of royalties paid in 2025 from the government's initial 2024 figure. This already-resolved domestic discrepancy is not a new cross-government discovery. **Parked on project-scope attribution.** Dividends have additional classification questions.

### 5. Mali: a real numerical gap is not yet a contradiction

The [amended 2023 B2Gold ESTMA return](https://s204.q4cdn.com/324791784/files/doc_downloads/estma/2023-ESTMA-B2Gold-Report-Amended.pdf) separates Fekola from Fekola Regional and reports **USD 59.06m dividends**. The [host workbook](https://itie.ml/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Annexes-ITIE-2023-_-Version-Definitive.xlsx), `Annexe 10-(2)!I7`, attributes **XOF 32,135,608,890** to Fekola. The Western conversion range maps that to **USD 51.211m–55.071m**, which does not overlap USD59.06m even with rounding.

However, the [host report](https://eiti.org/document/23795) discusses a 10% dividend withholding tax, while [ESTMA's definition](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/E-22.7/page-1.html) excludes ordinary-shareholder dividends. Neither inspected return settles the precise gross/net and qualifying-dividend treatment of these two figures. Grossing up by 10% would admit the Western amount, but that is a sensitivity calculation, **not a verified explanation**. Combining ad-valorem tax and ISCP also lacks an explicit category bridge to this return's royalty column. **Parked.**

### 6. Madagascar: incompatible conversion assumptions remain possible

The [UK filing mirrored by the FCA](https://data.fca.org.uk/artefacts/NSM/RNS/5196185.html), also available through the [Companies House JSON API](https://extractives.companieshouse.gov.uk/api/company/00719885/year/2023/json), identifies QMM as project MDG01 and reports **USD 3,527,727 royalties** for 2023. Note B specifies an annual-average conversion for Madagascar; a transaction-date rule must not be imported from another company's return.

The [host report](https://eiti.org/document/25571), table 110/p. 264, attributes **MGA 4,747,768,340.47 redevance** plus **MGA 11,078,126,125.81 ristourne** to QMM. Its conversion methodology uses **MGA 4,429.59/USD**. The exact Rio average, underlying payment currencies, and full category/recipient crosswalk are not established by these records. Converting the host sum at its average gives about USD3.573m; that difference alone is not proof. **Parked.**

### 9. Zambia: final reconciliation is not clearly a final company assertion

The [host report](https://eiti.org/document/25291), physical p. 119/printed p. 117, shows Lumwana's company royalty of **ZMW 1,295,435,702.84**, a government figure of **1,338,626,264.60**, and a reconciled final position of **1,317,031,032.60**. Notes refer to duplicated government records and 2020 amounts appearing as paid in 2023. The table does not unambiguously identify a revised company declaration adopting that final figure.

The [Barrick ESTMA return](https://www.barrick.com/files/doc_downloads/estma/Barrick_2023_ESTMA_Report.pdf) reports **USD 66.41m royalty** on its stated net-cash basis, translated at payment-date exchange rates. I cannot justify choosing either host figure as the same final net-cash assertion. Its broader taxes category is also not automatically identical to the host income-tax line. **Parked on reconciliation and payment basis.**

### 10. Ghana: recipient and currency coverage do not close

The [Galiano ESTMA return](https://s203.q4cdn.com/835664992/files/doc_downloads/estma/galiano-gold-inc-2023-estma-report-final.pdf) reports **USD 14.802m** project royalties, split into **12.888m MIIF + 0.752m Register General Department + 1.162m GRA**. The **9.0–11.6 GHS/USD** currency note accompanies particular recipient lines; it cannot automatically be applied to the whole total.

The [Ghana report](https://eiti.org/sites/default/files/2023%20gheiti%20mining%20sector%20reconciliation%20report.pdf), Appendix 3D, gives Asanko's **GHS 142,780,548** mineral-royalty figure for GRA/MIIF. It does not establish whether that figure includes the separately named Western recipient. Its methodology/discrepancy discussion also distinguishes tax-receipt cedi values from companies' internal currency conversions. **Parked on basket completeness and conversion basis.** The already-explained domestic government adjustment is excluded from potential Rabbit findings.

## Rules ready for implementation

Start with the two ownership comparisons, followed by Peru 2023, then Ity royalties. Each has an explicit entity crosswalk and a consistent specimen that can serve as a control for extraction.

1. Store the source statement, company attribution, stable project/entity identifier, period, original language, exact page or cell, retrieval date and source hash.
2. Normalize only the approved fact. For ownership, distinguish economic group interest from direct registered shares. For Peru, sum both mines and carry the two rounding intervals. For Ity, sum the six company royalty cells and use a currency interval.
3. Emit a contradiction candidate only when the normalized claims are mutually exclusive under **all** admitted dates, units, rounding and conversion choices. Missing entries and unproved zeroes do not count as affirmative claims.
4. Reopen comparability when a filing is amended, a project changes scope, a period is marked preliminary, or a reporting definition changes. Review the original pages before asserting that the company made conflicting statements.

These checks establish inconsistency between attributed assertions, when present. They do not decide which assertion is wrong, establish intent, or transform a government compilation into an independently inspected original company return.
